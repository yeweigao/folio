# Imagery

An image earns its place as evidence, explanation, or atmosphere; its source must fit that job.

- **An image has a primary job, not a vacant slot.** Remove it, and something must be lost.

| Job | Proves or teaches | Must carry |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | A place, product, person, interface, result | Caption (who, what, where, when, source) and a traceable origin |
| Explanation | A diagram, map, pictogram, process, mechanism, or a story that is the content | One drawing style for the piece, labels as live text |
| Atmosphere | Material, light, temperament | Honesty that it is atmosphere, never proof |

- **The job picks the source, not convenience.** Choose a source that can meet the task and its evidence needs; required imagery remains a material requirement.

| Source | Serves | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| The user's own photographs and screenshots | Evidence, atmosphere | Crop and correct; never alter what it proves |
| Generated by AI | Declared atmosphere or illustration | Fictional scenes stay distinct from documentary evidence; live lettering; one prompt grammar in the theme's image register; credited as generated |
| Found: the subject's own site, museums, archives, Wikimedia, Openverse | Evidence, atmosphere | License checked and credited; search the subject's world, not "premium" |
| Drawn in code | Explanation, authored illustration | The theme's drawing grammar and color roles; labels stay live text |
| Type and color alone | When imagery is optional | Better than an image doing the wrong job |

- **One strong image, not four weak ones.** Missing required imagery gets a labeled placeholder and is reported if unresolved at delivery.
- **The crop serves the subject, not the rectangle.**

| Relationship | For | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Contained | An object or document seen whole | Nothing lost to the frame |
| Edge to edge | A gesture, place, or material that composes the page | The crop strengthens the subject |
| Beside reading | Explanation with a visual counterpart | Text keeps its measure |
| Across a spread | A scene whose breadth matters | One coordinate system; subject clear of the seam |
| Type across image | A short statement whose form is the idea | One legible gesture |

- **Images share a visual grammar, not a filter.** The theme sets media, crop families, edges, and finish; photographs and diagrams may serve different jobs. Faces, hands, and marks are never cut by accident.
- **Separate the foreground, preserve the image.** Use placement, text tone, or local backing, scrim, or halo as needed; alter only the area reading requires. Check every text region on the final composite against the [production floors](../production.md), across relevant crops and states.
- **Captions carry facts, not mood.** Source on its own line; unknown says unknown; readable text is never baked into the image.
- **Resolution is computed, not guessed.** Pixels = mm ÷ 25.4 × dpi: print 300 dpi at trim, large formats ≥ 150, web at 2×.
- **People never become fabricated evidence.** Fictional figures are declared as such; real customers and documentary subjects are traceable. Consent from vulnerable subjects; hardship is never mood; no identifiable children without authorization.
