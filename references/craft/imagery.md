# Imagery

An image is evidence, explanation, or atmosphere, never wallpaper. Nothing marks a piece as AI-made faster than generated people with perfect skin, stock teams around a laptop, or a glossy render standing in for a real product.

- **An image has one job, not several.** Remove it, and something must be lost.

| Job | Proves or teaches | Must carry |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | A place, product, person, interface, result | Caption (who, what, where, when, source) and a traceable origin |
| Explanation | A diagram, map, pictogram, process, mechanism, or a story that is the content | One drawing style for the piece, labels as live text |
| Atmosphere | Material, light, temperament | Honesty that it is atmosphere, never proof |

- **The job picks the source, not convenience.** Take the first row the job allows; if none fits, use type and color.

| Source | Serves | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| The user's own photographs and screenshots | Evidence | Crop and correct; never alter what it proves |
| Generated by AI | Atmosphere; photographic or painterly imagery code cannot make | Never a real person, product, place, or event; no lettering; one prompt grammar across the piece, in the theme's image register; credited as generated |
| Found: the subject's own site, museums, archives, Wikimedia, Openverse | Evidence, atmosphere | License checked and credited; search the subject's world, not "premium" |
| Drawn in code | Explanation | The deliverable's grid, ink, and accent; one drawing style for the piece |
| Type and color alone | Any unit | Better than an image doing the wrong job |

- **One strong image, not four weak ones.** A placeholder is labeled and replaced before delivery.
- **The crop serves the subject, not the rectangle.**

| Relationship | For | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Contained | An object or document seen whole | Nothing lost to the frame |
| Edge to edge | A gesture, place, or material that composes the page | The crop strengthens the subject |
| Beside reading | Explanation with a visual counterpart | Text keeps its measure |
| Across a spread | A scene whose breadth matters | One coordinate system; subject clear of the seam |
| Type across image | A short statement whose form is the idea | One legible gesture |

- **One medium per piece, not a filter per image.** Documentary color, black and white, duotone, or halftone; two or three aspect ratios; edges on column lines; faces, hands, and marks never cut by accident. No vintage filters, fake grain, rounded corners, shadows, or tilt.
- **Type on an image is measured, not trusted.** Place it over a quiet area and adjust a neutral scrim until every text region holds 4.5:1; otherwise move the text off.
- **Captions carry facts, not mood.** Source on its own line; unknown says unknown; readable text is never baked into the image.
- **Resolution is computed, not guessed.** Pixels = mm ÷ 25.4 × dpi: print 300 dpi at trim, large formats ≥ 150, web at 2×.
- **People are real or absent, not staged.** No fabricated customers or documentary; consent from vulnerable subjects; hardship is never mood; no identifiable children without authorization.
