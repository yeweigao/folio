# Production charter

Floors for making and delivering work, not design philosophy. Read this and the surface before production; critique reports findings on supplied work and makes nothing new.

## Scope

- **The brief binds, not the habit.** Supplied facts, fixed wording, format, and brand come first; refinement keeps the existing system, redesign may replace it within scope.
- **Facts are exact; wording is a disclosed service.** Names, dates, numbers, places, quotations, and required text never change; other edits stay within scope and are reported. Fit frozen content by composition; if fixed facts conflict with a fixed text limit, ask which can change.
- **Nothing invented poses as real.** Label demo values as illustrative and calculated values with their basis; where requested real-world names, locations, hours, lineups, or product options are missing, mark them unprovided in the deliverable rather than fabricating them.
- **Truth and legibility are floors; style is chosen.** The brief fixes scope; the surface fixes viewing and export. Theme and dialect set style; craft and surface starting values may change, explicit floors do not.

## Viewing contract

Establish format, physical or viewport size, viewing distance or zoom, reading task, and languages. Infer routine choices and state material assumptions; ask only when the answer changes the work.

| Surface | Governs |
|---|---|
| [Slides](surfaces/slides.md) | Desk or projection, aspect, type floors, live text |
| [Poster](surfaces/poster.md) | Distance, cap height, crop, print preparation |
| [Handbook](surfaces/handbook.md) | Trim, binding, facing pages, density, print preparation |
| [Web](surfaces/web.md) | Reflow, keyboard, states, browsers |
| [Canvas](surfaces/canvas.md) | Overview and reading zoom, grouping, navigation |

- **Text separates from its actual ground.** Normal text ≥ 4.5:1; verified large text ≥ 3:1, otherwise use 4.5:1. Judge size after scaling and at the intended viewing distance; keep the surface's size floors. Essential non-text cues hold 3:1 against adjacent colors. These thresholds alone do not establish accessibility conformance; [color](craft/color.md) defines the screen-size test.
- **Reading text is live, not baked.** An image-filled or cropped title keeps a complete live-text equivalent.
- **Static work stays static.** Motion belongs to interactive work only.

## Frame and seams

- **Trim, bleed, safe area, and binding are separate, not one margin.** A new format is recomposed, not scaled.
- **A spread is two real facing pages, not a label.** Verify order and sides in the assembled file; a crossing image keeps one source, scale, and crop coordinate; faces and labels stay clear of the gutter.
- **Units are measured, not guessed.** In CSS, 1 in = 96 CSS px = 72 pt = 25.4 mm; scale the whole print layout together for an HTML preview. Screen CSS px need not be physical pixels; confirm trim and type sizes in the export.

## Inspection

- **Prove the system on its hardest unit first, not its easiest.** Render one with real content and fix its largest problem before extending.
- **Inspect the final export, not the draft.** Every unit at reading size, plus sequence, spreads, or viewports; contrast after overlays; content, fonts, crops, overflow, navigation. A fix earns a recheck.
- **Unverified is reported, not passed.** Delivery names the chosen system, substantive copy edits, and what remains unchecked; critique separates observed defects from proposed directions.

## Reuse

- **One deliverable does not change a shared system.** Record the template version and role mappings; keep adjustments local.
- **A family upgrade is versioned, not silent.** New version, change note, representative outputs rechecked, previous baseline recoverable; edit sources and rebuild, checking a dense and a sparse use.
- **Licenses travel with assets.** This skill's MIT license covers neither fonts nor photographs; see [type](craft/type.md).
