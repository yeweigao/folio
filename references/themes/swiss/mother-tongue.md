# Swiss — the mother tongue

The International Typographic Style: Müller-Brockmann's *Grid Systems*, Ruder's *Typographie*, Hofmann's Basel, *Neue Grafik*; grid, asymmetry, and objectivity, set for Chinese and Latin. It governs relationships, so it hosts other movements without becoming costume. Every dialect inherits this file and replaces only what it names, across the whole piece. Delivery follows the [production charter](../../production.md); numbers live in the craft files; the masters' moves live in [methods](methods.md).

## Lineage

- **Before it, 1917–1930s:** De Stijl (de-stijl), the Bauhaus (bauhaus), Russian Constructivism, Tschichold's *Die neue Typographie*, Vienna's Isotype.
- **Zurich and Basel, 1950s–60s:** Müller-Brockmann, Lohse, Vivarelli, Neuburg; Ruder and Hofmann; Gerstner's *Designing Programmes*; Akzidenz, Univers, Helvetica. The mother tongue.
- **Beside it:** Brodovitch's *Harper's Bazaar* (brodovitch), the Polish Poster School (polish-poster), the HfG Ulm (ulm), Crouwel's Total Design, Vignelli and Unimark.
- **Against it:** Weingart's Swiss Punk (swiss-punk), Memphis in Milan (memphis).
- **After it:** Kenya Hara's emptiness (ma 間), Swiss Brutal (brutalist).

## Grammar

- **The grid comes before the content, not after.** Six shared tracks, twelve to subdivide; margins, gutters, module, and leading set before anything is placed.
- **Asymmetry is the default, not a gesture.** Flush left, ragged right, unequal spans; centering needs a reason.
- **Rules, not boxes.** An opening rule establishes a table, index, or figure; hairlines separate; a closing rule completes. No shadows, no floating plates.
- **A field resets every foreground, not only the ground.** Paper, soft, dark, and accent each carry a complete palette, so any field nests in any other.
- **Text never shrinks to fit.** Recompose first; edit or paginate within scope.
- **The subject supplies the move, not the style.** Speaking units carry one; reference units repeat a dependable structure; a [variation](variations.md) marks a turn.

## Type

- **Roles before faces.** Reading, display, apparatus; one family often serves all three; a CJK/Latin pair is one voice.
- **Display has three lineages, none more refined.** A heavy grotesque for mass, a large light sans for scale without a black block, a high-contrast serif or Song for editorial and cultural weight. The subject and the viewing distance choose.
- **One hierarchy, held.** Weight-led, scale-led, or single-weight; about three levels per unit; a change of register is deliberate.
- **Titles inherit the reading family, unless another voice earns its role.** Sustained reading may take a text serif; apparatus a sans. See [fonts](../../craft/fonts.md).

## Color

| Role | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Paper · soft | The reading ground, white on screen and the stock in print; a quieter second field only when useful |
| Ink · muted | Main and secondary reading, contrasted on every field they touch |
| Line | Separation of groups already legible; never text |
| Dark | A change of register with its own complete foreground |
| Accent | The exception; an accent field is a deliberate turn |
| Signal | An optional second ink with a different job and a non-color cue |

- **The accent comes from the brand or the subject, and returns rarely enough to matter.** Values live in [color](../../craft/color.md).

## Images

- **A photograph is a fact, not a mood.** *Sachfotografie*, edge to edge or cut by a hard horizontal; no plate, shadow, radius, or scrim beyond legibility.
- **Drawing is native when it explains, not when it decorates.** Geometry, diagrams, maps, pictograms; illustration flat, one line weight, one style, on the grid, colored from the roles. Clip art, mascots, blob people, emoji, and echoing icon sets stay out.

## Space and furniture

- **One module, few axes.** Distances group, separate, or focus; leading keeps its own rhythm.
- **Furniture earns its place by function, not by look.** *Kopfzeile* and *Fusszeile* locate, *Verzeichnis* serves lookup, *Bildlegende* ties evidence to source, *Kennzahl* makes a consequential quantity visible, *Zeitachse* needs a real sequence, *Bemassung* a real measure.
- **A reading relationship comes before a layout.** Comparison, argument with aside, paired annotation, collection, continuous scene; see [grid](../../craft/grid.md).

## Caricature and habit

- **Swiss is a system, not grey minimal skin.** Helvetica, white, one volume, no scale extreme, a red square as ornament: the caricature.
- **White, black, one red, and hairlines is a habit, not a decision.** Use it when the brief chose it; otherwise let the content choose the furniture, type mode, and move.
