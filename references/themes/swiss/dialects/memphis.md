# Memphis — the party on the grid

**Invocation:** 孟菲斯 Memphis Group, Ettore Sottsass — Make work as Sottsass and the group made the 1981 collection: pattern as clothing, primitives colliding at jaunty angles, hierarchy by exuberance; never good taste, never a pattern that carries information.

Milan, 1981–88: Sottsass, Du Pasquier, De Lucchi, Shire; the Carlton bookcase, Sottsass's Bacterio laminate, Du Pasquier's patterns. Spoken in Swiss, Memphis keeps good form's grid and refuses its good taste.

- **Color clashes on purpose, not one accent.** Several saturated hues with black and white, chosen as relations.
- **Pattern is material, not ornament.** A laminate-like pattern clads a surface; never behind reading text, never posing as data.
- **Form plays, not only serves.** Primitives at odd proportions and off axis; delight is part of the function.
- **Contrary voices coexist, not resolve.** Type joins the clash in color, weight, and register.

**Hands in this school** — the DESIGN chooses one to govern; the others guest as moves, never as co-hands:
- **Ettore Sottsass — Bacterio (1978), the Carlton bookcase (1981).** Pattern as clothing, form as symbol. Suits subjects that refuse good taste on purpose. Never: pattern carrying information.
- **Alessandro Mendini — the Proust armchair (1978), the redesigns.** Take a classic and re-skin it: decoration as criticism of the canon. Suits homages and anniversaries. Never: a classic quoted unchanged.
- **Nathalie Du Pasquier — the Memphis textiles (1981–87).** Bold geometric pattern fields, drawn like architecture. Suits bands and grounds that must sing without depicting. Never: pattern at timid scale.
- **Andrea Branzi — Domestic Animals (1985).** Raw nature married to neon and laminate. Suits nature/technology collisions. Never: rustic styling.

**Swiss keeps:** the grid under reading text, the facts flush left, and every contrast floor.

**Test:** The clash changes how the subject is felt; remove it and the character goes.

**Caricature:** Squiggle confetti on everything, black outlines everywhere, an 80s pastel palette, Memphis as wallpaper behind a Swiss page.
