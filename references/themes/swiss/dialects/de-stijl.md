# de-stijl — equilibrium of planes

De Stijl and Neo-Plasticism, the Netherlands, 1917–31: Piet Mondrian, Theo van Doesburg, Gerrit Rietveld, Bart van der Leck, Vilmos Huszár; Mondrian's compositions with red, yellow, and blue, the Rietveld Schröder House, the journal *De Stijl*. It inherits the [mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and replaces only these rules, across the whole piece:

- **The lead is a balance, not an object.** The largest plane leads against unequal counterweights; never symmetry, never equal cells.
- **The grid is the picture, not the scaffold.** Black bars divide the field; text and images live inside its planes.
- **Primaries are planes, not an accent.** Red, yellow, and blue build the structure beside white, grey, and black.
- **Right angles, not diagonals,** unless the brief names van Doesburg's Elementarism, which turns the whole field 45°.

**Test:** Resize or recolor one plane, and the balance of the whole shifts.

**Caricature:** A card grid filled red, yellow, and blue; a black lattice laid over an ordinary page; Mondrian as a border.
