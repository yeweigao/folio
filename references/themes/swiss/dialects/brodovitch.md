# brodovitch — type against image

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Brodovitch's *Harper's Bazaar*, the Didot masthead, Fabien Baron's *Vogue Italia*: Bodoni and Didot colliding with photography, daring crops, black and white, condensed labels for place and date. For fashion, beauty, exhibitions, brand image, at 0.5–3 m; not for data argument, teaching, long documentary text, or wayfinding.

## What makes it brodovitch

- **Type and image touch, not sit side by side.** One display word covers, cuts, pierces, or yields to the picture; one focal point.
- **The crop dares, the gesture reads.** Extreme crops keep one readable gesture.
- **Owner's test:** on image-led units, type and picture are one composition.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | White; a soft stock in print | ≥ 45% |
| Ink | The deepest near-black | structure |
| Accent | One saturated cool hue, canonically cobalt, tinted only by opacity | ≤ 15% |

- Display Bodoni Moda or Didot (Noto Serif SC Black or a declared Song), one phrase per unit, starting on a column line; grotesque text; condensed uppercase labels only for place, date, author, number.
- Black and white or one duotone; credits name model, garment, photographer, source.
- Voice: few words, exact.

## Caricature

Fake luxury: gold gradients, glass, a centered wide-tracked serif logo, rose gold, color cut-outs on beige; Bodoni as body text; the accent flooding the page. Refuse it.

## Latitude

Free: display size, crop, accent fields, label tracking, paper temperature. Say so for color photography, competing display phrases, a second serif, or a second accent.
