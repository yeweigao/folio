# Brodovitch — the house editorial

**Invocation:** Brodovitch and the editorial houses after him — Compose the edited photograph, the sculptural serif and the paper or colour field as one relationship; let the reader's task choose its scale and tension. Never borrow another house's identity or substitute luxury styling for editing.

The lineage runs from Brodovitch's *Harper's Bazaar* (1934–58) and *Ballet* (1945) through Baron's *Bazaar* redesign (1992) to contemporary campaign books. The contemporary register is a synthesis, not an attribution of supplied work to those designers. A selected reference establishes its own construction, type and colour roles; the historical name does not erase them.

- **Photographs are edited material, not a compulsory lead.** Use monochrome people, objects or places; select a gesture and crop by role, from setting to strip to detail. A typographic or reading page may lead without a photograph. Do not force one crop scale across a series.
- **One house colour is a role, not a permanent hue.** Let it occupy a field, type plane or photographic tint beside ink, paper and grey. Choose it for the world or edition; a light or dark replacement changes the dependent foregrounds together. A reference colour is not another subject's identity.
- **The serif is an instrument, not a fashion label.** Compare actual words, sentences and numerals at matched visible height: terminals, counters, contrast and width must carry both reading and architectural scale. Select the real face, optical size and weights, then assign roles against a quiet grotesque. A Didone suits one register, not every house; never stretch a serif to fill a borrowed word's span. A condensed graphic word wall needs its own role in the reference-derived world.
- **Construct the relation from the material.** Start with a particular gesture, gaze, weight or interval in the image; in a type-led piece, start with its words and reading tempo. Compare different word/image/empty-field masses at thumbnail size before resolving the reading grid. Retain a relationship whose edge, axis, scale or interval depends on that feature and whose title and reading order remain clear. Extension, interruption, counterweight and held space are possibilities, never quotas. Quiet columns, framed plates or reference-derived enclosures belong when they organize this material; define their construction in the world's file.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **The contemporary house campaign book.** A synthesized register. Begin with the image series and the actual identity word; prove the serif, then compare the edited word/image/field relationships for each reading job. Inspect their continuity and changes as a sequence. Suits brand books, decks, launches and lookbooks. Never: a collision quota or content shrunk into a campaign template.
- **Alexey Brodovitch — *Harper's Bazaar* (1934–58).** Begin with the contact sheet; isolate one gesture, then sketch the word and image masses together across a spread. Let crop, scale and the gutter decide the tension. Suits image-led editorial and openers. Never: a stock figure beneath an unrelated centred serif.
- **Alexey Brodovitch — *Ballet* (1945).** Lay the photographs out as a sequence; edit blur, crop and density for movement, then use the page turn as a cut. Suits photographic narratives. Never: an automatic alternation of loud and quiet that ignores the material.
- **Fabien Baron — the *Bazaar* redesign (1992).** Begin with the letterform at publication scale and edit the photograph against its silhouette; use a restrained set of type and empty fields to make their relation decisive. Suits covers, identity exhibits and campaign openers. Never: a Didone word enlarged without an edited image relationship.

**Swiss keeps:** the shared grid and reading axes, white as structure, aligned facts and a quiet reading voice. The display serif may speak in sentences and figures; long reading and lookup remain legible editorial constructions.

**Test:** Remove the colour: type, crop logic and field relationships still identify the house. In an image-led speaking unit, change its defining gesture and name the composition decision that must change with it; swapping a file in an unchanged frame is insufficient. Quiet lookup and genuine peers retain regular measures. Recompose new content from the skeleton alone; test shared identity separately from reading and craft.

**Caricature:** a generic serif called fashion; tiny grey prose as texture; borrowed logos or crests; gold, glass or script as luxury; giant numerals, cutouts, cards or arrows repeated without a reading job. The frame's shape alone neither establishes nor disqualifies the hand.
