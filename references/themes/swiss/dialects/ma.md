# ma 間 — the charged interval

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Japanese modernism from Hiromu Hara's *die neue typografie* (1931) through Kamekura and Ikko Tanaka to Kenya Hara: MUJI, *Designing Design*, *White*, the Horizon campaign (2003), HAPTIC (2004). Emptiness, not simplicity: simplicity removes to clarify, emptiness removes to invite, and the viewer brings the meaning. For culture, craft and lifestyle brands, exhibitions, ceremonies, East Asian identity, and CJK work that deserves its own tradition, at 0.5–10 m; not for dense data, technical documentation, or anything that must sell loudly.

## What makes it 間

- **Emptiness is Hara's empty vessel, not scattered gaps.** Content gathers so the vessel stays whole.
- **Emptiness frames the content, not replaces it.**
- **One thing leads, isolated by space, not a composition.** A horizon, an object on white, a single mark, or one short line.
- **Small type in a large field, not large type in a small one.** Isolation and position carry the hierarchy.
- **Material is the texture, not a picture of it.** In print, the stock, uncoated surfaces, blind embossing; on screen, plain white.
- **Owner's test:** remove the emptiness and the piece says less; add a second focal thing and it says nothing.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | White, or an unbleached stock | ≥ 60% |
| Ink | A soft near-black; muted for apparatus | structure |
| Accent | Rare and small: one red mark, or the material's own color | ≤ 3% |

- Mincho or Song (Source Han Serif, Noto Serif JP or SC) for titles and text, a quiet sans for apparatus; vertical setting for short lines only.
- Centering is allowed for a single horizon or object; everything else stays on the grid.
- Photographs in natural light: a horizon, an object on white, a person small in a vast field; macro only of material.
- When a mark is needed, it is a circle or a square.
- Voice: few words, plain and specific; *kore de ii*, "this will do", not "this is the best".

## Caricature

Zen pastiche: beige everything, kraft-paper texture, ensō brush circles, stones, bamboo, washi overlays, cherry blossoms, Hokusai waves, rising-sun rays; emptiness with nothing to frame. Refuse it.

## Latitude

Free: proportion of emptiness, the one thing and its position, title direction, paper. Say so for a second focal thing, a second accent, illustration, large display type, or vertical body text.
