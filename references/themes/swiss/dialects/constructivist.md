# constructivist — the diagonal

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Rodchenko, Lissitzky, and the *Kino-Fot* covers: one diagonal, giant condensed type, red against black, typography as a call to act.

Reach for it for manifestos, mobilizing posters, launches, and cultural events seen at 3–10 m, where one claim and one fact must land. Not for dense argument, data tables, long text, or anything meant to feel gentle.

## Picture it

*Illustration, not a template.* A red band cuts from lower left to upper right at 45°, edge to edge. "READ MORE BOOKS" in condensed black capitals rides the band's upper edge. In the quiet lower right, on the grid: "Library open late, every Friday in November. Main hall." Nothing else, no stars, no figures.

## What makes it constructivist

- **Signature move**: one diagonal crosses the format and carries the eye toward the action or the date; condensed giant type runs along it or collides with it.
- **Motif**: that diagonal, at one angle for the whole piece (18° or 45°), starting from a grid intersection. It may be a solid band, a 3 px rule, or a field cut on the bias.
- **Rhythm**: red for a manifesto, paper for evidence, black for a turn; change fields when the content changes register.
- **Owner's test**: in greyscale, the diagonal and the scale of the type still shout.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | A warm stone stock or white | 40–60% |
| Ink | As the mother tongue | structure |
| Accent | A strong warm hue, classically red, the movement's color: the diagonal, one giant word, one key fact | 10–25% |

- Display: condensed grotesque in capitals (Druk Condensed, Oswald, Archivo Narrow; CJK Noto Sans SC Bold with tight tracking) at 96–200. Text: grotesque 16–24, never tilted, never capitals.
- Display may bleed but keeps one whole readable word; type on the diagonal tilts no more than 18°.
- Images: high-contrast black and white, halftone, or pure geometry; one per unit at most, never heroic low-angle figures.
- One number may lead in the display voice, with unit and source; supporting dates, prices, and quantities stay readable. Numbering 01 / 02 / 03 only where it is the reading order, aligned with the diagonal.
- Voice in copy: verb-first lines you could shout.

## The caricature

Asked for "constructivist", models produce Soviet kitsch: hammer and sickle, red stars, fake Cyrillic letters, propaganda heroes, aged paper. Refuse it, along with a second diagonal, red small text, tilted body copy, and giant type that covers the date.

## Latitude

Free: diagonal angle (one per piece), red field span, display size, paper temperature. Say so when adding a second angle, a second image, color photography, or a curve instead of the line.
