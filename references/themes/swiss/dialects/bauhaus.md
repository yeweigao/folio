# bauhaus — one shape, one primary

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Bayer's universal type, the Dessau workshop prints, and De Stijl's planes: geometry that explains structure, a single primitive shape, a single primary color, proportion as content.

Reach for it for education, product structure, system explainers, geometry and architecture, read at 0.5–3 m. Not for documentary narrative, fashion, emotional manifestos, or data-heavy argument.

## Picture it

*Illustration, not a template.* A tri-fold explaining how a school's three divisions connect: three circles at 48, 96, and 192 px share a baseline; the largest, in blue, contains the word "Upper School"; the others sit in ink outline. A 2 px line joins them. Futura carries everything. There is no second color.

## What makes it bauhaus

- **Signature move**: one primitive (circle, square, or triangle) repeats through the piece at changing scale and crop, and it means something: circle for cycle, square for module, triangle for direction or rank.
- **One primary**: red, blue, or yellow; never two in the same piece. A third level comes from line weight or a primary field, not another hue.
- **Owner's test**: remove the color and the shape system still explains the content.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper · ink | As the mother tongue; plain white paper | ≥ 50% |
| Primary | One fully saturated primary (a red, a blue, or a yellow), never two in one piece; the brand's own primary when it has one | ≤ 20% |

- Geometric sans throughout (Futura, Jost, Avenir; CJK Noto Sans SC). Scale 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 72 / 120; titles 32–48, display 72–120, text 16–20.
- Shapes sit on the 8 px module at 24 / 48 / 96 / 192 and act as container, measure, or direction for text, never as a corner ornament or a bullet.
- Prefer diagrams, sections, and proportion drawings to photographs; any image crop follows the chosen primitive.
- A light primary such as yellow takes ink at 7:1 and never white; a dark primary takes white at 4.5:1.
- Voice in copy: plain and explanatory, like a good diagram label.

## The caricature

Asked for "Bauhaus", models produce primary-color confetti: red circle, yellow triangle, blue square scattered as decoration, a retro poster font, a beige background. Refuse it, along with shapes used as icons or bullets and red-yellow-blue meant to look "childlike".

## Latitude

Free: which primary, which primitive, shape sizes, spans, field inversions. Say so when using two primaries together, a second primitive, photography, or shapes as decoration.
