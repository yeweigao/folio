# bauhaus — one shape, one primary

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Bayer's universal type, the Dessau workshop prints, De Stijl's planes: geometry that explains structure, one primitive, one primary, proportion as content. For education, product structure, system explainers, architecture, at 0.5–3 m; not for documentary, fashion, manifestos, or dense data.

## What makes it bauhaus

- **One primitive carries meaning, not ornament.** Circle, square, or triangle repeats at changing scale and crop: cycle, module, direction. It contains, measures, or points; never a bullet or a corner decoration.
- **One primary, not three.** Red, blue, or yellow, never two in a piece; a further level comes from line weight or a field.
- **Owner's test:** remove the color, and the shape system still explains the content.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper · ink | As the mother tongue, plain white | ≥ 50% |
| Primary | One saturated primary, the brand's own if it has one; light primaries take ink, dark ones white | ≤ 20% |

- Geometric sans throughout (Futura, Jost, Avenir; Noto Sans SC). Shapes on the module at doubling sizes.
- Diagrams, sections, and proportion drawings before photographs; any crop follows the primitive.
- Voice: plain and explanatory, like a good diagram label.

## Caricature

Primary-color confetti: scattered red circle, yellow triangle, blue square, a retro poster font, beige ground, "childlike" primaries. Refuse it.

## Latitude

Free: which primary, which primitive, sizes, spans, inversions. Say so for two primaries, a second primitive, photography, or shapes as decoration.
