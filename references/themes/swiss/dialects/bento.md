# bento — unequal modules

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

A product or system overview built from modules of different sizes on the Swiss grid, where one module clearly leads. The bento idea without its rounded-card cliché.

Reach for it for product overviews, design-system boards, canvases, feature maps, and dashboard narratives, at 0.5–1.5 m. Not for long reading, argument, or documentary.

## Picture it

*Illustration, not a template.* A 1440 × 900 board for a design system: the color tokens module spans six columns and two rows and is the first thing seen; a real component screenshot takes four columns; principles run as plain text in three; the version number "v4.2" sits at 48 px in a narrow module. Modules are separated by 1 px lines only.

## What makes it bento

- **Signature move**: four to seven modules of unequal size; one spans 6 or 12 columns and is the first read; the rest follow importance.
- **Separation**: white space, 1 px lines, or solid fields; one strength per screen. Modules are fields, not floating cards.
- **Owner's test**: squint, and the dominant module still jumps out before anything else.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper · ink | As the mother tongue | ≥ 55% |
| Accent | The product's or project's color: the dominant module, the current item, one number | ≤ 15% |

- Module backgrounds are paper, ink, or accent only; a "light" module is paper with a 1 px line, never grey.
- One grotesque. Scale 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48: module titles 20–24, text 14–16, labels 12, the lead number 48.
- On 1280: 50 margins, 12 × 80 columns, 20 gutters, row unit 160. Spans 3 / 4 / 6 / 12. More than seven modules means a second screen, not smaller modules.
- One number or one chart per module, with unit and source. Charts are bars, lines, or dots on a shared baseline, labeled directly.
- Key numbers that a dashboard must show sit in a typographic ledger (value, baseline, period, change) rather than in tiles.
- Voice in copy: one short line per module.

## The caricature

Asked for "bento", models produce the Apple keynote grid: equal rounded cards with soft shadows, an icon in every tile, gradients for depth, KPI tiles with donut gauges and colored status dots. Refuse it, along with cards inside cards.

## Latitude

Free: module count (4–7), dominant span, row unit, separation strength. Say so when adding an eighth module, rounded corners, more than two images, or card treatment.
