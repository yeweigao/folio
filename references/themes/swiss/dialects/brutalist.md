# brutalist — structure as content

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Contemporary exhibition graphics and engineering documentation at their most honest: the grid drawn in plain sight, system type, 1 px lines, raw material shown as it is. Restraint, not roughness.

Reach for it for engineering, infrastructure, transparency reports, specifications, and cultural projects for expert readers, at 0.5–3 m. Not for board storytelling, fashion, children, or anything that should feel soft.

## Picture it

*Illustration, not a template.* A spec page: the twelve column lines drawn at 24% ink; an index number "04" at 112 px in the first two columns; a changelog table with 1 px horizontal rules and right-aligned version numbers in IBM Plex Mono; one row flagged in the accent. No boxes, no shadows.

## What makes it brutalist

- **Signature move**: the grid, column lines, and container edges are drawn; the material (code, specs, data, screenshots) is presented directly. Structure is the content.
- **Motif**: the exposed grid plus oversized index numbers, one numbering system throughout.
- **Owner's test**: every visible line corresponds to a real structural boundary.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | White or a raw, unbleached stock | ≥ 50% |
| Ink | As the mother tongue; structural rules at full ink and at a quarter | structure |
| Accent | One hot or electric hue: current item, risk, one key number | ≤ 10% |

- Grotesque plus mono (Archivo or Helvetica Now with IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono; CJK Noto Sans SC). Mono only for code, paths, versions, coordinates, exact values.
- Scale 12 / 13 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 72 / 112: text 16–20, specs 13–14, index numbers 48–112.
- Tables first: 1 px horizontal rules, no verticals, no zebra stripes, numbers right-aligned. Code blocks 12–14 with line numbers.
- Screenshots cropped to the part that matters and readable, aligned to columns, no frames or shadows.
- Voice in copy: literal: specifications, versions, exact values.

## The caricature

Asked for "brutalist", models produce glitch art and terminal cosplay: green-on-black text, fake scanlines, noise textures, everything in monospace, deliberately broken layouts called "raw". Refuse it, along with grid lines used as decorative texture and "brutal" as an excuse not to typeset.

## Latitude

Free: index size, how much grid shows, table density, paper temperature. Say so when adding a second mono, gridlines as texture, a full-bleed photograph, or a second chart type.
