# Swiss Brutal — the structure shown

**Invocation:** 粗野主义 Brutalism — Make work as a document of record: the construction shown, everything set as specification, one hazard figure at most; never polish, never decoration.

Brutalism's exposed structure, from Le Corbusier's *béton brut* and the Smithsons to the web's brutalist revival (brutalistwebsites.com, 2014; Craigslist and the Drudge Report as raw precedents). Spoken in Swiss, Brutalism strips the grid of polish and shows it: no radius, no shadow, no apology, information as the only ornament.

- **The grid is visible, not implied, and quieter than the text.** Hairlines at one weight in a mid grey, never full ink; a heavier ink rule only opens or closes a table; zero radius, no shadows, no gradients; a box only when it is the real structure.
- **Information is the ornament, not images.** Monolithic sans titles and huge numerals; ledger rows, specs, versions, fields; a title may overflow the trim when the word is the structure.
- **Dense, not airy.** A working document, not a landing page; every band carries real content; whitespace is structural.
- **Paper, ink, one hazard, not an accent palette.** Newsprint or grey-white ground, black ink, at most one hazard red on a real alert or figure.

**Hands in this school** — a synthesized school, so its roster is three registers, not three masters; the DESIGN chooses one to govern:
- **The Document hand.** Everything set as specification: spec strips, ledgers, tabular figures, labelled rows. The dialect's default. Never: a band that carries nothing.
- **The Monolith hand.** One figure at architectural scale — a time, a number, a word — alone in its band. Suits the one figure the reader must not miss. Never: two monoliths competing.
- **The Construction hand.** The grid itself drawn — column rules and master lines, the making exposed as the picture. Suits systems and work about structure. Never: rules drawn where they carry nothing.

**Swiss keeps:** twelve columns on one left axis, flush-left setting, and a tight grotesque.

**Test:** Every visible line is a real boundary and every band holds working content; squint, and the text reads before the rules.

**Caricature:** Terminal cosplay: green on black, scanlines, noise; neo-brutalist stickers with thick outlines, fluorescent blocks, and hard shadows; ugliness on purpose; broken layouts called raw.
