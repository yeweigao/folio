# Swiss Brutal — the structure shown

**Invocation:** 粗野主义 Brutalism — Make work as a document of record: real data set as specification, the construction shown, one hazard figure at most; never polish, never decoration.

Brutalism's exposed structure, from Le Corbusier's *béton brut* and the Smithsons to the web's brutalist revival, with Craigslist and the Drudge Report as raw precedents. Spoken in Swiss, Brutalism strips the grid of polish and shows it: no radius, no shadow, no apology, and information as the only ornament.

- **The grid is visible, not implied, and quieter than the text.** Hairlines at one weight in a mid grey, never full ink; a heavier ink rule only opens or closes a table; zero radius, no shadows, no gradients.
- **Information is the ornament, not images.** Monolithic sans titles and huge numerals; ledger rows, specs, versions, fields.
- **Dense, not airy.** A working document, not a landing page; every band carries real content.
- **Ground, ink, one hazard, not an accent palette.** Newsprint or grey-white ground, black ink, and at most one hazard color on a real alert.

**Hands in this school.** A synthesized school, so its roster is three registers; one governs.
- **The Document hand.** Start from the real data and its schema. Set every row as specification (label, value, unit, source) with tabular figures, and generate the tables in code. Suits reports, pricing, specs. Never: a band that carries nothing.
- **The Monolith hand.** Find the one figure the reader must not miss (a time, a number, a word). Set it at architectural scale, alone in its band, and leave everything else as document. Never: two monoliths competing.
- **The Construction hand.** Draw only the tracks and master lines the layout actually uses, as the picture. Suits systems and work about structure. Never: rules drawn where they carry nothing.

**Swiss keeps:** twelve columns on one left axis, flush-left setting, and a tight grotesque.

**Test:** Every visible line is a real boundary and every band holds working content. Squint, and the text reads before the rules.

**Caricature:** Terminal cosplay (green on black, scanlines); neo-brutalist stickers with thick outlines and hard shadows; ugliness on purpose; broken layouts called raw.
