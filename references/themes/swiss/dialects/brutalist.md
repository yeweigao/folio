# brutalist — structure as content

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

The *béton brut* honesty of New Brutalism and the structural graphics of Norm and Experimental Jetset: the grid drawn in plain sight, system type, 1 px lines, raw material shown as it is. Restraint, not roughness. For engineering, infrastructure, transparency reports, specifications, expert cultural projects, at 0.5–3 m; not for board storytelling, fashion, children, or softness.

## What makes it brutalist

- **Structure is shown, not hidden.** Grid, column lines, and container edges are drawn; code, specs, data, and screenshots appear directly.
- **One numbering system, not decoration.** Oversized index numbers on the exposed grid.
- **Owner's test:** every visible line is a real structural boundary.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | White or a raw, unbleached stock | ≥ 50% |
| Ink | Structural rules at full and quarter strength | structure |
| Accent | One hot or electric hue: current item, risk, one key figure | ≤ 10% |

- Grotesque plus mono (Archivo or Helvetica Now with IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono; Noto Sans SC); mono only for code, paths, versions, coordinates, exact values.
- Tables first: horizontal rules only, numbers right-aligned; screenshots cropped to what matters, framed by nothing.
- Voice: literal, specifications and versions.

## Caricature

Terminal cosplay: green on black, scanlines, noise, everything mono, broken layouts called raw, gridlines as texture. Refuse it.

## Latitude

Free: index size, how much grid shows, table density, paper temperature. Say so for a second mono, decorative gridlines, or a full-bleed photograph.
