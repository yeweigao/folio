# gridnik — type built from the grid

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

Wim Crouwel, "Mr. Gridnik", and Total Design in Amsterdam: the Stedelijk Museum posters, the New Alphabet (1967), letters constructed from the same grid that holds the page. Systematic, cool, and visibly engineered; the grid is not behind the design but inside every letter.

Reach for it for technology, architecture, music and exhibition posters, research institutes, and identities that should feel constructed; 0.5–10 m. Not for warm community materials, long reading, or documentary.

## Picture it

*Illustration, not a template.* A square poster with the grid printed faintly in the accent. The title "SYSTEMS" is drawn from rectangles snapping to the same cells, each letter five cells high with square corners. Dates and venue sit below in Inter, aligned to the same columns as the letter strokes. The grid, the lettering, and the text share one geometry.

## What makes it gridnik

- **Signature move**: display lettering constructed on the page grid (rectangles, quarter-rounds of one radius, consistent stroke) with text aligned to the same lines.
- **Motif**: the visible construction grid, drawn faintly on display units.
- **Owner's test**: overlay the grid and every display stroke and text edge lands on it.

## House values

- Display: constructed letters drawn as vector shapes on the grid, with the real word also present as live text; or a geometric display face with square terminals. Text in Inter or the mother tongue's sans.
- Grid: square cells for display units; stroke width equals one cell or one gutter; one corner radius or none.
- Color: ink and paper with the accent for one element, or a single strong field color with ink type. The grid shows in the accent at 8–32%.
- Images: rare; geometric or architectural photographs cropped to whole cells.
- Voice in copy: terse, technical, numeric.

## The caricature

Asked for "retro-futuristic" or "techno", models produce neon wireframes, dot-matrix fonts on black, synthwave gradients, glitch effects. Refuse them. Gridnik is printed, flat, and calm; its futurism comes from the system, not from glow.

## Latitude

Free: cell size, stroke width, which letters are constructed, field color. Say so when mixing constructed and conventional display type or adding curves of a second radius. Keep the live-text equivalent.
