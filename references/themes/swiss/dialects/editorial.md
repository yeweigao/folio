# editorial — argued pages

*A dialect of the Swiss theme: it inherits [the mother tongue](../mother-tongue.md) and changes only what it names.*

The Vignelli Canon and Unimark's corporate discipline, Tufte's evidence, Minto's pyramid and Zelazny's McKinsey exhibits: argument units state a judgment, show the evidence, say what follows; reference units make lookup easy; a serif display voice gives the argument gravity. For boards, operating reviews, review committees, researchers, long-form reporting, at 1–3 m; not for image-led branding, fashion, children, or claims that must shout.

## What makes it editorial

- **Action title, one exhibit, a decision band, not three columns of bullets.**
- **The decision band holds implications and actions in one place, not scattered.** A full-width rule or solid band, two modules tall at least, flush left.
- **Owner's test:** the argument titles read in order are the executive summary.

## House values

| Role | Relationship | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | White; a warm stock in print | ≥ 50% |
| Ink | Near-black that may lean blue | structure |
| Deep | The ink at full bleed, not a new hue | ≤ 25% |
| Accent | Reserved for judgment and action: the band, the risk, the key figure | ≤ 12% |

- A serif carries titles, openers, and sustained argument; a sans carries labels, tables, and apparatus ([fonts](../../../craft/fonts.md)).
- Text on six to eight columns; at least two columns free of text except on full-bleed evidence.
- One documentary photograph per unit at most, traceable to its source.
- Voice: judgment, evidence, conclusion.

## Caricature

The consulting template: noun-phrase titles, three equal icon columns, a rule under every title, sources shrunk to grey, teal for "insight", centered titles, one italic word in a title. Refuse it.

## Latitude

Free: title span, exhibit height, band position, density, paper temperature. Say so for another typeface, another motif, or another photographic medium.
