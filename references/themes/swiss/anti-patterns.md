# Anti-patterns

Why defaults fail. The [mother tongue](mother-tongue.md) sets the house; the [production charter](../../production.md) holds shared floors. **Evidence** is a concrete failure of legibility, navigation, honesty, or production: test the result. **House** is this theme's preference; a brief, brand, or dialect may override it. Functional backing, shadow, or halo is judged by legibility; a style choice alone is not a failed check.

## Layout

| Pattern | Why it fails | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Centered hero, three equal cards, gradient button, logo wall | The most common generated page; it says nothing about this subject | house |
| Equal boxes for unequal importance | Equal space erases real priority | house |
| Floating rounded cards, cards in cards | The box replaces the grid; corners and shadows carry no meaning | house |
| KPI tiles | Each number floats without baseline, period, or meaning | house |
| An icon per bullet; icon walls | Pictures stand in for hierarchy and add nothing | house |
| Centering by default | Symmetry is the absent decision; the reading line weakens | house |
| Snake columns for ordered content | The order breaks across the Z | evidence |
| Numbers on unordered content; watermark numerals | Implies a structure that does not exist | house |
| Accidental gaps, or filler to hide them | Leftover reads unfinished; filler reads as noise | house |
| Two whole dialects on one unit | Competing systems dissolve the shared voice | house |

## Surface and color

| Pattern | Why it fails | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Gradients, glow, glass, drop shadows, fake grain, fake 3D | A familiar effect stands in for a subject-specific decision | house |
| Greys invented per component | The page turns into boxes | house |
| Several accents, or one accent for unrelated roles | Emphasis loses its meaning | house |
| Warm paper on screen by habit | A palette convention stands in for a decision | house |
| Fake luxury: gold gradients, glowing outlines on black, spaced serif capitals | The stereotype of expensive, not its substance | house |

## Type and copy

| Pattern | Why it fails | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Default fonts, extra families, or colored text as hierarchy | Unchosen voice; hierarchy without a system | house |
| A colored eyebrow above every heading | Repeated chrome builds a competing hierarchy | house |
| Filler verbs, "not just X but Y", em-dash drama, emoji | Rhetoric replaces information and reads as generated | house |
| Decorative English on Chinese pages | It signals "design", not meaning | house |
| A giant word with no claim behind it | Remove the word and nothing is left | house |
| Fake editorial: drop caps, quote walls, "Vol. 01" | Magazine skin standing in for an argument | house |

## Data

| Pattern | Why it fails | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Pie, donut, gauge for precise comparison | Angles and arcs hide close differences | evidence |
| Dual axes implying an unsupported crossing or correlation | Independent scales manufacture the apparent relationship | evidence |
| A chart on a card with a legend and a grey plot | The box isolates it; the legend makes readers look twice | house |
| Invented or unlabeled numbers | Deception, not design | evidence |
| Reading text baked into images | It cannot be edited, searched, or read aloud | evidence |

## Images and motion

| Pattern | Why it fails | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Generated people, places, or products presented as real | Fabricated evidence | evidence |
| Parallax, fly-ins, fade-up on every block | Effects compete with content | house |
| Text below the size or contrast floor; motion without a reduced alternative | Content becomes hard or impossible to perceive | evidence |
| Rotated body text, type over busy photographs | The reader pays for the experiment; experiment in display only | evidence |

## Habit

- **Newer defaults are still defaults.** A cream ground with a high-contrast serif and terracotta; near-black with one acid accent; a broadsheet of hairlines, zero radius, and dense columns. The last two overlap the Swiss vocabulary, which is why each needs a reason.
- **A plan that never names the subject is a default, not a decision.**
