# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by and visitors at the glass and in the street, Chinese and English readers of equal status, at two to five metres. In three seconds: what (THE GRID, 格网, three talks and an exhibition), when (Saturday 14 November 2026), where (Hall 2). Next: they come on the evening, no booking.

## The idea

On this poster, *the grid* becomes *the white between the talks*: a ladder where 15 minutes is one module of height, so the 45-minute pitch is three modules of equal white and no grid line is drawn. The third talk, The Interval, is the space the reader is already looking at.

## Sequence

One unit: poster, 1000 × 1414 px. Lead `THE GRID` / `格网` by isolation, the ladder of talks second. Base register.

## Assumptions and gaps

- Print size assumed A1 (594 × 841 mm), 1 px = 0.594 mm; the file is the screen master, no bleed.
- No Chinese titles or names were given for talks and speakers; they stay in English.
- No photograph supplied: nothing drawn, no placeholder; a real photograph of the Museum is the first addition (Horizon).
- The ladder claims nothing about 19:00, 22:00 or the foyer, which sit in the colophon.
