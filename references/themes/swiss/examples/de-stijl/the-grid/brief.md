# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by and visitors, Chinese and English readers, at two to five metres in evening light. In three seconds: what (THE GRID, 格网), when (14 November, 19:30–22:00), where (Hall 2). Then they come; entry is free.

## The idea

On this poster, *the grid* becomes *the evening's clock*: black bars fall at 19:00, 19:30, 20:15, 21:00 and 22:00, and each plane is drawn to its duration at 3.1 px a minute.

- Spans of 30, 45, 45 and 60 minutes give the unequal planes; The Interval is tallest.

## Sequence

One unit. THE GRID in red leads, 格网 in blue answers, yellow time planes follow, the clock column runs down the left.

## Assumptions and gaps

- A1 (594 × 841 mm) assumed; no paper size, printer or proof given.
- 20:15 and 21:00 are starts only; each talk plane runs to the next bar, unlabelled as a duration.
- The foyer exhibition has no end time; none is printed.
- 格网 is stacked one character per line in Noto Sans SC; no Chinese face is named.
- Lower left reads as a ledger; the clock bars are fixed, so it stays.
