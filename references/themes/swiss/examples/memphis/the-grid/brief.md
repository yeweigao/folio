# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by in the city, reading in passing from two to five metres. In three seconds: THE GRID, three talks and an exhibition; Saturday 14 November 2026; Hall 2, Museum of Design. Close up: times and speakers. They turn up; entry is free, so no booking and no URL.

## The idea

On this surface, *the grid* becomes *a laminate that is cut*: the 6 × 8 module field is tiled whole by pieces, one primitive per talk in its own laminate (square in graph-paper rule, triangle in 45-degree stripes, disc with a square gap in dots). The three towers step down one module each, so the staircase is the order of the evening.

## Sequence

One unit, a Cover with the talks as a subordinate sequence: THE GRID, date block, staircase of towers.

## Assumptions and gaps

- "Nine dialects, one mother tongue" is printed as given; the nine are not listed.
- Fictional programme: `Illustrative example` is set at reading size on the bottom band.
- No speaker photographs; the names are real, the programme is not.
- Hues are screen values; no print proof or bleed.
