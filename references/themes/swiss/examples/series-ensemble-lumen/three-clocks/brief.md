## Reader and outcome

Passers-by in tram shelters and foyers, reading from three metres. In three seconds: a concert, Three Clocks, the ensemble. On a pause: date, time, place, programme, price. Outcome: they remember the evening and visit ensemble-lumen.ch.

## The idea

On this surface, *Reich's Clapping Music, the one work of the three with a computable score*, becomes *the poster's field*: twelve pulses are the tracks, twelve phases the rows, one blue clap traced from top-left to bottom-right, so time drifting out of step is a picture you can count.
Pattern x x x . x x . x . x x .; shifts 0 to 11 shown, the return to unison lies below the edge. Ligeti and Haydn are programme text only.
