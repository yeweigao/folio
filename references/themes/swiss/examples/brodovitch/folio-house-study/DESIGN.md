# folio house · Swiss, editorial campaign book

An editorial identity for a house's image studies, essays, exhibitions and collections, on pages, screens and posters. These values address independent reading; projected work requires a new distance proof and larger reading sizes before changing word count.

> Use alone. Read Overview and Images before choosing values. S is the format's short side; numeric tokens below are at S = 1000 and scale by S / 1000. A span-derived display size is computed once from the span. Each piece brings its own content and edition colour; it keeps this identity and construction grammar.

## Overview

Swiss, the contemporary editorial campaign book — Brodovitch's image editing and Baron's typographic scale: compose the edited image and the word as two architectural masses, then place the reading around their relationship; never borrow another house's marks or reduce the method to a centred serif over a photograph. This is an inferred contemporary register; no authorship of the supplied boards is established.

World: keep a distinct display serif, neutral grotesque reading, monochrome photography, a single edition colour, and a grid that joins image, type and paper fields. A new piece changes the crop, leading word, proportion of fields and frame construction according to its reader's job. The brand word is an identity asset; other content belongs in the brief.

Method: first identify what the reader must recognize, read or compare. Edit the photographic gesture and sketch the dominant type at thumbnail scale together. Choose a construction from Forms, build its shared axes, then place supporting copy in a quiet field. Compare the complete sequence before adding anything: density and field changes follow a change of subject or reader task. Regular peers may stay regular; there is no compulsory loud/quiet alternation. Do not add numbered panels, arrows or frames merely to resemble the references.

Amplitude: full architectural scale for identification and exhibits; reduced scale for essays, lookup and peer collections. A quiet page remains in this house through the serif's shape, editorial measures and shared field edges; it need not manufacture a collision.

Anti-reference: equal rounded content cards under a small centred heading, uniform photo thumbnails with interchangeable captions, or a generic presentation footer. Caricature is specified below.

## Images

Use licensed monochrome editorial photographs, retaining tonal detail, and credit the actual source. These finished examples share an explicitly generated coast, embedded in each SVG as atmosphere rather than documentary evidence. New pieces bring the licensed photographs their subjects require; multiple crops of one image never imply multiple places or observations.

A setting image meets the trim or the shared edges of adjoining fields; retain a contextual horizon or recognizable whole subject. A specimen sits in an inset frame and preserves the subject's identifying contour. A narrow section strip may cut context but retains an identifiable gesture. A detail crop may isolate texture only when the content identifies it as a detail and the piece supplies context elsewhere. Fit all frames by uniform cover scaling and focal translation; never stretch a photograph or zoom every crop to the same scale. On portrait formats, choose a new frame and crop rather than shrink a landscape page.

A silhouette is a foreground photographic layer, cut closely around the observed edge, with no sky fringe or polygonal background wedge. Preserve its source aspect ratio; reflection is allowed only as an explicitly identified graphic repeat of the same source image, never as a new observation. A foreground cutout meets a deliberate base, trim or containing shape, rather than floating on an accidental rectangular cut.

A letter window uses an outline from the selected real font; a photo shows through that shape. This aperture is an inferred extension of the reference's patterned mark and cutout depth, rather than a source-identical device. Keep the letter recognizable through a continuous stem, bowl or crossbar and at least one counter where the glyph has one. The accent may occupy another plane. An overlapping silhouette may interrupt part of the glyph, never all identifying structure. Use the same photograph for a cutout and its window only when they describe graphic depth rather than a factual scene.

## Layout

Grid system: a compound editorial grid. Set a module m = S / 8. Use twelve equal tracks across W; derive gutters as m / 6 and reading margins as m / 3. Establish a few shared vertical and horizontal field edges before positioning content. A quiet page may leave several tracks empty. Full-bleed image and colour fields may cross margins; reading text remains within them.

Landscape can place a narrative beside parallel panels, a dominant image beside a text field, or a word across a foreground. Portrait recomposes these as vertically ordered fields, retaining a dominant type/image relationship and readable measures. Do not reproduce the same page at a smaller scale. The module governs repeated frame divisions, not a mandatory decorative tile pattern.

Peer groups share their alignment, caption offset and gutters. A numbered collection reads across rows unless distinct labelled groups establish another path; a column gutter alone does not establish groups. A collage can vary image height to preserve crop identity, but neighbouring boundaries remain linked to the grid. A comparison uses equal evidence scales even if a campaign collage varies crops. Counts have explicit labels and a predictable reading order; prominence reflects the reader's question, not arbitrary staggering.

Place empty space where it separates reading from spectacle or joins a group. Between a giant word and an independent rule or caption allow visible ink clearance of at least one body line-height. Between independent reading groups allow at least one body line-height; between a heading and its own copy allow half a body line-height. Measure actual glyph ink, including italic overhangs and descenders, not text boxes. Intentional image/type overlap belongs only to the lead construction. Supporting copy, figures and labels never collide.

## Typography

Typography is the identity's principal instrument. The display selection is Source Serif 4, optical size 60, with real 400, 500, 600, 700 and 400 italic instances. Select a replacement only by comparing the actual brand word, a sentence headline and numerals at matched visible height: open counters, sculptural terminals, controlled stroke contrast and a convincing architectural silhouette must survive both sentence and giant scale. A generic body serif or a nominal font-family declaration is insufficient. The original reference face is unconfirmed.

Use Source Serif 4 400/500/600/700 and 400 italic at optical size 60 for the named serif roles. Inter 400/700 carries reading and labels; Oswald 700 is reserved for the graphic word wall. Obtain licensed font files for new work, embed used faces and verify actual glyph rendering. The finished SVGs are self-contained and carry their font-license notices. Check coverage for new copy. For CJK, select an editorial Mincho/Song display companion with comparable contrast and visible height, then recompose; this translation remains untested.

Display: use serif 400 upright for the identity word, subject word and initial; upright 400 numerals or 400 italic section numerals, consistently within one sequence. Real weight changes belong to an explicit weight specimen or a consistent emphasis role, not arbitrary letter styling. Set the brand word, subject word, initial or numeral to its allocated span, with visible ink height usually 0.22–0.50 S; a letter-depth construction may reach or exceed S. Preserve the serif's native width and natural proportions. A shorter identity word is recomposed within its field rather than stretched to imitate a longer reference name. Condensed word walls alone may use graphic compression, and must remain recognizable.

Narrative title: serif 400, 52–76 at S = 1000, at least 1.8 times body; use natural case dictated by content. Local panel heading: grotesque 700 or serif 500, 39–44, at least 1.45 times body; choose one treatment for the piece. Body: grotesque 400, 26, line-height 1.4, measure 35–65 characters in an essay; short narrow-panel copy may use 20–35 characters. Labels: grotesque 400/700, 19–22, without ornamental tracking. Metadata: 18 minimum. A numeral may instead be a span-derived serif display, with its factual label beside it. Use real weights; a weight specimen shows actual font files, never synthetic bold.

These size ratios refer to perceived visible height: inspect the render. Make a supporting paragraph recede by field, tone and a decisive heading step; never shrink it below its reading floor to mimic unreadable reference text. Reflow long headings, split a long essay into columns or continue onto another page before reducing this hierarchy. Keep paragraphs and tables live; only constructed glyph windows may use outlines. A cropped decorative word or numeral requires the necessary word/count in an intact reading label elsewhere.

## Colors

Ink and paper carry reading; grey is subordinate reading only where contrast holds. One edition colour carries house identity through a full field, a selected type plane, a photographic tint or an isolated panel. The reference blue is the reconstruction edition, not a permanent brand requirement. Choose another hue and value from the new edition's intent; use the same accent across its pieces.

Determine `onAccent` from the actual new colour. Prefer house ink when it passes 4.5:1 and has at least as much contrast as paper; otherwise choose the higher-contrast of pure black and paper. Pure black is an allowed foreground fallback for middle-value accents where house ink and paper both fail. Reset all type, labels, rules and action cues on that field together. `accentOnPaper` uses the edition colour only if its contrast passes; otherwise it uses ink. A photo tint remains monotone within the same accent. Measure text on the actual image beneath it; move reading to a quiet field or add a deliberate tonal wash where necessary. Do not assume white works over every monochrome crop. No gradient, metallic effect or shadow supplies luxury.

## Shapes

Shapes divide editorial fields. Rectangles join image and colour through common edges. A stepped field is a connected orthogonal boundary whose advances and returns follow m; avoid isolated decorative squares. Translucent accent rectangles can reveal the same underlying image at an editorial boundary, never act as text contrast protection without measurement.

Parallel column panels share height and a radius of approximately 0.20 m. An inset outline uses one continuous contour across paper and photo, with matching outer-corner radii around 0.30 m and a fine stroke of 1–3 at S = 1000. A circular statement field inscribes a true circle within its allocated square; its accompanying rectangular reading field shares its width. A ticket is a paper rectangle with one centred semicircular cutout at each of two opposing edges, each radius around 0.35 m; size the text safe area beyond the notches. Do not combine all these frame families on one page.

Depth comes from occlusion between live display type, letter windows, colour and cut-out photograph; no simulated shadows or bevels. A small directional cue is allowed for a real next action or sequence. Any capsule action is a label for an actual action, not recurring page furniture. The brand word and font-derived initial are the identity marks; do not invent a crest or copy another house's monogram.

## Forms

- Cover / opener / close: choose a single word-image relation or a spare serif statement and a narrow photographic field; keep identity and necessary action readable, ban a montage of previews.
- Statement: isolate the sentence in paper, colour or an inscribed circle; subordinate any image, ban an unrelated numeral as the lead.
- Exhibit: make one photo, cutout, letter window or typographic specimen lead; give enough uninterrupted structure to recognize it, ban equal competing spectacles.
- Argument / essay: use a serif judgment title, readable grotesque columns and evidence in an adjoining field; continue text at the same measure, ban an oversized graphic that crowds out the reasoning.
- Sequence: label the actual order, use cropped architectural numerals only where the order leads; preserve intact labels, ban arbitrary number stacks for unordered peers.
- Comparison: give comparable specimens equal scales and aligned labels; accent identifies one meaningful distinction, ban crop-driven false differences.
- Collection: align peer entries in columns or a photo wall with coherent gutters and captions; vary height only to preserve evidence or crop identity, ban invented ranking.
- Reference / data: use labelled serif figures with quiet reading or a ruled lookup table; photo and display recede, ban campaign framing masquerading as data encoding.
- Exercise: place the instruction and usable answer area in one reading field, with the image only as the task's material; ban an answer hidden in decoration.

## Variations

At a change of subject or a lead exhibit, a white edition may turn to full accent or ink; change all dependent foregrounds and retain the same faces and grid. At a quiet essay, reduce display amplitude and photographic area while keeping a clear serif lead and field relationship. A portrait field rearranges the construction by reading order and crop identity; it does not scale down the landscape layout. These are inferred extensions of the reference family and require their own transfer evidence.

## Do's and Don'ts

Render at intended size and as a sequence. A thumbnail must reveal one lead; reading size must reveal distinct title, body and label roles, comfortable actual-ink gaps, natural photo proportions and clean silhouettes. Preserve exact facts; distinguish fictional specimens from observations. All reading remains live and above its floor. Require 4.5:1 for body/labels, 3:1 for large type and essential cues. Licenses travel with assets. Verify the actual export; an SVG proof does not establish PPTX or print quality.

Test: remove the edition colour and the serif, crop logic, field edges and reading relationships still form one house. A typography-only page may keep its meaning. Change the governing type/image relationship and frame grammar and it should cease to be this identity. A successful new page demonstrates that brief and declared assets, not general reliability.

Caricature: tiny grey copy used as texture, one collision on every page, a Didone chosen solely because fashion was named, borrowed logos, gold/script/glass effects, and a gallery of arbitrary luxury-looking cards.
