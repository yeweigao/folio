# Schönbrunn editorial · Swiss / Brodovitch

## Overview

Swiss, the Brodovitch dialect — **Alexey Brodovitch, Ballet (1945)**: compose from the image as editable material; carry its movement, interruption or held interval through crop, type and page together. Never fit a photograph into a finished title-and-panel arrangement and call its font a master's method.

World: cultural posters, photographic editorial and fashion pages whose reading is composed as an edited passage. The governing hand is Brodovitch; the high-contrast serif is this world's chosen material, not a second governing hand or proof of authorship. Keep the script-specific fonts, factual reading and Schönbrunn-yellow palette declared below. A piece brings its own gesture, words and reading tempo. Quiet reference material stays quiet and orderly.

Method: begin with one specific fact of the subject, before selecting a layout. Where an image leads, inspect its actual directional gesture, weight, interval or tension and compare small studies with different word/image/empty-field relationships, using that same material. Edit its crop/scale and the title's silhouette together. Choose the relationship whose principal edge, axis, interval or scale would have to change if that specific material changed; a clean adjacency alone is not the idea. At thumbnail size, retain a recognizable title, a clear entry and a deliberate next step; a material relationship that obscures these needs revision. A held empty field may oppose the image's movement, a title may extend or interrupt it, and an edited sequence may make it continue; none is a compulsory effect. These are digital translations of a photographic editing method, not copies of the historical book's printing or layouts. Only after that relationship works at thumbnail size should the grid and supporting facts be resolved.

For image-free speaking material, the wording, duration or order of the content supplies the material to edit: compare two groupings or measures and keep the one that makes that particular reading felt. For lookup and genuine peers, preserve predictable order and regular measures; do not invent a spectacle merely to force a signature. Across units, change amplitude where the content changes, not by automatic alternation.

Amplitude: the subject decides the lead's force and the supporting field's quiet. Legibility is a floor, not the criterion for choosing the leading composition. A complete pose and an intact rectangular photograph are options, not the default aim of editing.

Anti-reference: the same large-title field, protected photo rectangle and utility strip applied to unrelated subjects; a fashionable serif and house colour with no material-dependent decision.

## Images

Use licensed monochrome photographs as setting, exhibit or atmosphere. Generated imagery is permitted as declared illustration; it never stands in for a named person, commissioned portfolio work or documentary evidence. Keep source and generation attribution with the deliverable; detailed production metadata does not occupy the reading flow.

A setting meets the trim or a shared image/colour edge and preserves the contextual horizon or scene. An exhibit uses an inset or adjoining plate whose crop retains its identifying gesture. A panoramic strip can follow a horizontal subject, but its width, interruption and relation to reading still come from the actual image. A deliberate detail may cut a face, limb or context when that cut carries the idea; distinguish this from accidental clipping. Keep enough identifying structure elsewhere for the subject to remain recognizable. Choose each crop by role and by the chosen movement/interval. Scale uniformly with cover/crop positioning; never distort the source ratio or impose one crop scale on all subjects.

A cutout follows the actual subject boundary without background fringe. Register image and silhouette when they describe the same scene. A letter window is a purposeful contemporary interpretation: retain a recognizable stem, bowl or counter. A typography-only page needs no photographic placeholder.

## Layout

S = min(W, H); tokens are at S = 1000 and scale by S / 1000, except span-derived display sizes. Web sizes retain their explicit floors.

Grid system: compound editorial columns, derived after the lead relationship is chosen. Unit u = S / 72; reading margin 4u; gutter u. Use six main tracks and twelve subdivisions on wide fields, deriving widths after subtracting margins and gutters. Field edges, text starts, captions and rules share selected track lines; construction tracks stay invisible.

A poster gives its identifying word and image one dominant relationship; facts have an independent readable axis. A spread uses the fold deliberately: a continuous image may cross it, while reading keeps clear measures on either side. A portfolio reflows by task. At wide viewports unequal reading and image columns may adjoin; when either becomes too narrow, stack them in reading order with new crops and margins of at least 24 px. Verify at 1440, 768 and 320 px; no horizontal overflow or scaled-down desktop text.

Paper, yellow or ink may be the main field. Adjoining fields have a clear common edge; their relationship is composed rather than mixed behind reading. Repeated peers share widths, caption offsets and row spacing. Numbered collections read across rows unless labelled groups establish another order. A reference table keeps its label/value and baseline alignment beside a graphic plate.

Clearance protects independent reading groups; it does not require the title, image and field to live in separate rectangles. Give an independent word and its unrelated reading group one body line-height of visible clearance. When the leading relationship crosses a crop or another plane, register that relationship precisely, retain necessary reading and keep supporting copy clear. A heading sits half a body line-height from its text; reading groups are at least one line-height apart. Measure actual ink, including serif overhangs and CJK punctuation. Space separates reading from spectacle or holds the lead in its field; do not fill it with ornaments.

## Typography

The Latin display face is **Bodoni Moda 400**: sharp stroke contrast, unbracketed serifs and open counters at publication scale. Use its actual static outline; do not claim an optical-axis setting for a file without that axis. The Chinese display companion is **Source Han Serif SC 400**, with real 500 available for a subordinate heading. Put the Latin face before the CJK fallback or explicitly mark script runs; a CJK family containing Latin must not replace the selected Latin serif.

Reading and labels use **Inter 400/500/700**, paired with **Source Han Sans SC 400/500/700** for Chinese. All use SIL OFL licenses. Embed used subsets with their copyright/license notices in the finished HTML. Use real supplied weights and disable synthetic bold and italic; an italic requires an actual corresponding face. Verify rendered Latin and Chinese glyph runs separately.

Proof the title, a sentence and figures at matched visible height before substituting a face. Display remains thin and sculptural at large scale, with hairlines sturdy enough in the actual render. Do not substitute heavy 800/900 Song or a generic body serif to fill width. Match Latin/CJK visible height and density rather than nominal size; paired CJK display generally needs 0.80–0.88 of the Latin size, adjusted by actual glyphs.

Display: upright 400 at natural width. Fit the largest size satisfying both allocated span and block-height limits; the principal display block usually occupies 0.24–0.44 S in visible height. A short word leaves deliberate unused width or takes meaningful lines. Never stretch it horizontally. Rebreak a long title before weakening the hierarchy. Registered copies for an intentional cut remain one word, with one complete accessible reading label.

The reading hierarchy has explicit jobs, not one nearly uniform text setting. At S = 1000: a principal date, decision or action uses grotesque **700 at 48**; primary reading uses **400 at 32**, while a programme topic can use serif **400 at 42–44** for its distinct visible silhouette; supporting names, doors, captions and secondary explanations use grotesque **400 at 22**. A lookup entry or short time code may use **700 at 22–36**, matched consistently across peers. The thin display serif stays 400; it does not require the supporting information to stay light. Never synthesize the 700 faces.

Where size establishes rank, adjacent tiers should differ by about 1.4–1.6 in visible height. If similar sizes have different reading jobs, distinguish them with an unmistakable real 700/400 weight or the serif/grotesque voice; a 30/29 size change or 500/400 weight change alone is insufficient. Select only the roles this piece needs, with at most four visible levels on a poster. Recompose for this hierarchy instead of making all information small, regular or bold.

Body leading is 1.4–1.55 with 40–65 Latin or 22–36 Chinese characters per measure. Necessary poster date, location and action remain at least 30 at S = 1000; secondary details may use the 22 role for close reading. Captions never fall below 18. Reference figures are consistent along a shared axis. Tiny copy never imitates the reference's texture.

On web pages recompose to 16–20 px body, 1.5–1.7 leading, and labels/captions of at least 14 px; necessary facts use body size. Give the principal contact/action real 700 at 20–22 px and its supporting label 400 at 14–16 px. Keep a decisive serif lead and the same script assignments. Narrow screens change breaks, field order and crop; never scale the desktop composition as one image. Links name the action, support keyboard use and show focus.

## Colors

Schönbrunn yellow **#FBD26A** is the edition's identity field: a masthead, reading column or selected plane. Use this value as this project’s screen palette. Use paper for quieter secondary reading instead of automatically filling the entire page with yellow. Ink **#151515** carries every reading role, rule and action on yellow and paper. Paper **#FFFFFF** also reverses on ink. Secondary **#565656** is subordinate reading on paper, never miniature copy on yellow.

Reset every foreground when the field changes. White text on yellow fails contrast and is not used. A new edition may replace the hue while retaining the role: select the higher-contrast of black and white, using house ink only if it passes. Measure text on actual image pixels; move reading to a quiet field or deliberate caption strip where needed. No second chromatic accent, gradient, metallic effect or shadow supplies luxury.

## Shapes

Fields and natural letterforms do the work. A rectangular plate joins grid edges; a rule of 1–2 at S = 1000 separates actual reading groups. A stronger frame is reserved for a specimen needing a boundary. A rounded enclosure or stepped edge may organize particular material when its construction is declared; neither becomes recurring furniture.

Depth comes from photographic overlap, an edited edge or real glyph aperture. Keep contours continuous where they join paper and photograph. Crop marks, arrows, icons, capsules and panels are not decorative editorial credentials. The subject's gesture or title's silhouette supplies the decisive boundary.

## Forms

- Cover / opener: an identifying serif word or sentence and an edited image or colour field; facts keep an independent axis, never a montage of previews.
- Statement: one sentence leads in a calm field; no compulsory photo or numeral.
- Exhibit: isolate one image, letter or specimen and label it clearly; avoid equal competing spectacles.
- Essay / argument: a serif judgment title, readable grotesque paragraphs and adjoining evidence; continue at the same measure before reducing type.
- Collection / portfolio: align peers and attach each caption directly; change image height for its gesture without suggesting a ranking.
- Sequence / comparison: preserve actual order or shared evidence scales with direct labels; crop freedom never creates false differences.
- Programme / reference: align time, item and attribution on shared rows, or use a label/value ledger; the title supplies spectacle and lookup stays quiet.
- Data: give quantities units and direct labels in a table or honest chart; never encode counts with campaign framing or arbitrary numeral area.
- Exercise: one clear instruction and usable answer space; an image is task material, not a hidden answer.
- Close / contact: one proved claim or usable action in the same type and field roles; no invented testimonial or ornamental button.

## Variations

An image-led piece may turn from yellow to an ink plate and paper reading, keeping yellow structural. A quiet reference reduces display amplitude and image area while retaining its serif and shared axes. A real content turn can move a field edge, crop or title scale; peers keep shared measures. No variation is compulsory.

## Do's and Don'ts

Render at actual size and as thumbnails. Verify one lead, visible type levels, comfortable ink gaps, natural photographic proportions, intact necessary facts and complete foreground resets. Reading stays live; facts are exact and generated imagery declared. Require 4.5:1 for reading and 3:1 for large type and essential cues. Verify the actual deliverable and responsive widths; screen PNGs do not establish a print proof.

Test: name the specific material decision visible in the lead. For an image-led speaking unit, changing the gesture should force a meaningful change to its composition, not just a new file in the same frame. This counterfactual is not imposed on quiet lookup pages. Remove yellow and the type/image/field relationships still form one editorial family. A quiet page belongs without a forced collision. A new subject made from this file must retain script-specific serif silhouettes, shared axes and crop logic, rather than merely a font name and swatch.

Caricature: a generic serif called fashion; heavy CJK masking the chosen Latin face; tiny prose; borrowed marks; metallic luxury effects; the same cutout or giant numeral repeated without a reading job.
