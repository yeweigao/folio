# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by at the museum's windows and in the street, two to five metres away, reading English and Chinese equally (assumed). In three seconds: what (THE GRID, 格网), when, where. Next: they come on the night, without booking.

## The idea

On this surface, *the evening's clock* becomes *the module*: 12 columns are 19:00 to 22:00 at 15 minutes each, so the starts (19:30, 20:15, 21:00) land on column lines and each talk is a primitive hung from its start: The Module a red square, The System a blue circle, The Interval a yellow bar.

## Assumptions and gaps

- Shapes are 4 modules wide by the doubling rule; only the left edge, on the thread, is data. Talk lengths are not given.
- The 15-minute column is not printed.
- Which talk takes which form follows the series sequence (square, circle, bar); the brief does not state it.
- The programme is fictional; the foot band says `Illustrative example`.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline, the fallbacks change the design.
