# Apple + Engineering style — the mechanism as proof

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s is made: show how it works — a section, an exploded view, a diagram, a figure — and let the mechanism be the hero; never a mood without a mechanism, or a performance claim with nothing to check it by.

Airflow and motors, exposed kits, modular systems: the proof is a mechanism.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **The mechanism joins or replaces the photograph** — in section or diagram, or the product in use with its working visible.
- **Every performance claim carries a figure or a section,** the figure on a shared baseline.
- **Diagrams are native:** flat, one line weight, few node types, cropped tight so the mechanism holds its size; a system too wide for its slot shows part on the cover and the whole on the proof unit.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Dyson — airflow as story.** Signature: airflow, motor and filtration in section and diagram; the product in use with its mechanism visible; performance figures as the proof. Goes wrong as: a lifestyle photograph with no section or figure; a rendered explosion that cannot be read.
- **Teenage Engineering — the exposed kit.** Signature: light grey and white with one signal orange; visible screws and joints; tiny tracked labels like a panel legend; a product that reads as a kit. Goes wrong as: a hidden mechanism; colour as decoration; play that hides how it works.
- **Vitsoe — the modular system as proof.** Signature: one module, every part a whole multiple of it; the system shown assembled and disassembled on white. Goes wrong as: a dimension off the module; a system shown only assembled.
- **Visitor:** Braun, one Rams move — a control that does exactly one thing.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, the product as sculpture, the single accent, drawing native when it explains.

**Test:** Cover the lifestyle photograph: section, diagram and figure still prove how it works. Cover the figure: the section alone still shows the mechanism.

**Caricature:** a spec sheet under a lifestyle photograph; a 3D explosion that cannot be read; every node type in one diagram.
