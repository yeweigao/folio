# Apple + Consumer style — the object in a life

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s is made: the object or the place shown in the life it belongs to, photography leading, colour drawn from that life, the shop quiet beneath; never a spec sheet posing as a life, or chrome louder than the photograph.

Wearables, sport, marketplaces, homes. The proof is a life: a ring worn daily, a shoe in motion, a home stayed in.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **The life replaces the plinth.** The object is photographed in use, not on a solid field; cover text sits in a calm zone of the photograph, never on a plate.
- **The shop stays quiet.** Search, tags, cards and purchase bars neutral and consistent; colour comes from the life or the product, and sale or success colours only as pricing signals.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Oura — the warm paper wellness.** Signature: cream and earth — mustard, clay, olive; a high-contrast editorial serif with a quiet grotesque; photography-led layouts; one decisive action colour. Goes wrong as: a cool tech page; a sans-only display; a spec sheet posing as a life.
- **Nike — the kinetic commerce.** Signature: towering uppercase lockups on campaign photography, above dense near-monochrome retail: black pill actions, grey search and tag pills, tight product cards. Goes wrong as: a quiet headline; a chromatic chrome element; a card without a photograph.
- **Airbnb — the generous marketplace.** Signature: white; its rose-red on every primary action, search and rating; modest display weights that trust photography and space; pill search; softly rounded cards. Goes wrong as: a heavy display face; hard corners; a second voltage.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, the single accent, the change of ground as divider.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the life — a rhythm, a motion, a stay — still reads from photograph and type alone. Swap the brand: another site, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** stock lifestyle photographs; star ratings and promo badges; chrome louder than the photograph.
