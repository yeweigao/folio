# Apple + Consumer style — the object in a life

**Invocation:** Apple + Consumer — Make the page as a consumer brand's is made: the object or the place shown in the life it belongs to, photography leading, colour drawn from that life, the shop quiet beneath it; never a spec sheet posing as a life, or chrome louder than the photograph.

Wellness wearables, sport, marketplaces, homes. The proof is a life — a ring worn daily, a shoe in motion, a home stayed in; spoken in Apple, the gallery becomes a page where the reader already pictures the object in their own life.

- **Photography leads; the shop stays quiet.** Search, tags, cards and purchase bars stay neutral and consistent, so commerce never interrupts the life. Cover text sits in a calm zone of the photograph, never on a plate.
- **Colour comes from the life or the product.** Earth from a wellness life; sale and success colours only as pricing signals; one marketplace voltage for actions. A chromatic chrome element is decoration.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Oura — the warm paper wellness.** A high-contrast editorial serif with a quiet grotesque; cream and earth — mustard, clay, olive; photography-led layouts; one decisive action blue on calm surfaces. Suits wearables and daily rhythms. Never: a cool page, a sans-only display, a second action colour.
- **Nike — the kinetic commerce.** Towering uppercase lockups burned into campaign photography, above dense near-monochrome retail chrome: black pill actions, grey search and tag pills, tight product cards; sale and success the only accents. Suits sport and kinetic campaigns. Never: a quiet headline, a chromatic chrome element, a card without a photograph.
- **Airbnb — the generous marketplace.** A clean white canvas; one rose-red voltage for every primary action, search orb and rating; modest display weights that trust photography and space over type; pill search bars; softly rounded cards. Suits marketplaces, travel and homes. Never: a heavy display face, hard-cornered cards, a second voltage.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent, the product as sculpture, one lead per screen, the change of ground as divider.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the life — a rhythm, a motion, a stay — still reads from photograph and type alone. Swap the hand: another brand, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a spec sheet posing as a life; chrome louder than the photograph; a chromatic chrome element; a quiet Nike headline, a heavy Airbnb one.
