# Apple + Keynote style — the presented deck

**Invocation:** Apple + Keynote — Make the deck as a launch is presented: one thesis per slide, live text over a calm zone, one dominant image; never a web tile shrunk onto a slide, or text baked into a picture.

The launch keynotes, from the stage reveal to the feature walkthrough. The tile becomes one slide, a sequence of tiles a sequence of scenes. A surface, not an industry: it governs structure, and the dialect's hand governs look. Read with the slides surface, projected.

- **One thesis, one slide.** The title states the claim; the body proves or shows it. A slide that needs two heroes is two slides. One number per slide, unless comparison is the point: then a figure set shares one baseline, the decisive figure in accent, the rest muted.
- **Text stays live.** Titles, notes and body are editable text over the image, never in it. The image keeps 25–35% of its frame calmer than the hero area for the text; nothing critical in the outer 8%.
- **One dominant subject per slide:** a cover, divider, concept, comparison, data, workflow or closing poster.
- **Builds order attention.** Cuts between slides; a change of ground at a turn; no transition by default. Reduced motion is the same deck, static.
- **Steps, on a 13.33 × 7.5 in slide, are a floor.** Cover title 72 pt / 600 / 1.05; slide title 54 / 600 / 1.10; body 20 / 400 / 1.47; caption 14; fine print 12; a display number at 90 or more / 600, its label beneath, the group mid-frame. A thesis that will not hold these steps is two slides. Covers vary across a set.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **The keynote presenter — the launch stage.** Reveal one idea at a time, at the largest size the room allows; the next slide is a new scene. Suits presentations and announcements. Never: a slide read while the speaker talks.
- **The gallery hand — the product pages.** Photograph the subject as a sculpture on a solid field, then set the live title in the calm zone it leaves. Suits covers, dividers and exhibits. Never: a photograph crowded by copy.
- **The data hand — the numbers slide.** One number at display size, its label beneath, its context in one quiet line. Suits data and proof. Never: a chart that needs a legend.

**Apple keeps:** the tile rhythm as a slide sequence, the single accent, the Display and Text cuts, the single product shadow, parchment and near-black fields.

**Test:** Projected, every slide reads from the back of the room in one glance; without its image, each title still states one claim.

**Caricature:** a webpage screenshot on a slide; text baked into a full-page picture; bullet walls; a transition on every slide; a chart with a legend, a grid and a gradient.
