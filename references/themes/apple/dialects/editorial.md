# Apple + Editorial style — the long read and the vista

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› + Editorial — Make the long read as Apple's environment pages are made: light leads, a vista where a product tile would be, the argument carried by atmosphere and fact together; never a product tile stretched into an essay, or a vista as wallpaper.

A surface, not a brand: it governs structure, the brand governs look.

- **The lead leads, at 300.** Leads 24 px / 300 / 1.5; reading text 17 / 400 / 1.47.
- **A vista, of three kinds:** a photograph; where the subject has none, the industry's proof — an interface, a dashboard, a material — at full bleed; or a section or cutaway readable as a diagram. Each takes one headline and at most one action.
- **A frame opens the story; it does not hold it.** In a fixed frame the vista dominates and the headline sits on it at display scale; what does not fit is the next page.
- **One column, generous air.** About 980 px, centred, its text flush left; only the footer goes dense.
- **Facts stay checkable.** A claim the vista makes is stated beside it, sourced in a caption.

**Goes wrong as:** a stock vista under a slogan; body at 300, too light to read; three headlines on one vista; a centred body column; a footer as dense as the story.

**Test:** Remove the vista: the leads still make the argument, in order. Remove the text: the vista alone still names one subject, not a mood.
