# Apple + Finance style — the figure that must line up

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s is made: figures that line up and can be believed, one voltage colour spent sparingly, trust carried by restraint; never a dark page posing as a dashboard, a second accent, or a figure that will not align.

Payments, banking, transfers, consumer finance, exchanges. The claim is a number, and the number must be checkable.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **The proof is a figure inside a product** — a dashboard, a card, a balance, a quote — at gallery scale. A crop of a dashboard is a detail; the whole one belongs on a proof unit.
- **Figures line up.** Tabular numerals, or a right-aligned column on the decimal; proportional figures that will not align are a data error.
- **One voltage colour** for action and highlight; everything else neutral, cream or night.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Stripe — the balance sheet as a homepage.** Signature: white; a flowing colour-wave gradient behind the hero only; light display weights with tight tracking; dark dashboard mockups as proof; its indigo on the action. Goes wrong as: gradient on every section; a heavy headline; a dark page.
- **Mercury — two worlds, one pill.** Signature: an ink-violet night homepage and pale product pages, held by one indigo pill; a quiet sans at unusually light weights. Goes wrong as: one world only; a heavy display face.
- **Wise — the transfer as a magazine page.** Signature: its bright lime green against forest-green ink; an unusually heavy display sans; rounded cards each holding a figure. Goes wrong as: a bank-blue page; a timid display; a card without a figure.
- **Revolut — the consumer finance brochure.** Signature: black or off-white; oversized headlines; cards, phones and terminals as hero objects in near-black sections; deep saturated product colours. Goes wrong as: a timid headline; a hero with no product object.
- **Coinbase — the institutional exchange.** Signature: white; one blue spent scarcely; modest display weights; dark editorial heroes carrying interface cards. Goes wrong as: crypto neon; bombast; a decorative shadow.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, the change of ground as divider, the single accent, the interface as visual focus.

**Test:** Cover the voltage colour: the figures still line up and the page still reads as finance. Swap the brand: another site, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a dark page posing as a dashboard; a voltage field; figures that will not align; a glowing chart as hero.
