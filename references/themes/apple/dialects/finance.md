# Apple + Finance style — the figure that must line up

**Invocation:** Apple + Finance — Make the page as a finance brand's is made: figures that line up and can be believed, one voltage colour spent sparingly, trust carried by restraint; never a dark page posing as a dashboard, a second accent, or a figure that will not align.

Payments, business banking, transfers, consumer finance, exchanges. Its claim is a number, and the number must be checkable across rows, cards and pages; spoken in Apple, the gallery becomes a page that is checked, not only admired.

- **Figures line up.** Tabular numerals; where the tool cannot set them, a right-aligned column on the decimal, never claimed as tabular. Proportional figures that will not align are a data error.
- **One voltage colour.** For action and highlight; everything else neutral, cream or night. A voltage field spends the trust the restraint built.
- **The proof is a figure inside a product.** A dashboard, a card, a balance, a quote. A crop of a landscape dashboard reads as a detail; the whole dashboard belongs on a proof unit.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Stripe — the balance sheet as a homepage.** A white ground; a soft gradient wash behind the hero only; light display type, tight tracking; a dark dashboard mockup over the white as proof; cream bands as rhythm. Suits payments and pricing. Never: a dark page, a gradient on every section, a heavy headline.
- **Mercury — two worlds, one pill.** An ink-violet night world on the homepage and a pale light world on product and pricing, held together by one indigo action pill; translucent secondary pills; a quiet sans at unusually light weights. Suits business banking. Never: one world only, a heavy display face.
- **Wise — the transfer as a magazine page.** A pale green-tinted canvas, rounded white cards, a vivid lime accent, sage neutrals, an unusually heavy near-black display sans. Suits transfers and cross-border money. Never: a bank-blue page, a timid display, a card without a figure.
- **Revolut — the consumer finance brochure.** A stark black or off-white canvas; a cobalt-violet brand colour among deep saturated product colours; oversized headlines; product objects — cards, phones, terminals — full width inside near-black sections; pills and rounded cards. Suits consumer apps and cards. Never: a timid headline, a hero without a product object.
- **Coinbase — the institutional exchange.** A white canvas; one blue spent scarcely on primary actions; modest display weights for editorial calm; white sections, grey bands and dark editorial heroes in rotation; depth from card on card, never a decorative shadow. Suits exchanges and institutional crypto. Never: bombast, a voltage spent everywhere.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent, the change of ground as divider, one lead per screen, the interface as visual focus.

**Test:** Cover the voltage colour: the figures still line up and the page still reads as finance, not a generic landing page. Swap the hand: another brand, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a dark page posing as a dashboard; a gradient on every section; a voltage field; figures that will not align; bombast on an institutional page, timidity on a consumer one.
