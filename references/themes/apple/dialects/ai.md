# Apple + AI style — making intelligence tangible

**Invocation:** Make work as an AI brand's page is made: Apple as the base, then one brand from this industry as the governing hand, chosen by what kind of intelligence the input claims; intelligence itself has no photograph, so each brand lends it a material — paper, night, sunset — and the page must never pretend the material is the intelligence; never a generic blue gradient, never a robot illustration, never a claim without a surface to check it on.

The AI industry, from assistants to frontier models to studios. What separates it from other industries is that its subject cannot be photographed: there is no object, no interface that is the product in the way a dashboard is, no figure that lines up. Each brand therefore invents a material to stand in for intelligence — a warm paper, a dark cosmos, a sunset — and the choice of material is the design. Spoken in Apple, it keeps the single-accent discipline, the weight-led hierarchy and one lead per screen, and turns the gallery into a page where an abstract claim is given something a reader can look at and trust.

Choose this dialect when the input is an assistant, a model, a studio, or any subject whose claim is intelligence, generation or understanding. Then choose one brand inside it as the governing hand, by what kind of intelligence it claims — humanist and editorial, engineered and cosmic, or warm and open — not by which brand is most famous, and not by picking two at once.

- **The industry is the router; the brand is the look.** Industry names the problem — intelligence has no photograph — and the brand hand supplies the material that stands in for it: cream paper, near-black cosmos, sunset gradient. An AI dialect that names no brand has no material; a material used without the industry's problem has no reason.
- **One governing hand per work.** The chosen brand governs the whole work; other brands in this industry visit only as single moves, one detail each. Two governing brands on one unit — Claude's cream paper under xAI's sunset, for example — is two dialects, not one.
- **The material is a stand-in, and must be named as one.** Paper, night and sunset are materials for an abstract claim, not proof of it. The page still needs something checkable — a product surface, a model card, a code or chat surface — or the material becomes decoration pretending to be evidence.
- **No generic AI look exists in this dialect.** Cool blue plus slate, a robot, a glowing orb and a gradient wash are not a brand; they are the absence of one. Any page that could belong to any AI company has failed to choose a hand.
- **No values are specified here.** Ground, accent, type and button shape are drawn from the chosen brand's own knowledge, checked against that brand's philosophy below. A value that contradicts the philosophy is wrong even if it matches a remembered brand detail.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Claude — the warm paper editorial.** A tinted cream canvas, serif display headlines, warm coral calls to action, dark navy product surfaces for code and model showcases, a humanist sans for body, cream feature cards. The brand voltage is the cream and coral pairing, deliberately warm and humanist where most AI brands use cool blue and slate. Suits assistants and editorial AI that claim to be humane. Never: a cool blue page, a sans-only display, a gradient wash, a claim without a product surface to check it on.
- **xAI — the engineered cosmos.** A strict near-black canvas broken only by white pill outlines, occasional warm sunset and dusk gradient accents, a geometric sans for display, an uppercase tracked monospace caption face. The whole system reads as engineered and cosmic, unmarketed. Suits frontier models that claim scale and engineering. Never: a friendly rounded page, a coloured field, a robot illustration, a gradient as the page itself.
- **Mistral — the sunset as signature.** Atmospheric sunset gradients layered over photography, a horizontal sunset stripe bar closing every page, warm cream-yellow surfaces, a saturated orange primary action, a near-serif voice for hero displays. The sunset is the signature, repeated until it is unmistakable. Suits open and European AI that claims warmth and openness. Never: a cool blue page, a sunset used once and forgotten, a sans-only hero that loses the near-serif voice.

**Master edition rules.** Internal padding is never zero: text never touches its box edge, and title boxes are taller than their type so a detail title never overlaps its product card. A product-surface crop shows the screen interior only; chrome peeking at an edge means the crop is rejected and redone. A label that wraps into its context line is shortened, not squeezed. Serif display is a substitute, never embedded, so spacing is buffered against substitution shifting line breaks.

**Alignment and accent, restated.** When a hand from this dialect governs, its single accent replaces every Apple blue role for that work, and Apple blues retire for that work. Body, leads, captions, card names and prices are flush left, ragged right, in every surface and format; only a hero, vista or cover headline may centre.

**continuous corner:** A hand may choose square forms; every nonzero radius uses Apple continuous corner. Radius sets size, not contour. Nested corners share their centre and subtract the gap from the outer radius for the inner one. Use the theme's native API or continuous corner path; plain circular rounding and a superellipse are forbidden. Write this construction into the standalone DESIGN.md.

**Apple keeps:** the single-accent discipline, weight doing the hierarchy, whitespace as divider, one lead per screen.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the material — paper, night or sunset — still reads, and the page still carries something checkable beyond the material. Swap the governing hand: the same intelligence claim must look like a different AI brand, not the same page with a colour changed.

**Caricature:** a generic blue gradient with a robot; a glowing orb as the whole page; a material with nothing checkable on it; two governing brands on one page; a cool blue page pretending to be Claude, or a friendly rounded page pretending to be xAI.
