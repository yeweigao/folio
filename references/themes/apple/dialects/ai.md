# Apple + AI style — making intelligence tangible

**Invocation:** Apple + AI — Make the page as an AI brand's is made: intelligence has no photograph, so the hand lends it a material — paper, night, sunset — and sets something checkable on it; never a generic blue gradient, a robot, or a claim with no surface to check.

Assistants, frontier models, studios. The subject cannot be photographed: there is no object, and no figure that lines up. Each brand invents a material to stand in for intelligence, and the choice of material is the design; spoken in Apple, an abstract claim is given something a reader can look at and trust.

- **The material stands in; it never proves.** Paper, night or sunset carries the mood; a product surface — a chat, code, a model card — carries the claim. Its crop shows the screen interior only.
- **No generic AI look.** Cool blue on slate, a robot, a glowing orb, a gradient wash: the absence of a hand. A page that could belong to any AI company has not chosen one.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Claude — the warm paper editorial.** A tinted cream canvas; serif display headlines; warm coral actions; dark navy product surfaces for code and model showcases; a humanist sans for body; cream feature cards. Warm and humanist where the industry runs cool. Suits assistants and editorial AI. Never: a cool blue page, a sans-only display, a gradient wash.
- **xAI — the engineered cosmos.** A strict near-black canvas broken only by white pill outlines; rare warm dusk gradients; a geometric display sans; tracked uppercase mono captions. Engineered and unmarketed. Suits frontier models claiming scale. Never: a friendly rounded page, a coloured field, a gradient as the page.
- **Mistral — the sunset as signature.** Sunset gradients over photography; a horizontal sunset stripe closing every page; warm cream-yellow surfaces; a saturated orange action; a near-serif hero voice. Repeated until unmistakable. Suits open and European AI. Never: a cool blue page, a sunset used once and forgotten.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent, weight as hierarchy, the change of ground as divider, one lead per screen.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the material still reads, and something checkable still sits on it. Swap the hand: the same claim becomes another AI brand, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a blue gradient with a robot; a glowing orb as the whole page; a material with nothing checkable on it; a cool page posing as Claude, a friendly rounded one posing as xAI.
