# Apple + AI style — making intelligence tangible

**Invocation:** Apple ‹+ brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s is made, or Apple's own: intelligence has no photograph, so the brand lends it a material — paper, night, sunset — and sets something checkable on it; never a generic blue gradient, a robot, or a claim with no surface to check.

Assistants, models, studios. The subject cannot be photographed; each brand's choice of material is the design.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **A material replaces the product photograph.** It carries the mood; a product surface — a chat, code, a model card, readable, its crop the screen interior only — carries the claim.
- **The assistant's own sign is product, not decoration.** An orb, a glow or a gradient that shows the assistant at work may lead; the same device as ambience is the caricature.
- **Apple alone, the material is Apple's:** the glow that spreads from the product surface, on Apple's grounds.
- **No generic AI look.** A page that could belong to any AI company has not chosen a material.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Claude — the warm paper editorial.** Signature: a tinted cream canvas; serif display headlines; a warm coral-terracotta action; dark product surfaces for code and conversation; a humanist sans for body. Goes wrong as: a cool blue page; a sans-only display; a gradient wash; an orange glow.
- **xAI — the engineered cosmos.** Signature: strict near-black broken only by white outline pills; a geometric display sans; tracked uppercase mono captions; rare warm dusk light. Goes wrong as: a friendly rounded page; a coloured field; a gradient as the page.
- **Mistral — the sunset as signature.** Signature: warm sunset stripes in orange through yellow, repeated until unmistakable; warm cream surfaces; a saturated orange action; a blocky, pixel-built mark. Goes wrong as: a cool blue page; the sunset used once and forgotten.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, weight as hierarchy, the change of ground as divider, the single accent.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the material still reads, and something checkable sits on it. Swap the brand: the same claim becomes another AI site, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a blue-violet gradient with a robot; an orb or glow with nothing it does; a material with nothing checkable on it.
