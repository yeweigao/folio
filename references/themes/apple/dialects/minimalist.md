# Apple + Minimalist style — nothing unnecessary

**Invocation:** Apple + Minimalist — Make the page as a minimalist product's is made: remove whatever does not change how the object is used, let material and function carry it, let quiet be the brand; never decoration, a loud mark, or a feature list posing as a product.

Industrial design, no-brand retail, minimal audio. The claim is subtraction itself: the product is good because nothing unnecessary remains. Apple already subtracts; this school subtracts the brand as well. A mechanism to show is Engineering; craft and heritage, Luxury.

- **Subtraction is judged by use, not emptiness.** Stop at the last element that changes use; an empty page that is hard to use has mistaken emptiness for minimalism.
- **Material is the colour.** Neutral grounds, aluminium, wood, paper, plain packaging; colour only from the material. A hand with no chromatic accent gets none, and Apple blue does not decorate.
- **The mark is quiet or absent.** The object, not the logo, is the identity.
- **One quiet grotesque.** Regular weights, generous leading, no display face competing with the object.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Braun — less, but better.** Dieter Rams's ten principles made into products: functional, honest, unobtrusive, long-lasting, as little design as possible; radios and record players as systems whose parts combine. Suits systems and appliances. Never: a feature that does not change use, parts that do not combine.
- **MUJI — no-brand quality.** Only what is necessary: plain packaging, natural materials, no mark shouting, products that fit any life because they claim none. Suits everyday products, home and stationery. Never: a loud mark, a product that demands attention.
- **Bang & Olufsen — material minimal audio.** Aluminium, wood and textile as the palette; the product as sculpture in a quiet room; sound the only performance claim. Suits audio and home tech. Never: a plastic chrome detail, a feature list posing as sound.
- **Visitor:** Aesop, the shop as a quiet gallery of amber glass and plain labels.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent where there is one, the product as sculpture, one lead per screen, reduction as method.

**Test:** Cover the brand name: the object still reads as functional and material. Add one element back: if the page is no worse without it, it never belonged.

**Caricature:** an empty page that is hard to use; a loud logo on a quiet product; a decorative colour; a feature list posing as minimalism; a display face competing with the object.
