# Apple + Showroom style — the object, the message, the price

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s showroom is made: the photograph proves the object, one message per screen, and buying is part of looking; never a card grid posing as a showroom, chrome over the photograph, or a price kept for the end.

Cars, rockets, drones, cameras: a physical object at full scale, its silhouette and material judged by eye.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **The photograph is the page.** The object at the largest scale the screen allows; chrome frosted, ghosted or absent; the claim in a calm zone of the image, shortened until it reads there.
- **Price stays, or is deliberately absent.** A selling page keeps price and configurator in view while the photograph changes; an inspiring page shows none.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Tesla — the showroom with the price left in.** Signature: one full-viewport photograph and one message per screen; normal tracking, two weights; small rectangular buttons, its blue on the primary only; price and configurator always visible; no gradient, shadow or border. Goes wrong as: pills; a heavy headline; three messages on one screen.
- **SpaceX — the mission as austerity.** Signature: pure black; full-bleed photograph and video of rockets and landscapes; uppercase display in tight leading; one ghost outlined button per band. Goes wrong as: a coloured accent; a filled pill; a card grid.
- **DJI — the studio showroom in tiles.** Signature: white; studio renders in square tiles in a white gutter; every action a rounded pill in one blue; small tier labels in a product colour. Goes wrong as: one hero posing as the range; a tile without a tier or price.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, the product as sculpture, the single accent, empty photograph as divider.

**Test:** Cover the accent: photograph, message and price — or its absence — read in that order. Swap the brand: another showroom, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** the object in a card grid under a discount banner; chrome over the photograph; a price hidden until checkout; a gradient wash.
