# Apple + Showroom style — the object, the message, the price

**Invocation:** Apple + Showroom — Make the page as a showroom is made: the photograph proves the object, one message per screen, and buying is part of looking; never a card grid posing as a showroom, chrome over the photograph, or a price kept for the end.

Cars, rockets, drones, cameras: a physical object at full scale, its silhouette and material judged by eye. Spoken in Apple, the gallery becomes a room the visitor walks through, object to object.

- **The photograph carries the page.** The object at the largest scale the screen allows; chrome frosted, ghosted or absent.
- **Price stays, or is deliberately absent.** A selling page keeps price and configurator in view while the photograph changes; an inspiring page shows no price, and no checkout chrome creeps in.
- **The claim sits where the photograph is calm.** A cover line goes in a quiet zone of the image and is shortened until it reads there.
- **Covers vary across a set.** Full bleed and half frame both; one cover structure repeated is not a system.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Tesla — the showroom with the price left in.** One full-viewport photograph and one message per screen; normal tracking, an explicit break with Apple; two weights, the heavier restrained; small square buttons; one electric blue for the primary action; price and configurator always visible; no gradient, shadow or border; a frosted nav. Suits cars, ranges and configurators. Never: a pill, a heavy headline, three messages on one screen.
- **SpaceX — the mission as austerity.** A pure black canvas; full-bleed photographic and video heroes; uppercase display in tight leading; one ghost outlined pill per band; all-caps eyebrows; black, white and the imagery. Suits missions and claims of scale. Never: a coloured accent, a filled pill, a card grid.
- **DJI — the studio showroom in tiles.** Full-bleed studio renders in square tiles in a white gutter; every action a rounded pill in one engineering blue; tight near-black type at stepped opacities; small tier labels in a product colour; option cards and a sticky purchase bar. Suits drones, cameras and ranges sold by tile. Never: a single hero posing as the range, a tile without a tier or price.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent, the product as sculpture, one lead per screen, the empty photograph as divider.

**Test:** Cover the accent: photograph, message and price — or its absence — read in that order. Swap the hand: another showroom, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a card grid posing as a showroom; chrome over the photograph; a pill in a Tesla work, a filled pill in a SpaceX work; a gradient wash; a price hidden until checkout.
