# Apple + Store style — the shop and configurator

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› + Store — Make the page as the Apple Store is made: white cards on a hairline grid, chips for choices, a price beside every name, one buy action that never moves; never a gallery tile forced to sell, or chrome louder than the product.

A surface, not a brand: the denser tool mode of the same grammar. It governs structure, the brand governs look.

- **Cards, not tiles.** White on parchment, 1 px hairline, radius 18, padding 24. The image slot on top takes the industry's proof at one shared crop — photograph, interface, dashboard, material; name and price below at body size, flush left. With no price, the second line carries a plan, a figure or an action.
- **Chips for choices.** A pill per option: thumbnail, label, price difference; the selected chip takes a 2 px border in the action colour, and nothing else changes.
- **Comparison is shared scale.** Variants in one row, one crop, one scale, direct labels; the decisive difference alone takes the accent.
- **The buy action stays put.** A frosted sticky bar: the running price left, the buy pill right.

**Goes wrong as:** a red discount banner; stars as decoration; a card with a shadow, a badge and a gradient; a choice hidden in a paragraph; a price larger than the product name.

**Test:** Cover the prices: the grid still reads as one product family. Cover the photographs: name, price and choice still let a buyer decide.
