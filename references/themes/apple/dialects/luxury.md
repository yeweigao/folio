# Apple + Luxury style — craft, heritage and one product

**Invocation:** Apple + Luxury — Make the page as a house's is made: craft and heritage prove the claim, one product carries the page, serif speaks where Apple speaks sans, one signature colour; never a spec sheet, chrome over the photograph, or another house's mark, pattern or photograph.

Fashion, leather, jewellery, watches, premium beauty. The proof is craft made visible: stitching at detail scale, a garment in weather, a movement, a house's history carried by one product. Spoken in Apple, the restraint stays and the gallery becomes a house. A car or configurator is Showroom; subtraction is Minimalist.

- **Serif is the display voice; that is the overturn.** A Didone or house serif for display, a quiet grotesque for labels; a sans-only page has kept Apple's voice and lost the house's. Space allows for a substituted serif: a wrapped title takes a taller box, and the body moves.
- **One signature colour,** the house's own, as a pill, link or highlight; never a field or wash.
- **Price is quiet, craft is loud.** Price present, never larger than the product name, never the headline.
- **Nothing of a house is borrowed.** A check, monogram, knight, interlocking mark or photograph belongs to its house; the form transfers, the marks never.

**Hands in this school.** One governs; the others visit as single moves.
- **Burberry — heritage restored.** A serif wordmark restored after a sans era; a historic knight; one trench coat carrying the house; British weather, function and realism as the narrative; one saturated house blue; the house pattern as the product's own motif. Suits heritage and outerwear. Never: a sans wordmark, a range with no single product.
- **Hermès — craft in minimalism.** A neutral palette that lets the product shine; crisp product and softened surroundings; refined type; craft detail as proof; one orange, the only accent. Suits leather and timeless products. Never: a second accent, a craft claim without a detail photograph.
- **Chanel — timeless black and white.** Black and white as foundation, clean lines, ample space, classic type, the product leading. Suits timeless fashion and beauty. Never: a colour field, a crowded grid, a headline louder than the product.
- **Louis Vuitton — the digital flagship.** The site as a flagship store: immersive photography and video of leather and stitching at detail scale, elegant layouts, interaction that lets the visitor explore without being overwhelmed. Suits flagship ranges. Never: a catalogue grid posing as a flagship, animation that decorates rather than reveals craft.
- **Visitors:** Tiffany, one colour as identity; Cartier and Rolex, precision photographed at a scale where it can be judged; Dior, a cinematic serif narrative with the product inside it.

**Apple keeps:** the single accent, the product as sculpture, one lead per screen, space as divider.

**Test:** Cover the signature colour: craft, heritage and the single product still read, in that order. Swap the hand: another house, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** a borrowed check, monogram or knight; a second accent; the signature colour as a wash; a price larger than the name; a spec sheet posing as a house; a sans-only display.
