# Apple + Luxury style — craft, heritage and one product

**Invocation:** Apple + ‹brand› style — Make the page as ‹brand›'s house is made: craft and heritage prove the claim, one product carries the page, serif speaks where Apple speaks sans, one signature colour; never a spec sheet, chrome over the photograph, or another house's mark, pattern or photograph.

Fashion, leather, jewellery, watches, beauty. The proof is craft made visible: stitching at detail scale, a garment in weather, a movement, a house's history in one product.

**What the industry overturns in Apple**
- **Serif is the display voice.** A Didone or house serif for display, a quiet grotesque for labels; a sans-only page has kept Apple's voice and lost the house's.
- **Craft is loud, price is quiet.** A detail photograph proves each craft claim; price is present, never larger than the product name.
- **Nothing of a house is borrowed.** A check, monogram, knight or interlocking mark belongs to its house; the form transfers, never the marks.

**Brands.** One governs; the name carries its look, the signature anchors it.

- **Burberry — heritage restored.** Signature: the restored serif wordmark and equestrian knight; one trench coat carrying the house; British weather as the narrative; its saturated knight blue. Goes wrong as: the check everywhere; a sans wordmark; a range with no single product.
- **Hermès — craft in minimalism.** Signature: a white page; its orange, the only accent; crisp product against softened, neutral surroundings; craft detail as proof; playful drawn moments that never crowd the product. Goes wrong as: a second accent; a craft claim without a detail photograph.
- **Chanel — timeless black and white.** Signature: black and white only; clean lines; ample space; classic type; the product leading without shouting. Goes wrong as: a colour field; gold; a headline louder than the product.
- **Louis Vuitton — the digital flagship.** Signature: white and black, the brown kept for the leather itself; the site as a flagship store; immersive photography and film of leather and stitching at detail scale; elegant spare layouts. Goes wrong as: the monogram as wallpaper; a catalogue grid posing as a flagship; animation that decorates.
- **Visitors:** Tiffany, one blue as identity; Cartier and Rolex, precision photographed where it can be judged; Dior, a cinematic serif narrative.

**Apple keeps:** one lead per screen, the product as sculpture, the single accent, space as divider.

**Test:** Cover the signature colour: craft, heritage and the single product still read, in that order. Swap the brand: another house, not a recolour.

**Caricature:** fake luxury — gold, glowing outlines, spaced serif capitals over a stock photograph; a borrowed pattern; a price larger than the name.
