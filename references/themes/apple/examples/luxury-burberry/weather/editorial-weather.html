A house on this coast keeps a weather record, not a lookbook.

这间屋子留的是天气记录，不是造型册

The house keeps a register of every front that crosses this coast. A coat is cut to answer a measurement, not a season: how much water arrives, how long it keeps arriving, and from which direction the wind carries it.

A lookbook asks what the coat looks like. The register asks what it has to withstand. That is why the same cloth appears across the whole range, and why the only thing that changes between coats is how much weather each one is made for.
