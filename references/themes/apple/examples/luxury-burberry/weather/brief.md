# Brief — The weather is the only brief

An Editorial opener and its first reading column, about the weather record a
house keeps instead of a lookbook. The house, the place, the register and every
figure are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Highland`
- `The weather is the only brief`
- `天气是唯一的设计简报`
- `Read the weather record`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`
- `A house on this coast keeps a weather record, not a lookbook.`
- `这间屋子留的是天气记录，不是造型册`
- `Rainfall at Glen Orra`
- `Spring`
- `214 mm`
- `Summer`
- `168 mm`

- `Highland climate register, 2026`
- `Illustrative example`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
weather vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. Every rounded shape uses
the shared continuous-corner path. No Apple product, brand product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
shop. In three seconds: one subject, one sentence. Next: they read the weather
record rather than return to the range.

## The idea

On this surface, *a year of Highland weather* becomes *the house's archive of
proof*: the coat is not shown in a storm to sell a mood but to answer a
climate, so the story is told as a record a reader can check line by line,
and the atmosphere is the argument only until the figures arrive.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: the weather as the only brief, register: base (a
   dark weather field, one headline, one action, no competing detail).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (light field, flush-left
   serif leads and body, one checkable figure with its source).
3. Close — the register line and the illustrative disclaimer, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The vista is an illustrative weather field, declared in the caption, not a
  photograph; atmosphere is its job, never evidence.
- The four rainfall figures are fictional values for an illustrative study;
  the page says so with `Illustrative example`.
- No price, no product photograph and no second action appear in the opener;
  the vista's claim is stated again in text beside the figure.
- The bars share one zero baseline and are labelled directly; no axis is cut
  and no smoothing is invented.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
