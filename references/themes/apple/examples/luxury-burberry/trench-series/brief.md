# Brief — Choose the weather

One Store buy page for the Highland trench series: three coats told as three
kinds of British rain, chosen by weather rather than by colour. The house,
the coats, the prices and the choice are fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. Every rounded
shape uses the shared continuous-corner path. No Apple product, brand product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A buyer on a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, choosing one coat from a small
range. In one glance: the range, the quiet prices, and that one coat is chosen.
Next: they name the weather they walk in, then buy without hunting for the
action.

## The idea

On this surface, *the rain a city gets* becomes *the configurator*: the three
coats differ by the weather each was made for, not by a finish, so the buyer's
first decision is to name a climate, and only then does a product name or a
price come into view.

## Sequence

1. Cover / exhibit — lead: the range claim, register: base (white canvas, one
   serif headline, one quiet lead).
2. Collection / comparison — the weather chips and three coats at a shared
   scale, register: base, denser; one decisive choice takes the house blue.
3. Close — the running selection, total and one buy action, register: pause;
   the action stays put.

## Assumptions and gaps

- One coat drawing is made per card at the same scale and crop, with a slightly
  longer hem for each wetter climate; they are illustrative stand-ins, declared
  in the caption, not photographs of real garments.
- The three rain names are a fictional classification; no meteorological scale
  is quoted.
- All prices are fictional and quiet, never larger than the product name;
  `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- No second accent, no gradient field and no product photograph appear; the
  house blue is the only accent and stays on chips, links and the buy action.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
