# Brief — Nothing added, nothing missing

One Bento range grid for the Noir collection: the whole range read in one
glance, tiles sized by importance, one quiet price per tile and one number
tile that carries its own check. The house, the pieces and the prices are
fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px: a Bento range
grid with a white gutter, tiles of different sizes and one black field turn.
Google Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling
asset. Every rounded shape uses the shared continuous-corner path. No Apple
product, brand product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A visitor on a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, meeting the whole range for
the first time. In one glance: how few pieces the house makes, what each is
called, and that the range is one family. Next: they open the collection
rather than read a list of specifications.

## The idea

On this surface, *one house's restraint* becomes *the range's index*: a
collection that adds nothing chromatic can only be told by how few things
each piece needs, so the grid is read as a count of what was left out, and the
small black number tile is the one tile that states the count.

## Sequence

1. Cover / collection — lead: the wide tile's judgement, register: base
   (white field, one serif claim, one product, one quiet price).
2. Index — the small tiles and the black number tile, register: base, denser;
   one tile turns to black, one claim per tile, prices quiet.
3. Close — one black action and the disclaimers, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- Each garment is drawn as an illustrative stand-in, declared in the caption;
  no house mark, monogram, flower or borrowed pattern appears.
- The `6` is a fictional count for an illustrative range; the tile carries its
  label and context, and `Illustrative example` states the fiction.
- The Bento grid is an index, not proof: each range tile's quiet price is its
  check, and the number tile carries its own checkable figure.
- No chromatic colour, no blue, no gradient and no second accent appear;
  black carries the one action and the one field turn.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
