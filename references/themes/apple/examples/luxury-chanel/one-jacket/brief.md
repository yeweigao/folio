# Brief — The cut has nowhere to hide

One Keynote cover-and-proof slide for a single Noir jacket, told entirely in
black and white: a silhouette, and the one seam left to justify it. The house,
the garment, the section and its dimension are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Noir`
- `The One Seam`
- `一道缝`
- `Black and white leave the cut nowhere to hide.`
- `黑白之间，剪裁无处可藏`
- `outer face`
- `the one seam`
- `inner face`
- `6 mm`
- `seam allowance`
- `See the construction`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Google
Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset.
Every rounded shape uses the shared continuous-corner path. No Apple product,
brand product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a small hall, four to eight metres from the screen,
with a speaker talking. In one glance: one garment, one judgement, and the one
detail that must carry it. Next: they look at the seam section rather than at
a second product.

## The idea

On this surface, *the absence of colour* becomes *the only thing the cut has
to prove*: with black and white as the whole palette, nothing can be carried
by hue, so the jacket is reduced until a single seam is the one detail a
reader can inspect, and that detail becomes the argument for the garment.

## Sequence

1. Cover / statement — lead: the judgement, register: base (white field,
   serif display, one centred silhouette as the exhibit).
2. Exhibit / argument — the seam section, register: pause; a readable section
   with live labels and one dimension, declared as an illustration.
3. Close — one black action and the disclaimers, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The jacket and the seam section are illustrative stand-ins, declared in the
  caption, never a photograph or a real pattern piece; no house mark,
  monogram, flower or borrowed pattern appears.
- `6 mm` is a fictional seam allowance for an illustrative study; the page
  says so with `Illustrative example`.
- Black carries every action role; no chromatic colour, no blue and no second
  accent appear anywhere on the slide.
- No price, no comparison of products and no second figure appear: one
  garment and one detail keep the slide to a single thesis.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
