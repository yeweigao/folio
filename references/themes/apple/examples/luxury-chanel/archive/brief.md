# Brief — A house that argues with dates

An Editorial opener and its first reading column, about the register Noir
keeps instead of a seasonal story. The house, the archive, the dates and every
figure are fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
black vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. Every rounded shape uses
the shared continuous-corner path. No Apple product, brand product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
collection. In three seconds: one subject, one judgement. Next: they read the
register rather than a description of the clothes.

## The idea

On this surface, *the archive's dated register* becomes *the proof of timelessness*: a
house that claims to be timeless cannot argue with a season, so it argues with
a register of pieces a reader can compare year against year, and the figures
beside them are the only things allowed to change.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: dates as the argument, register: base (a black
   field turn with a complete foreground palette, one headline, one action).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (light field, flush-left
   serif leads and body, one checkable register with its source).
3. Close — the register line and the illustrative disclaimer, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The hanging garment in the vista is an illustrative line stand-in, declared
  in the caption, never a photograph; no house mark, monogram, flower or
  borrowed pattern appears.
- `1955`, `1971`, `1998` and `2026` with their figures are fictional entries
  for an illustrative study; `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- The register is a reference form, not a bento index: figures align in a
  right-aligned column so the reader can look a year up.
- No chromatic colour, no blue, no gradient and no second accent appear;
  black carries the field turn and the action, white carries the page.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
