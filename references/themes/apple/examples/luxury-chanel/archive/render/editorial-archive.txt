The archive is kept as a register, not a mood.

档案是一份登记，不是一种情绪

The archive is not a room of beautiful things. It is a set of dated entries: what was made, in which year, and the one measure that changed. A house that claims to be timeless has to keep a record that can be checked.

Nothing in the register is a season. Each line is a piece held to the same cut, and the figure beside it is the only thing that differs. Read down the years and the argument makes itself: the clothes did not need to change.
