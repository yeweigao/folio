# Brief — One bag, three sizes

One Bento page for the shop side of a fictional leather house: the same bag in
three volumes, drawn at one scale, with the dimensions, the volume, the weight
and the price of each set down where a buyer can check them. The house, the
line, the figures and the prices are fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px, built as a Bento
grid that carries the range's comparison and purchase task. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset and every tile,
swatch and chip-shaped edge uses its path at the 18 / 5 steps of the world's
ladder. No chromatic accent: the leather is the only colour, the action is ink.
No house mark, pattern or borrowed photograph.

## Reader and outcome

A buyer on a laptop, fifty to seventy centimetres from the screen, deciding
which of three sizes to carry. In one glance: that the three are one bag in
three volumes, drawn against one scale, and what each costs. Next: they read one
card's litres and weight and add that size to the bag.

## The idea

On this surface, *the range* becomes *one drawing at one scale*: a house that
sells craft cannot compare itself with a table of numbers without losing the
object, so the three sizes stand on one baseline at 1:2 and every figure sits
under the silhouette it belongs to — the craft claims take the loud tiles, the
prices stay at body size.

## Sequence

1. Lead tile — the three silhouettes on one baseline at 1:2, `Only the panels
   grow.`, register: base (bone panel, one drawing).
2. Number tile — `9` hours of hand work, unchanged across the range, register:
   dark turn (a whole near-black tile).
3. Material tile — a calf swatch at 3.2 mm with the two facts a swatch can
   carry, register: base, quieter.
4. Collection — three product tiles, one size each: name, dimensions, litres,
   grams, price, one action, register: densest.
5. Close — the tax line and the two disclaimers, register: pause.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The three silhouettes are one drawing at one scale: 28, 36 and 44 cm read as
  140, 180 and 220 px, so the comparison is a fact of the drawing. The tiles
  below repeat the same elevation at 0.72 of the lead's scale — a reuse at a new
  size, declared here, not a second proof.
- Litres are the external dimensions multiplied; 28 × 20 × 12 = 6.7 L,
  36 × 26 × 15 = 14.0 L, 44 × 31 × 18 = 24.6 L. Weights and prices are fictional
  and consistent with the other two pieces in this world.
- Every bag, the swatch and the hide are drawn stand-ins, declared in the
  caption; the leather gradient is a material tone, not a photograph.
- One number tile only: two posters of equal weight would turn the craft claim
  into a KPI row.
- Prices sit below the name and at body size, never larger than the product
  name.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
