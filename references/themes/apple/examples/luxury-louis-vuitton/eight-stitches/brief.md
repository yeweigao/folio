# Brief — Eight stitches to the inch

One Keynote proof slide for a fictional leather house's seam standard: a single
detail of hand stitching at the scale where its pitch can be counted. The house,
the seam and every figure are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Voyager`
- `Seam note · Automne 2026`
- `Eight stitches to the inch.`
- `一英寸，八针`
- `Three numbers describe a seam. Only the pitch can be counted on the surface.`
- `Pitch`
- `3.17 mm`
- `Count`
- `8 per inch · 3.1 per cm`
- `Thread`
- `0.83 mm linen, 18/3`
- `Hide`
- `3.2 mm calf, two plies`

- `Read the seam note`
- `8 stitches`
- `25.4 mm`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Google Fonts
are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. Colour and
face come from the world's Tokens: white, bone panel, a dark leather ground, ink
and muted, Cormorant Garamond for display, Inter for labels and reading, Noto
Serif SC and Noto Sans SC for Chinese. No chromatic accent, no Apple blue, no
house mark, pattern or borrowed photograph. Every text shape carries internal
padding.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a small hall, four to eight metres from the screen,
with a speaker talking about why a hand seam is worth its hours. In one glance:
one seam, one number, and the fact that the number is countable. Next: they read
the seam note, or they look at the stitches and count them.

## The idea

On this surface, *the pitch of a seam* becomes *the only evidence the house can
offer*: a claim about craft cannot be photographed into proof, but a hundred
millimetres of thread and a marked inch can be counted on the screen, so the
slide gives the count the whole right half and pushes every other number into a
quiet column.

## Sequence

1. Statement / exhibit — lead: `Eight stitches to the inch.`, register: base
   (white field, serif display, one left axis).
2. Detail turn — the seam fills the right field edge to edge as a dark plate,
   register: dark turn (a whole field of leather with a complete foreground
   palette on that ground); two dimension marks and one count are the only
   marks laid over it.
3. Reference — five rows of specification and one quiet action, register:
   pause; the table is the lookup, the plate is the proof.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The window is drawn as an exact inch: nine holes, eight threads, 3.17 mm
  apart, so the count on the page and the count in the sentence are the same
  count. 3.1 per cm is the same pitch stated in the other unit, rounded.
- The seam is a drawn stand-in, declared in the caption; it is read as a
  drawing of a seam, never as a photograph of one.
- One chromatic event only: the leather itself. The action is ink, not a
  coloured pill; no second accent appears anywhere.
- The house, the standard and `seam 04` are fictional; `Illustrative example`
  states this on the slide.
- No price appears: a seam note argues a method, and a price here would give the
  slide a second lead.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting, and the
  display box is tall enough to clear descenders under substitution.
