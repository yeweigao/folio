A night is not one number. It is a line that keeps changing its mind.

一夜不是一个数字，是一条不断改主意的线

The band above is one night, drawn at 1.86 pixels per minute. Each row is a stage, and every vertical step is the ring deciding the stage had changed. Nothing is smoothed and nothing is averaged.

Light sleep took 4 h 18 m across six blocks, more than half of the 7 h 12 m asleep. Deep sleep took 1 h 16 m in two blocks, both finished by 05:06. The line leaves the awake row twice: 22 minutes at 23:52, and 24 minutes at 03:06.

Those two blocks are the whole difference between time in bed and time asleep. A report that printed only the total would call this an ordinary night.
