A bay is not assembled. It is stacked.

一套搁架不是装起来的，是叠起来的

The upright, shelves, brackets, cross-tie and feet all land on the same 45 mm measure. The assembled bay and each loose part share one ruler, drawn at 1:4, so the height of a wall is a count instead of a cut list.

Five part types make one bay. The shelf can move through forty positions without a new upright, and a longer wall adds the same lengths again. This is a modular system by arithmetic: Shelf parts note, 2026.
