The whole bay comes apart
into five parts.

整个单元拆开，只有五种零件

Illustrative render, not a photograph

A bay is not assembled. It is stacked.

一套搁架不是装起来的，是叠起来的

Five parts leave the factory. Two uprights carry the load, shelves hang on brackets, one cross-tie holds the pair, and two feet take the floor. No part is left- or right-handed, and no part is cut for one wall.

The brackets hook into slots at 45 mm intervals, so a shelf's height is a decision rather than a dimension. Move it two slots and it is still the same bracket, in the same hole, at the same price.

The parts on this sheet are the whole system. A longer wall is not a longer part; it is one more bay of the same five, and the ruler under them is why.

That is the only claim the system makes: the parts are few enough to count, and a count is the only thing a longer wall changes.
