# Brief — Shelf, taken apart

An editorial opener and its first reading column: one bay laid out as its five
parts on the module ruler, beside the bay assembled. The system and every
dimension are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Disassembly`

- `整个单元拆开，只有五种零件`
- `assembled`
- `one module, 45 mm`
- `drawn at 1:4`

- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed proof
field at the top holding the parts sheet, over a centred reading column with
flush-left text. Google Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a
local sibling asset. No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader alone at 50–70 cm, arriving from the configuration page. In three
seconds: the bay is five part types, and every part's length is a count of the
same 45 mm. Next: they read why that makes a longer wall cheaper to extend than
to replace.

## The idea

On this surface, *taking the bay apart* becomes *a measurement*: each part is
laid along the same ruler at the same scale as the assembled bay beside it, so
the reader sees that nothing is cut, only counted.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — the parts sheet and the assembled ghost on one ruler, one
   headline; register: dark proof field (a readable drawing, not a photograph).
2. Argument — the column: what the five parts are, how a shelf position is a
   decision, and what a longer wall costs; register: pause.
3. Close — the note line and the declaration; register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The drawing is an illustrative stand-in, declared in the caption; dimensions
  are invented and consistent with one 45 mm module.
- The ghost is drawn at the same 1:4 as the parts, so its height reads against
  the upright's length; the ruler is the shared scale for both.
- No accent is used: the piece has no action, and the drawing's one value
  (white on a dark board) carries the hierarchy.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
