# Brief — Shelf, one module

One launch slide: a single bay drawn on its module, with the counts named and
the dimension chain showing the unit. The system and its dimensions are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Shelf`
- `Module`
- `Every part is a whole number of 45 mm.`
- `每个零件，都是 45 毫米的整数倍`
- `1,800 mm`
- `900 mm`
- `270 mm`
- `135 mm`
- `45 mm`
- `900 mm (20 × 45)`
- `Upright`
- `two per bay`
- `four per bay`
- `two per shelf`
- `Bracket`
- `Change the length and the count changes; nothing new is made.`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Google Fonts
are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple
product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A room at four to eight metres, the presenter talking. In one glance: one bay,
one unit of 45 mm, and every part measured as a multiple of it. Next: they look
at the counts and see that a longer wall is only more of the same parts.

## The idea

On this surface, *the 45 mm module* becomes *the only ruler*: the elevation is
dimensioned in modules rather than millimetres alone, so the reader counts the
system instead of reading a specification list.

## Sequence

1. Statement — lead: every part is a whole multiple of one module, register:
   base (one light field, one reading axis at the left margin).
2. Exhibit — the bay at the right, dimensioned twice: 1,800 mm down the
   upright, 900 mm along the module chain, with three part counts named.
3. Close — the consequence and the declaration, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The elevation is drawn, not photographed, and is declared in the caption.
- The bay is shown without its contents: the slide is about the frame, and a
  loaded shelf would add a second subject.
- Only three part counts are named; the bracket's own multiple is given in the
  body, so the drawing keeps one label per part type.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
