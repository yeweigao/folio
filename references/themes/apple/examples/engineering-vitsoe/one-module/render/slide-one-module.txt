每个零件，都是 45 毫米的整数倍

A bay stands 1,800 mm tall: forty modules of 45. A shelf is 900 mm wide and 270 mm deep: twenty by six. A bracket is 135 mm: three.

Nothing is cut to length on site, so a longer wall is more modules rather than a new part.

Change the length and the count changes; nothing new is made.
