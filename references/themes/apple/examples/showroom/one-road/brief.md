# Brief — One road

An editorial opener and its first reading column about one stretch of road and
the range of a single charge. The road, the route study and every figure are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Volt`
- `The road a single charge can finish.`
- `一次充电走得完的一段路`
- `A figure says how far. A road says what far is.`
- `数字说多远，路说远是什么`
- `402 km`
- `one charge, measured along the Coast Road`
- `Harbour 0 km`
- `Ridgeway 148 km`
- `North Cape 402 km`
- `Volt route study, 2026`
- `Read the route note`
- `Volt · from ¥80,400`
- `Volt route note · 2026`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. The world's Tokens govern
colour and face: carbon, graphite, pewter and ash with one electric blue for
the single link, all corners square, Inter with a Chinese serif/sans pair. No
Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the range
slide. In three seconds: one road, one sentence. Next: they read the route note
rather than return to the shop.

## The idea

On this surface, *a stretch of road* becomes *the proof of the range*: a figure
tells a buyer how far, but a distance is believed only when it has a place to
pass through, so the story opens on one road long enough to hold the number and
lets the landscape argue that the figure describes a real journey rather than
a laboratory result.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: `The road a single charge can finish.`, register:
   vista turn (a full-bleed drawn road, one centred headline, no competing
   detail, one caption declaring the drawing).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (dark field, flush-left
   leads and body, one checkable figure with a drawn section and its source).
3. Close — one link and one quiet price, register: quiet; the price never
   becomes the headline.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The vista is a drawn road study, declared in the caption; atmosphere is its
  job, never evidence. The distant car is part of the same illustration.
- `402 km` is the world's fictional full-charge figure, checked here against a
  drawn elevation section, not a survey; the section is a readable diagram.
- Waypoint distances are fictional; `Illustrative example` states this.
- Only the vista headline centres; leads, body, labels, captions and the price
  stay flush left. One electric-blue link is the only accent on the page.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting. Every
  text shape carries internal padding so no line touches a frame edge.
