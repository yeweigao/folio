# Brief — Per charge

One launch slide for the range of a single charge of a small electric car. The
car, the price and every figure are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Volt`
- `From ¥80,400`
- `One charge outlasts your week.`
- `一次充电，跑得比一周更远`
- `402 km`
- `on a single charge`
- `单次充电续航`
- `The price, divided by a single charge.`
- `A week of driving`
- `156 km`
- `One full charge`
- `Volt range note · 12 June 2026`
- `2026 年 6 月 12 日`
- `Reserve`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9).
Google Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling
asset. The world's Tokens govern colour and face: carbon, graphite, pewter
and ash with one electric blue for the primary action only, all corners square,
Inter with a Chinese serif/sans pair. No Apple product, photograph, logo or
font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a launch room, six to ten metres from the screen,
speaker talking about range. In one glance: how far one charge goes, and what
the car costs. Next: they reserve, having made the division for themselves.

## The idea

On this surface, *the distance of one charge* becomes *the denominator of the
price*: a small car is judged less by how far it can ever go than by what one
charge returns for what it costs, so a buyer needs the range and the price in
the same glance — a figure that stands alone, with the price hidden or revealed
later, is an unfinished claim.

## Sequence

1. Statement / argument — lead: `One charge outlasts your week.`, register:
   dark turn (a whole carbon field with a complete foreground palette on that
   ground).
2. Data exhibit — the one number, its label and a comparison on a shared zero
   baseline, register: pause; the number group sits mid-frame, spaced down,
   never stranded at the top.
3. Close — the source line and one square blue action, register: quiet; the
   price stays visible throughout.

## Assumptions and gaps

- No KPI trio, no second number group: one decisive figure carries the slide,
  and the comparison bar is its check, not a rival claim.
- The price stays on screen from the first line to the last; the electric blue
  is spent on one square action only, never on a bar, field or headline.
- `156 km` is a fictional week of driving; `402 km` a fictional full charge;
  both are illustrative and the page says so.
- The car silhouette is drawn, declared in the caption, and is an exhibit —
  the proof is the readable number and the labeled comparison, not the object.
- The reserve date and the source line are fictional; `Illustrative example`
  states this on the slide.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting. Every
  text box carries internal padding so no line touches a frame edge.
