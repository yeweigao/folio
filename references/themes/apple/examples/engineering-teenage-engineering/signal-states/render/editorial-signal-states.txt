The panel at one moment. 12 keys, 4 knobs, four states, one colour. Drawn at 3×. Illustrative render, not a photograph.

The panel spends its one colour on state, and on nothing else.

Four states, one hue, and the difference is drawn. The colour sits in the same place on every key — a light at the top-left corner — and the state is how that light is filled.

Filled is live: the key is sounding now. An outline is armed: the key is queued for the next bar. A dashed outline is waiting: it is in the pattern, but the pattern has not reached it. A hairline is off: the key is not in the pattern at all.

Four keys, one grey ink. The states still separate, because they were never separated by colour.

With the colour removed, the four states still read.

The colour marks four states. Everything else on the panel is an edge.
