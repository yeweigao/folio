A preview link is a page that exists for one change.

一条预览链接，只为一次改动存在

A deploy does not end when the build finishes. It ends when someone else can open the change and say what they think of it.

Relay gives every push its own address. The page behind it is real, but it is not public; it exists for the people who were sent the link.

The link is the review. Nothing has to be described in a message, because the thing itself is already there to be opened.
