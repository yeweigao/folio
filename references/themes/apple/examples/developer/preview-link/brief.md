# Brief — Before it goes live

An editorial opener and its first reading column, about the preview link a
deployment leaves behind. The project, the address and the figures are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Relay`
- `Before it goes live, it already has an address.`
- `上线之前，它已经有一个地址`
- `Open the preview`
- `relay / preview / checkout`
- `staged, not live`
- `A page waiting for a decision.`
- `Nothing on it is public yet.`
- `A preview link is a page that exists for one change.`
- `一条预览链接，只为一次改动存在`
- `1 preview URL per change`
- `push`
- `build`

- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
proof-field vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
product page. In three seconds: one staged page with its own address. Next: they
read the field note rather than return to the shop.

## The idea

On this surface, *a preview link* becomes *a place a team can stand before
anything is public*: the story opens on an address that exists for one change
and for no one else, and argues that a deployment is not finished until
someone other than its author can open it.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: a readable preview interface at full bleed, register:
   dark-band turn (one dashboard section on a dark band, one headline and one
   action, a complete foreground palette on that band).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (flush-left lead and body,
   one checkable figure and a readable flow diagram).
3. Close — the field-note line, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The browser frame is an illustrative Relay interface, declared in the
  caption; it is readable evidence, not a photograph or a placeholder bar.
- `1 preview URL per change` is a fictional illustrative figure; the page says
  so with `Illustrative example`.
- No price and no second action appear in the opener.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
