# Brief — One deploy, eight seconds

One number slide for a deployment platform, argued by a single build's log
rather than by a list of features. The build, its timings and the project are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple
product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a small hall, four to eight metres from the screen,
speaker talking. In one glance: one deploy takes eight seconds, and the log
shows where those seconds went. Next: they read the deploy log rather than a
feature list.

## The idea

On this surface, *the time a deploy takes* becomes *the only argument for the
platform*: instead of claiming speed, the slide puts one build's whole timeline
on a single line, so the reader watches the seconds pass and the claim checks
itself against the log.

## Sequence

1. Statement / data — lead: the one decisive figure, register: gradient-word
   turn (one headline verb in gradient at hero scale, nothing else gradient).
2. The deploy log stands beside the figure as the exhibit; the number, its
   label and its context sit mid-frame on a single left axis, with one quiet
   action beneath.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The log is an illustrative Relay interface, declared in the caption; it is a
  readable build log, never a photograph or an abstract placeholder bar.
- The durations and the total are fictional illustrative figures;
  `Illustrative example` states this on the slide.
- No comparison, no second number and no chart legend appear; one build is the
  whole claim.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
