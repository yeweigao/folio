# Brief — Relay for teams

A Bento store page for a team plan, argued as one number of people who may
deploy together. The plan, the price, the activity and the reliability figure
are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Relay`
- `Teams`
- `Ship as one team.`
- `One queue, twelve people, no waiting for a turn to deploy.`
- `12`
- `people who can deploy at once`
- `on the Team plan, with one shared queue.`
- `Team activity`
- `Mira deployed checkout just now`
- `Otis previewed api-edge moments ago`
- `June deployed landing earlier`
- `99.99%`
- `of deploys reached the edge`
- `measured on illustrative builds`
- `Team`
- `¥320 / month`
- `Choose Team`
- `Every deploy gets a preview link.`
- `Shared with the whole team before it goes live.`
- `See how previews work`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px: a Bento grid of
unequal tiles on a hairline gutter. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple product, photograph,
logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A team lead on a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, choosing a plan for a group.
In one glance: the widest tile states how many people may deploy at once, and
the price sits beside it as a consequence. Next: choose the plan, or read how
preview links work.

## The idea

On this surface, *a team plan* becomes *a count of who may ship at once*: what
a team buys is not storage or seats but the right to deploy together, so the
plan is argued as a single number of people and everything else on the page is
a consequence of it.

## Sequence

1. Index / lead — lead: the wide tile carries the one decisive number,
   register: base (light field, one claim per tile, sized by importance).
2. Collection — a readable team-activity interface, one number poster, a plan
   tile and a feature tile, register: base, quieter; each tile holds one claim.
3. Close — the foot line, register: pause.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The team-activity panel is an illustrative Relay interface, declared in the
  caption; it is readable evidence, not an abstract placeholder bar.
- `12`, `¥320 / month` and `99.99%` are fictional illustrative figures;
  `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- No equal-weight card wall and no KPI trio: tiles differ in size, and only one
  tile is a pure number poster.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
