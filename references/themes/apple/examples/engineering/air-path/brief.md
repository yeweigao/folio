# Brief — Aero, one length of air

An editorial opener and its first reading column: the air's route drawn once,
with the pressure it carries plotted along it. The product and every figure are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Aero`
- `The air's route is fixed. Its pressure is lost in one place.`
- `风只走一条路，压力只在一个地方下降`

- `Filter`

- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed proof
field at the top, holding the route and its pressure profile, over a centred
reading column with flush-left text. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader alone at 50–70 cm, arriving from the section slide. In three seconds:
the air has one route, and something happens to its pressure at one station.
Next: they read the three paragraphs that explain the loss.

## The idea

On this surface, *a pressure loss* becomes *a shape you can see on the route*:
the same five stations carry the names above and the pressure curve below, so
the one dip in the line is the whole argument and the accent has only one place
to sit.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — the route and its pressure profile on one axis, one
   headline; register: proof-field vista (a readable figure, not a photograph).
2. Argument — the column: what the path is, where the pressure goes, what the
   outlet figure costs; register: pause.
3. Close — the note line and the declaration; register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The figure is drawn, not measured; it is an illustrative stand-in declared in
  the caption, and its numbers are fictional.
- The five station names are the same five as the section slide, but the device
  here is the curve, not the cutaway — the same subject, a different proof.
- The accent appears once, on the filter, because the filter is the one place
  the claim points at.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
