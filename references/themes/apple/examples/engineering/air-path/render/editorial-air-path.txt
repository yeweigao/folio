A filter is the only part of Aero that removes something.

滤网是 Aero 唯一取走东西的部件

Air enters at the bell mouth and leaves at the grille without turning aside. The path stays fixed through one throat, one rotor, one diffuser and one nozzle; the filter is where pressure first falls.

Across the filter, the pressure drops 38 Pa. The impeller returns 76 Pa at 2,100 rpm, leaving the outlet with 420 L/s. The curve above follows the same five stations from bell mouth to grille, measured from the bell mouth to the grille, one run.
