AeroAirflow note

The air's route is fixed.
Its pressure is lost in one place.

风只走一条路，压力只在一个地方下降

Static pressure along the route, measured from the bell mouth to the grille, one run.
Illustrative render, not a photograph

A filter is the only part of Aero
that removes something.

滤网是 Aero 唯一取走东西的部件

Air enters at the bell mouth and leaves at the grille without being turned aside. Between those two points the path is fixed: one throat, one rotor, one diffuser, one nozzle. Nothing branches, and nothing is stored.

At the filter the static pressure falls by 38 Pa and does not come back on its own. The rotor has to pay for it: 76 Pa across the blade disc, at 2,100 rpm. Everything the fan does downstream is the recovery of that one debt.

The outlet figure is what remains after the debt is settled. 420 L/s leaves the grille because the machine has already spent the pressure it took to clean the air.
