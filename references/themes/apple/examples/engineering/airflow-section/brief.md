# Brief — Aero, the airflow section

One launch slide: the fan cut in half, with each performance figure standing
under the station that measured it. The product and every figure are fictional
and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Aero`
- `Airflow section`
- `Every figure hangs from the station that measured it.`
- `每个数字都挂在测量它的那一段上`
- `Cutaway at the horizontal axis. The wall is shown solid, at its full thickness.`
- `Inlet`
- `96 mm`
- `throat at the bell mouth`
- `Impeller`
- `2,100 rpm`
- `nine blades, one speed`
- `Diffuser`
- `38 Pa`
- `pressure recovered`
- `Outlet`
- `420 L/s`
- `airflow, 1 m from the grille`
- `Fictional test figures at 44 dB(A), Aero engineering note, 2026.`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Google Fonts
are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is loaded from a local sibling asset.
No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A room at four to eight metres, the presenter talking over the slide. In one
glance: this is a fan, cut open, and the numbers belong to named points on it.
Next: they read the section left to right and stop at the outlet figure.

## The idea

On this surface, *the duct's cross-section* becomes *the ruler*: the four
figures are not a table under the picture but the stations where they were
taken, so the reader measures the fan by following the air rather than scanning
a list.

## Sequence

1. Statement — lead: the claim that every figure belongs to a station,
   register: base (one light field, one reading axis at the left margin).
2. Exhibit — the cutaway runs the full width; four station leaders drop to one
   shared baseline, and the outlet figure alone takes the accent.
3. Close — the source line and the declaration, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The section is drawn, not photographed, and is declared in the caption; it is
  an illustrative stand-in, not evidence of a real fan.
- All figures are fictional and come from one stated run; `44 dB(A)` is given in
  the source line rather than on a fifth station, so the slide keeps one axis.
- Only one accent appears — the outlet figure and the air leaving it — because
  the outlet is the claim the section exists to prove.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
