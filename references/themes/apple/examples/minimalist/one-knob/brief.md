# Brief — Mono 2, one dial

One launch slide for a small radio whose whole interface has been reduced to a
single control. The radio, its price and its date are fictional.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Mono 2`
- `One dial, and nothing else to set.`
- `只剩一个旋钮，别的都不用调`
- `It switches on at the rest notch and tunes from there.`
- `Dial diameter`
- `Ø 60 mm`
- `Band`
- `88–108 MHz`
- `Detents`
- `60`
- `60 detents across the band.`
- `Cabinet`
- `240 × 150 × 110 mm`
- `Weight`
- `1.3 kg`
- `¥1,280`
- `Available 12 March 2027`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9).
Google Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset.
No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at a launch, four to eight metres from the screen. In one glance:
this radio has one control, and the control is a real dial with a real scale.
Next: they check the five figures and believe the claim, because the object in
the picture can be measured.

## The idea

On this surface, *a subtraction* becomes *a measurable object*: the slide does
not say "minimal", it shows the single dial at the size the front panel gives
it and puts the diameter, the band, the detent count and the weight beside it,
so the reduction can be checked rather than admired.

## Sequence

1. Cover / exhibit — lead: the one-dial claim, register: base (light field).
2. Evidence — the radio's front at scale, one dial, one scale, one grille,
   register: base.
3. Close — the five figures and the date, register: pause.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The drawing is the front of Mono 2 at 3.17×, so the dial measures 190 px for
  its stated 60 mm; the grille and the scale are drawn at the same scale.
- Scale ticks are major marks, not the 60 detents; the detent count is stated in
  text, not counted from the drawing.
- The radio, its price and its availability are fictional; `Illustrative
  example` states this on the slide.
- No accent colour is used: this world's action is neutral ink, and colour
  would be decoration on a Braun-handed page.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
