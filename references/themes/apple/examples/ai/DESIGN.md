# Fern, a writing assistant · Apple + AI, Claude hand

A visual world for a fictional writing assistant, not a real Claude product: intelligence has no photograph, so warm paper stands in for it, and a product surface checks every claim. Dialect: AI, governing hand Claude. Forms shown: Keynote slide, Store card, Editorial opener, Flyer sheet, Website page; Bento grid as a variation for a many-feature unit.

> Use alone: S = 1000 is the reference short side; multiply reference sizes by actual S / 1000. Web px values below are reference units at S = 1000, not fixed sizes for every format. Derive the grid from W × H. Recompose when scaled type would breach the delivery surface's size floor; never shrink facts to fit. Read Overview and Images before any value. Content lives in the brief, never in this file. The material is a stand-in, not the proof; the product surface is the proof.

## Overview

Apple as base, AI as dialect, Claude as governing hand — Set the writing or editing claim on warm paper and prove it with readable document or conversation content; never let an atmospheric material stand in for product evidence.

World: Fern, an assistant that helps write and edit, its chat and document surfaces, its pricing, its story, across a launch deck, a pricing page and a story opener. Keep Apple's single-accent discipline and one lead per screen; let Claude's philosophy govern the look — warm cream canvas, serif display, warm coral calls to action, dark navy product surfaces, humanist sans for body.

Method: Separate the content into a claim, its visible evidence and a decision or lookup task. Build the proof field before setting the labels; select Keynote, Store or Editorial structure by the reader's task, then recompose the reading axis and crop for the actual format.

## Images

No robot illustration, no glowing orb, no generic blue gradient. The visual proof is a product surface: a chat surface, a document surface, a code or model card, shown at rest with readable content inside it. Product surfaces are fictional interface mockups, never a real product's interface, and are labelled as such. Crop: product-surface crops show the screen interior only; a crop that lets keyboard keys or chrome peek at an edge is rejected and re-cropped to the interior.

## Layout

Grid system: Gallery field — derive the proof field and one reading axis from W × H; Store subdivides that field into equal peers, Editorial joins it to a reading column, and Keynote assigns one claim to each slide.

Warm cream canvas with cream feature cards and dark navy product surfaces as contrast. Hero: headline with a product surface below it as proof. Slide field 16:9, critical content inside the central 80%. Store field: cream cards on a hairline grid, one plan per card, product surface thumbnail on top, name and price below. Editorial field: one centred column, with product surfaces as proof figures inside the story. Every text shape carries internal padding of at least 11.34 reference units at S = 1000 (0.3 cm at 96 dpi); a shape whose text touches its box edge is unfinished. Title boxes are taller than their type and clear descenders: detail images, cards and body text sit below title descenders, never overlapping them. Number groups sit mid-frame: number, label and context spaced down the slide, never stranded at the top. Body, leads, captions, card names and prices are flush left, ragged right; a centred container is never centred text; only hero, vista and cover headlines may centre.

## Typography

A slab-serif display face for headlines, a humanist sans for body. Serif speaks; sans labels and reads. No sans-only display, no generic blue-gradient headline, no mono body copy.

Concrete type for this fictional world: Roboto Slab for display, Source Sans 3 for body and labels; pair Chinese with Noto Serif SC for serif display and Noto Sans SC for sans roles. These are open-source substitutes, not the named brand's proprietary faces. Tokens below are authored example values, not measured brand specifications.

## Colors

Tinted cream canvas, ink text, warm coral for calls to action, dark navy for product surfaces, cream feature cards. The brand voltage is the cream and coral pairing, deliberately warm and humanist where most AI brands use cool blue and slate. No cool blue page, no gradient wash as the page itself.

Concrete field roles: canvas is the page, panel the card, dark a proof-field polarity, ink the main reading colour, muted the secondary reading colour; onDark and onAction are their contrasting text. Hairline is a non-text separator. Action is the single action accent. Text uses only the foreground/ground pairs in Tokens; other product or gradient colours carry no reading text without a new checked pair.

## Shapes

Every nonzero radius uses **continuous corner**; square forms stay square. 圆角用 Apple continuous corner：直边处曲率为 0、到角中最大，肩部在约 1.53R 内完成（路径为拟合，非解析 G2），禁用普通 circular border-radius，禁用超椭圆 squircle；嵌套同心，内半径 = max(0, 外半径 − 间距)。 The contour is geometry, not an API: in units of R, one corner is A (0, 1), C (0, .96) (0, .82) (.07491, .63149), C (.16906, .37282) (.37282, .16906) (.63149, .07491), C (.86841, 0) (1.08849, 0) (1.52866, 0), B (1.52866, 0). Build that path in whatever the delivery format offers — a native continuous corner, an SVG or canvas path, a freeform shape in a slide, or Figma at 60% smoothing — and use the same path for fill, border and media clip, with R clamped to min(R, h/2, w/(2 × 1.52866)). This 1.53R construction is the project's implementation brief, not an Apple-published formula; inspect the delivered edge at size.

Softly rounded cards, cream feature cards, dark navy product cards, no heavy shadow, no decorative border. Calls to action in warm coral, secondary actions as quiet outlines. No pill on every control; shape follows the card system, not a template.

## Forms

- Keynote: one thesis per slide, serif headline, product surface below as proof, live text over a calm zone.
- Store: one plan per card, product surface thumbnail on top, name and price below, one buy action per card.
- Editorial: opener with the assistant's story in serif leads, then long leads with product surfaces as proof inside the column.
- Flyer: one A4 sheet, real internal padding so no text touches a frame edge, one claim, one dominant image, figure or price at display size in the footer rule.
- Website: full-width hero image, hero plus three sections plus footer, hierarchy carried by size and weight, centred container with flush-left body, leads, captions and prices.
- Bento grid: many Fern features on one page, tiles sized by importance, one claim per tile, cream feature cards as tiles, specs as small number posters.

## Variations

One break per unit. A dark-surface turn: one product surface on a dark navy card inside a cream page, with a complete foreground palette on that card. A serif-lead turn: one long serif lead opening a story section, with body returning to sans below it.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do: warm cream canvas, serif display, coral calls to action, product surface as proof, one governing hand per work. Don't: a cool blue page, a sans-only display, a robot illustration, a glowing orb, a gradient wash as the page, a claim without a product surface to check it on, a second accent colour.

## Three rules from the supplementary test

- Accent replacement: the Claude hand's warm coral replaces every Apple blue role for this work — link, filled CTA, focus ring and dark-ground link — and Apple blues retire for this work; coral beside Apple blue would be two accents.
- Vista: the Editorial opener uses a proof-field vista — a product surface at full-bleed with one headline and at most one action, where the surface itself is the atmosphere.
- Bento as index: the Bento variation is an index, not proof — each tile's claim must be provable in a following unit, or the tile itself must carry its checkable number, label or figure.

## Master edition — what changed

Master edition: cover re-cropped to the screen interior only, titles and numbers given taller shapes with 0.3 cm margins, label shortened — fixing a detail title overlapping its navy card and a number label wrapping into its context line. Limitation remaining: serif display is Georgia in the master deck (substitute, never embedded); line breaks may shift on a machine with other fonts, so spacing is buffered. Store on the web is left text with right image, price row plus total; Editorial on the web is vista opener plus flush-left long-read column; Bento on the web is main image first, four-tile index after, numbers at equal size. Fonts in the master deck are named, never embedded; spacing is buffered against LibreOffice substitution shifting line breaks.
