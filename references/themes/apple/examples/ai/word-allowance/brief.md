# Brief — Fern: an allowance of words

One store page for a writing assistant priced by how much it reads with you,
rather than by a list of features. The plans, the counter and the price are
fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Fern`
- `Plans`
- `How much will you write this month?`
- `这个月，你打算写多少？`
- `Fern is priced by the words it reads with you. Choose an allowance; the draft keeps count.`
- `Choose a monthly allowance`
- `2,000 words`
- `10,000 words`
- `Unlimited`
- `1,240`
- `of 10,000 words used this month`
- `Your plan`
- `10,000 words a month`
- `¥96 / month`
- `Every plan includes drafts, rewrites and one clean export.`
- `Buy`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple
product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A writer on a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, deciding how much help to pay
for. In one glance: the price is a quantity of words, and a live counter shows
what that quantity looks like. Next: choose an allowance, then buy without
hunting for the action.

## The idea

On this surface, *a plan* becomes *a monthly allowance the writer watches fill
up*: Fern is priced by the words it reads with you, so the checkable thing is a
counter inside the draft, and choosing a plan is choosing how far that counter
may go before the month ends.

## Sequence

1. Exhibit / cover — lead: a document surface carrying a live word count,
   register: dark-surface turn (one navy product surface on the cream field).
2. Collection — the allowance chips and the price row, register: base, quieter;
   every choice is one quantity, every consequence is one price.
3. Close — the sticky buy bar, register: pause; the price and the action stay
   put.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The counter on the right is an illustrative Fern interface, declared in the
  caption; it is readable product evidence, not a photograph or a placeholder
  bar standing in for an interface.
- The counter, the allowances and the price are fictional illustrative figures;
  `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- No feature comparison table appears: the purchase is one quantity, and a
  table would give the buyer a second decision.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
