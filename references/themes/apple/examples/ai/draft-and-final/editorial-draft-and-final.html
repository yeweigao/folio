Draft

She had meant to leave before the light went, but the room was warm and the rain had not yet started, so she stayed, and later she was glad that she had stayed.

Final

The room was warm, so she stayed.

Fern does not add. It takes away, and the taking is the part worth reading.

删掉，才是值得读的那部分

The first version explains itself. It accounts for the light, the rain and the decision to stay, as though a reader could not be trusted to arrive there alone.

The second version keeps only the room and the staying. Nothing is added to explain why; the sentence is shorter because the writer stopped asking it to carry the whole evening.

What survives is not a summary. It is the same passage with the scaffolding removed, and the difference between the two is the only evidence the note needs.
