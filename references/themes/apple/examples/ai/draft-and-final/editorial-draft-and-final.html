Fern does not add. It takes away, and the taking is the part worth reading.

The first version explains the light, the rain and the decision to stay. The second keeps the warm room and the choice; the edit leaves a reader room to arrive at the rest.

One passage is enough to see the operation. Count what remains, then compare it with what the first draft carried.
