# Brief — What a second draft takes away

An editorial opener and its first reading column, about a single rewrite. The
passage, the counts and the note are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Fern`
- `What a second draft takes away`
- `第二稿拿走了什么`
- `Read the editing note`
- `Draft`
- `Final`
- `33 words became 7`
- `Counted on one illustrative passage; the figures are fictional.`
- `Fern editing note, 2026`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
proof-field vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
product page. In three seconds: one passage, shown twice, and the claim that
something was removed. Next: they read the editing note rather than return to
the shop.

## The idea

On this surface, *the distance between a draft and a final* becomes *a
subtraction the reader can recount*: the story never claims Fern writes well,
it lets one version be taken away from the other, so the argument is a
difference the reader computes rather than a promise about the assistant.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: one passage as draft and as final, register:
   proof-field vista (a readable manuscript comparison at full bleed, one
   headline and one action).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (a serif-lead turn opens the
   story; body returns to sans; one checkable figure with its caption).
3. Close — the editing-note line, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The vista is an illustrative Fern document surface, declared, not a
  photograph; the readable text comparison is the proof.
- `33 words became 7` is a fictional figure for one illustrative passage; the
  caption and `Illustrative example` state this on the page.
- No price and no second action appear in the opener.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
