## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed proof
field over a centred reading column, text flush left inside it. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset and the five
diagram nodes use it. No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A shop owner alone at a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, asking why the payout
is smaller than the day's sales. In three seconds: one amount splitting into two.
Next: they follow the four balances and see where the fee is taken, then check
the subtraction in the column.

## The idea

On this surface, *a payment* becomes *a balance that is copied forward*: the
argument is not that the fee is small but that each step holds a different
number, so the page draws the same money arriving at four places and shows the
one moment where it splits.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: the diagram as the vista, register: base (cream proof
   field, one headline, one diagram, no action).
2. Argument — the reading column, register: pause (white field, flush-left lead
   and body, one table that repeats the subtraction).
3. Close — source and the illustrative declaration, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The diagram is an illustrative Ledger interface, declared in the caption; it
  is readable as a flow — five labelled nodes, four connectors, one branch.
- The arithmetic closes: 1.45% of ¥392.00 plus ¥0.30 is ¥5.98 after rounding,
  and ¥392.00 − ¥5.98 = ¥386.02; the rounding is declared in the source line.
- No rate table and no pricing claim appear; the fee is one line of the story.
- The four timestamps are fictional and consistent across the drawing and the
  column.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
