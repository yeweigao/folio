A sale is not one movement of money. It is four balances in sequence.

一笔钱不是一次移动，而是四个余额依次出现

At 14:02 the card is charged ¥392.00, and in the same second Ledger authorises it. The amount is already two amounts: ¥5.98 is the fee, and ¥386.02 is what the shop has earned.

That balance is not a bank account. It is a number Ledger keeps, and it moves to the bank once a day, on the next working day.

Every figure in the diagram is one sale, not an average, so the parts have to add back to the charge: ¥392.00 minus ¥5.98 is ¥386.02.
