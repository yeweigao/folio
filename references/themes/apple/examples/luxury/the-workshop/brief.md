# Brief — The workshop

An editorial opener and its first reading column about the room where one
fictional leather house works. The house, the history, the figures and the
price are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Atelier`
- `The workshop is the provenance.`
- `工坊，即出处`
- `Read the house note`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`
- `A bag can be photographed anywhere. It is understood where the hands work.`
- `一只包可以被拍在任何地方，却只在双手工作的地方被理解`
- `7 panels`
- `cut from one hide, for one Solstice bag`
- `The Solstice · ¥12,800`
- `Since 1978`
- `自 1978 年起`
- `Atelier house note, 2026`
- `Atelier house note · 2026`
- `Illustrative example`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. The world's Tokens govern
colour and face: white, cream and neutral greys with one orange as the only
accent, Cormorant Garamond for display and a Chinese serif pair, Inter for
labels. Every nonzero radius uses the shared continuous-corner path. No house
mark, monogram, pattern, product photograph or font file is borrowed.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
series page. In three seconds: one room, one sentence. Next: they read the
house note rather than return to the shop.

## The idea

On this surface, *the workshop* becomes *the provenance of the object*: a bag
can be photographed anywhere, but a reader trusts the craft only when they can
picture where the hands work, so the story leads with the room and lets the
object follow from it — the place is the argument, the bag is the evidence.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: `The workshop is the provenance.`, register: vista
   turn (a full-bleed drawn room, one flush-left headline, at most one action,
   one caption declaring the drawing).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (white field, serif lead,
   flush-left body, one checkable figure with a drawn cut plan and its source).
3. Close — one quiet price and the house line, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The vista is a drawn room study, declared in the caption; atmosphere is its
  job, never evidence. The proof is the readable `7 panels` figure and the
  drawn cut plan, not the scene.
- `7 panels` and the cut plan are illustrative; `Since 1978` is a fictional
  founding date. `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- Price is quiet and never the headline; only the link carries the orange.
- Only a vista headline may centre; here it stays flush left, and leads,
  body, labels, captions and the price stay flush left with it.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; the serif display box is tall enough to clear
  descenders under substitution. Every text shape carries internal padding.
