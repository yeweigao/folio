# Brief — The making

One launch slide for the order in which a single leather bag is made, judged at
the scale of a stitch. The house, the bag, the price and the date are fictional
and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Atelier`
- `The order is the value.`
- `顺序，就是价值`
- `Four moves, taken in order, are all that stand between a hide and an edge that will outlast it.`
- `Read the making note`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`
- `Cut 裁`
- `Skive 削`
- `Stitch 缝`
- `Burnish 封`
- `The Solstice · ¥12,800`
- `Atelier making note · Autumn 2026`
- `2026 年秋季`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9). Google
Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. The
world's Tokens govern colour and face: white, cream and neutral greys with one
orange as the only accent, Cormorant Garamond for display and a Chinese serif
pair, Inter for labels. Every nonzero radius uses the shared continuous-corner
path. No house mark, monogram, pattern, product photograph or font file is
borrowed.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a presentation room, six to ten metres from the
screen, being told why one bag costs what it costs. In one glance: that the
value lies in the order of the making, not in a list of features. Next: they
read the making note.

## The idea

On this surface, *the order of a bag's making* becomes *the measure of its
value*: a house can name a price, but it can only justify one by showing what
the hand did first, so the slide asks the viewer to judge the object at the
scale where every step is still a separate decision rather than a finished
surface.

## Sequence

1. Statement / exhibit — lead: `The order is the value.`, register: base
   (a clean white field, one serif headline, generous air).
2. Argument — a craft macro at detail scale and a readable section through one
   edge with four named steps, register: pause; the section is the proof, the
   macro is the subject.
3. Close — one orange action and a quiet price, register: orange turn (one
   accent moment on an otherwise neutral page).

## Assumptions and gaps

- The four steps carry the sequence by name, not by `01/02/03` and not by a
  spec figure: the readable section is the proof, never a spec sheet.
- The macro and the section are drawn stand-ins, declared in the caption; craft
  is shown at detail scale, never claimed from a borrowed photograph.
- Price is quiet and never larger than the bag name; `The Solstice · ¥12,800`
  sits below the action.
- One orange element on the page; no second accent, no orange field or wash.
- The house, bag, price and date are fictional; `Illustrative example` states
  this on the slide.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; the serif display box is tall enough to clear
  descenders under substitution. Every text shape carries internal padding.
