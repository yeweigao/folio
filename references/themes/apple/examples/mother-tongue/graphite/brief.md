# Brief — Graphite, a finish in the store

One buy page for a new finish of an existing small speaker: a hero, a choice of
finishes, an accessories grid and a sticky buy bar. The product and every price
are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1440 × 1000 px. Google Fonts are
allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. Every rounded
shape uses the shared continuous-corner path, with radius from the skeleton's
ladder. No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A buyer on a laptop, 50–70 cm from the screen, deciding which finish to buy.
In one glance: the object, its price, and that it is available. Next: choose a
finish, then buy without hunting for the action.

## The idea

On this surface, *a colour choice* becomes *a change in what the room hears*:
the three finishes are placed as chips with no photographs of their own, and
only one product render is shown, because the decision is about a surface, not
about three different speakers.

## Sequence

1. Cover / exhibit — lead: the Graphite hero, register: base (light field).
2. Collection — the finish chips and the accessories grid, register: base,
   quieter; every card carries one name, one price, one action.
3. Close — sticky buy bar, register: pause; the price and the action stay put.

## Assumptions and gaps

- One product render is reused across the hero and the cards at different
  crops; the reuse is declared, not presented as a second proof.
- All three product drawings are illustrative, declared in the caption; they
  are not evidence of real objects.
- Prices are fictional; `Illustrative example` states this on the page.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
