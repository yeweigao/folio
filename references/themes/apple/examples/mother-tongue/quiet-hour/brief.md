# Brief — Lumen Quiet Hour

One launch slide for an evening listening event, projected in a small hall.
The programme is fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Lumen Quiet Hour`
- `One hour with nothing else to hear.`
- `一小时内，只剩音乐`
- `Saturday 21 November 2026`
- `20:00–21:00`
- `City Library, Reading Room 3`
- `城市图书馆 第三阅览室`
- `Free, 40 seats`
- `免费入场，40 个座位`
- `Reserve a seat`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1600 × 900 px (16:9).
Google Fonts are allowed; the continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset.
No Apple product, photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

An audience at the back of a small hall, four to eight metres from the screen,
speaker talking. In one glance: what the evening is, when, where. Next: they
reserve one of the seats.

## The idea

On this surface, *the library's closing hour* becomes *the product's only
specification*: once the room stops making noise, a speaker has nothing left to
prove but silence around the music, so the slide withholds every figure and lets
one hour of quiet be the claim.

## Sequence

1. Cover / statement — lead: the one-hour claim, register: dark turn (a whole
   near-black field with a complete foreground palette on that ground).
2. The product stands in the calm field as the exhibit; the event details and
   one action queue beneath the title on a single left axis.

## Assumptions and gaps

- No price, no specification and no comparison appear: the claim is the hour,
  and adding a figure would give the audience a second lead.
- The product render is drawn, declared in the caption, not a photograph;
  read as an illustration, never as evidence of a real object.
- The event, the room and the seat count are fictional; `Illustrative example`
  states this on the slide.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
