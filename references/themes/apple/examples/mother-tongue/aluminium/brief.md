# Brief — The aluminium that disappears

An editorial opener and its first reading column, about the finish of a small
speaker. The material study and its figures are fictional and must say so.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `The aluminium that disappears`
- `看不见的铝`
- `One piece of aluminium, anodised to 12 µm, is why the room goes quiet.`
- `12 µm`
- `A surface that stops reflecting the room`
- `让房间不再被反射的表面`
- `Read the material note`
- `Lumen material study, 2026`
- `Illustrative example`
- `Illustrative render, not a photograph`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file, one `.unit` of 1200 × 1600 px: a full-bleed
vista over a centred reading column. Google Fonts are allowed; the
continuous-corner helper is a local sibling asset. No Apple product,
photograph, logo or font file is used.

## Reader and outcome

A reader on a laptop, alone, 50–70 cm from the screen, arriving from the
product page. In three seconds: one subject, one sentence. Next: they read the
material note rather than return to the shop.

## The idea

On this surface, *anodising* becomes *the removal of the room*: the surface is
photographed so close that it stops being an object, and the one figure that
survives the crop — the thickness of the layer nobody sees — is the argument.

## Sequence

1. Opener / vista — lead: the invisible surface, register: base (dark field,
   one headline, one action, no competing detail).
2. Argument — the centred column, register: pause (light field, flush-left
   lead and body, one checkable figure with its caption).
3. Close — the material study line, register: quiet.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The vista is an illustrative material macro, declared, not a photograph;
  atmosphere is its job, never evidence.
- `12 µm` is a fictional figure for an illustrative study; the page says so.
- No price, no comparison and no second action appear in the opener.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline fallbacks change the setting.
