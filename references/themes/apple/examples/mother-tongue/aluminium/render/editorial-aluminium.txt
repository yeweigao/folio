A surface that stops reflecting the room

让房间不再被反射的表面

One piece of aluminium, anodised to 12 µm, is why the room goes quiet.

The grille is cut into the same sheet, so the opening and the surface keep one reflection. Nothing is added to the outside of the object, and nothing on the outside asks to be looked at.

What the eye reads as a colour is a thickness. At this scale the room disappears from the metal, and the speaker stops competing with the things around it.
