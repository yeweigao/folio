# Handbook

Brochures, handbooks and catalogs are read slowly and returned to. Density can be high, but every page needs a way in. The proof judges it, not the screen.

- **A4 starting system:** margins 18 top, 21 bottom, 19 inner, 15 outer (mm); six tracks, 6 mm gutters; body 10.5 / 18.4 pt.
- **A5 starting system:** body 9.5 / 15.2 pt; never three text columns.
- **Other formats:** text width = trim − inner − outer; tracks combine into a readable measure (Chinese 28–40 characters, Latin 60–75).
- **Spreads are real facing pages.** A crossing image keeps one crop, clear of the binding.
- **Few levels.** At most four heading levels, plus body and caption.
- **A paragraph is marked once:** an indent or a space, never both.
- **Pages have types:** chapter opener, text page, plate, reference.
- **Print:** bleed 3 mm, safe area 5 mm; saddle stitch in multiples of four; PDF/X-4 with fonts embedded.

**Done means:** every page of the final PDF is viewed at reading size, spreads included. Body is at least 10.5 pt (A5: 9.5), captions at least 8.5. Unproofed color is reported.
