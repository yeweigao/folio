# canvas

On an infinite canvas the unit is the frame, and position is information: distance groups, alignment relates, direction orders. A board is not pages scattered on a plane.

## Space

- 8 pt spatial grid; frames sized and placed in multiples of 8; inside a frame, 12 columns with 20 gutters; board margin 50.
- Distance is grouping: frames in a group 80–160 apart, groups at least 240 apart. No colored group boxes.
- Every frame shares an alignment line (top, left, or center) with a neighbor. Sequences have a main direction; reference boards have predictable groups and entry points.
- Standard frames, at most three kinds per board: web 1440 × 900 (mobile 390 × 844), slides 1280 × 720 or 1920 × 1080, A4 preview 1240 × 1754 (150 dpi), square 1080 × 1080, overview 2560 × 1440.

## Readable at every zoom

Set body text for reading at 100% and navigation titles for the overview zoom. At 25%, a 32 px title appears only 8 px high; scale titles or provide an overview navigator rather than assuming they remain readable. Reorganize crowded boards without deleting required content.

## One skeleton per board

Linear (one row or column, for flows and storyboards), swim lanes (two to four lanes, for roles or scenarios), or map (one overview frame with three to six details around it). A board title sits top left at 40–64 with one line of explanation and a date.

Each frame has one task: a claim, a comparison, a sequence, or lookup. Reference frames keep stable labels and structure. Inner padding starts at 48–64; paper background throughout.

## Where boards go generic

| The template move | Instead |
|---|---|
| Sticky-note wall in rainbow colors with handwritten fonts | Real frames, one accent, type at a real scale |
| Dozens of unnamed frames with no order | Name and group frames by task; number real sequences, use descriptive labels for lookup |
| A different background or accent per frame | One paper, one accent for the board; status colors only with a legend, at most three |
| Connector spaghetti | Orthogonal lines only, 1 pt relation and 2 pt main flow, never crossing a frame; use alignment and distance first |
| Grey rounded rectangles labeled "Mobile" | Real breakpoint designs, or nothing |

## Done means

- Navigation remains readable at overview zoom and body text at reading zoom; entry points and grouping are clear.
- Layers named frame / block / element, no "Frame 12" or "Rectangle 8"; drafts and guides hidden before export.
