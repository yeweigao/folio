# Canvas

On an infinite canvas the unit is the frame, and position is information: distance groups, alignment relates, direction orders.

## Starting system

- 8 px spacing grid; frame positions and padding use it. Preserve exact device, slide, and trim sizes; internal tracks fit their content. Board margin 48 px.
- Frames within a group 80–160 px apart; groups ≥ 240 px apart.
- At most three frame kinds per board: web 1440 × 900 px (mobile 390 × 844 px), slides 1280 × 720 px or 1920 × 1080 px, A4 preview 1240 × 1754 px, square 1080 × 1080 px, overview 2560 × 1440 px.
- Board title at 40–64 px with one line and a date; frame padding 48–64 px; board navigation keeps one treatment.

## Principles

- **Grouping is visible, not inferred.** Space or labeled regions distinguish groups; the theme sets their appearance.
- **Every frame aligns with a neighbor, not with nothing.** Top, left, or center.
- **One skeleton per board, not a scatter.** Linear for flows, swim lanes (two to four) for roles, or a map: one overview with three to six details.
- **One task per frame.** A claim, a comparison, a sequence, or lookup.
- **Each zoom has its reader.** Body for 100%, navigation for the overview; scale titles or add a navigator rather than hope.
- **Connectors are a last resort, not wiring.** Orthogonal, 1 px for relations and 2 px for the main flow, never across a frame.

## Where boards go generic

| The template move | Instead |
|---|---|
| Color-coded notes without a key | Explicit groups, readable labels, and a redundant code |
| Dozens of unnamed frames | Named frames grouped by task |
| A different style per frame | Shared navigation; local appearance follows each frame's task |
| Grey boxes labeled "Mobile" | Real designs, or nothing |

## Done means

- Navigation readable at overview, body at reading zoom; layers named frame / block / element; drafts and guides hidden before export.
