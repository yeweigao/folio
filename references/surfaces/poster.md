# Poster

One unit seen in passing. It works in three seconds from three metres, or it is walked past.

| Size | Trim (mm) | Distance | Title cap-height floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | 841 × 1189 | 10–30 m | 50–150 mm |
| A1 | 594 × 841 | 5–10 m | 25–50 mm |
| A2 | 420 × 594 | 3–10 m | 15–50 mm |
| A3 | 297 × 420 | 1–3 m | 5–15 mm |
| A4 | 210 × 297 | 0.5–1 m | 2.5–5 mm |

- **Cap height in mm ≥ distance in m × 5.** This is a floor, not a target.
- **Screen masters are in CSS px at 96 dpi:** 1 pt = 4/3 px, 1 mm ≈ 3.78 px. Changing the unit never re-decides a size.
- **Three seconds carry the subject, not the inventory.** For an event: what, when, where, at text size.
- **Four levels at most, with real jumps.**
- **A fixed title is recomposed, not compressed.**
- **Bleed 3 mm and safe area 5 mm**, as the printer specifies.

**Done means:** the subject and the essentials are recognized at the intended distance; PDF/X-4 with fonts embedded; one proof before the run.
