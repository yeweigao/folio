# Poster

A single unit seen in passing: it works in three seconds from three meters, or it is walked past.

## Starting system

| Size | Trim (mm) | Distance | Distance-derived title cap-height floor (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | 841 × 1189 | 10–30 m | 50–150 mm |
| A1 | 594 × 841 | 5–10 m | 25–50 mm |
| A2 | 420 × 594 | 3–10 m | 15–50 mm |
| A3 | 297 × 420 | 1–3 m | 5–15 mm |
| A4 | 210 × 297 | 0.5–1 m | 3–5 mm |

- Use distance (m) × 5 as a minimum title cap height in mm; this is a legibility floor, not a display-size target. Start with the format-specific ranges below and keep cap height above the floor; font size (pt) ≈ cap height (mm) ÷ 0.7 × 72 ÷ 25.4. Verify at the real distance.
- Grids: A2 margins 28, 12 × 23 mm, gutters 8 (content 364); A3 margins 20, 12 × 15.92, gutters 6; A4 margins 15, 6 × 25, gutters 6. Module 8 mm; bleed 3, safe 5, as the printer specifies. Landscape swaps trim dimensions and recomputes the grid; a bound spread is two pages, governed by the handbook and binding.
- A2 at 3 m: title 120–300 pt, text 36–48 pt, credits 14–18 pt. A3 at 1–3 m: title 72–160 pt, text 18–24 pt, credits 10–12 pt. Title ranges are starting points; a graphic display may exceed them if essentials remain legible.

## Principles

- **Three seconds carry the subject, not the inventory.** For an event: what, when, where, at text size, never caption size.
- **One lead, not a collage.** One image or typographic composition; supporting facts grouped tightly; space placed around the focus.
- **Four levels at most, with real jumps.**
- **A fixed title is recomposed, not compressed.** Facts and fixed wording stay exact; length changes the composition, never the tracking or the words.

## Where posters go generic

| The template move | Instead |
|---|---|
| Everything given equal emphasis | A clear lead with supporting facts on shared axes |
| A border unrelated to content | A frame that groups, directs, or defines the crop |
| An unrelated stock photograph | An image that identifies or interprets this subject |

## Print

- The printer's profile, ink limit, and rich-black recipe; small text and hairlines in 100K; spot colors when specified, otherwise proofed.
- Images 300 dpi at trim (≥ 150 for large formats); PDF/X-4, fonts embedded, marks outside the bleed; one proof before the run.

## Done means

- Subject and essentials recognized at the intended distance; facts unchanged, edits disclosed; bleed, color space, and resolution right for the printer, anything unproofed reported.
