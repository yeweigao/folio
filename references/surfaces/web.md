# Web

A page is read, scrolled, clicked, used by keyboard, and loaded on a slow phone. It is not a tall poster.

- **Content width = min(1440, viewport − 2 × max(24 px, 5vw)).** Columns collapse where the content breaks; the page reflows down to 320 px.
- **Text 16–20 px, leading 1.5–1.7, measure up to 65 characters** (CJK 25–35).
- **The first screen shows the thing, not a hero formula.** What it is, one sentence, one action, and the real product or number.
- **Sections follow tasks, not threes.**
- **Proof is real or absent.** Only real quotes and permitted logos.
- **Content is visible by default, not revealed on scroll.**
- **Interface words name the action.** Buttons are a verb plus an object; errors say what happened and how to fix it.
- **Motion has a job** (state change, feedback, continuity, focus): 100–300 ms, transform and opacity only, at most two things at once. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep state changes and drop the rest.
- **Access is the floor.** Visible focus, semantic headings, alt text, reserved image space.

**Done means:** opened at 1440, 768 and 320 px in a real browser, at enlarged text, and by keyboard.
