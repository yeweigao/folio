# Slides

A deck is presented at a distance or read alone; choose one mode per deck. Argument slides make one point; reference slides make lookup easy. Preserve an existing deck or brand while meeting the mode's floor.

## System

- 1280 × 720; margins 70; 12 columns × 73 with 24 gutters (content 1140); column n starts at 70 + 97n; module 8 px.
- One master, vertical zones in px: header rule 26–32, kicker 50–70, reading-slide title 78–141, content 157–653, footer 688–708. A graphic display on a cover or opener may use the content zone and exceed the display range; reading text keeps its floor.

| Role | Desk (read alone) | Projected (≥ 3 m) |
|---|---|---|
| Display: cover, chapter, key figure | 60–140 | 96–160 |
| Slide title | 44–60 | 48–64 |
| Lead | 22–28 | 32–36 |
| Body | 18–22 | 26–32 |
| Compact body | 16–18 | 22–26 |
| Labels, chart titles | 13–16 | 18–22 |
| Ticks, sources, page numbers | 12–13 | 16–18 |

## Principles

- **Distance sets size, and size sets the word count, not the reverse.** The last row reads the body; reflow before cutting copy or adding slides.
- **The exhibit changes; the axis does not.** Every slide hangs from the same master lines.
- **The content owns the area, not the furniture.** A data slide gives the chart most of the field; a cover carries only its essentials.
- **Modules are sized by priority, not by count.** Parallel parts share gutters; equal peers may take equal spans.
- **A teaching deck separates doing from answering.** A stage label says what to do now; answers sit on their own slide; focus words take weight or position, not a new color.

## Where decks go generic

| The template move | Instead |
|---|---|
| Title and six bullets on every slide | The form each slide's job needs ([forms](../forms.md)) |
| Agenda as icon cards | Chapters on one axis, or an opener per section |
| Topic titles on argument slides | Judgment titles where copy may change |
| The same volume throughout | A change of field or scale at real turns only |
| A stock photo per slide | Images only where they prove or set a declared mood |
| Fly-ins and cube transitions | Cuts; a build only to order attention |
| A "Thank you" close | The one claim the audience should keep |

## Done means

- Every slide viewed at full size in the actual render: title breaks, CJK glyphs, real weights.
- Text live; charts native or vector; nothing below 16 px when projected.
- Argument titles read in order are the executive summary.
