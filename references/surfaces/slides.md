# slides

A deck is read in a room, at a distance, one page at a time, while someone talks. Each slide makes one point the back row can read. These numbers are a starting system for 16:9; an existing deck or brand wins.

## Canvas and grid

- 1280 × 720; margins 70; 12 columns × 73 with 24 gutters (content 1140). Column n starts at 70 + 97n. Baseline 8 px.
- One master for the whole deck: header rule 26–32, kicker 50–70, title 78–141, content 157–653, footer 688–708. The exhibit changes; the axis does not.

## Size for the distance

Choose one viewing mode per deck. The test is whether the last row can read the body text.

| Role | Desk (read alone) | Projected (≥ 3 m) |
|---|---|---|
| Display: cover, chapter, key figure | 60–140 | 96–160 |
| Slide title | 44–60 | 48–64 |
| Lead | 22–28 | 32–36 |
| Body | 18–22 | 26–32 |
| Compact body | 16–18 | 22–26 |
| Labels, chart titles | 13–16 | 18–22 |
| Axis ticks, sources, page numbers | 12–13 | 16–18 |

A larger size means fewer words. Budgets: an argument slide 300–450 characters of Chinese or about 120 English words; a data slide ≤ 120 characters plus a chart filling half the content area; a cover ≤ 40 characters.

## Where decks go generic

| The template move | Instead |
|---|---|
| Title + six bullets on every slide | One idea per slide, in the form its job needs: statement, exhibit, argument, sequence, comparison, drill |
| Agenda as four equal cards with icons | One line per chapter on a single axis, or a chapter opener per section |
| Topic titles ("Market overview") | A judgment title; titles read in order make the summary |
| Every slide at the same volume | Every 5–7 slides, a turn in field or scale; no grammar more than twice running |
| Stock photo on every slide | Images only where they prove or set mood; most argument slides need none |
| Fly-in builds and cube transitions | Cuts; a build only to control the order of attention |
| A closing slide that says "Thank you" | Close by returning to the one claim the audience should keep |

Modular slides (several blocks on one page) appear at most once or twice per deck, with 4–7 unequal blocks, 24 px gutters, and no rounded or shadowed boxes.

## Classroom decks

When the deck teaches: give each activity a stage label that tells students what to do now; keep questions and answers on separate slides so answers are not seen early; mark focus words with weight or position rather than new colors; size for the last row.

## Done means

- Rendered and viewed at full size, every slide; title line breaks checked in the actual render.
- CJK fonts lead the font stack, so bold does not silently fall back.
- Text is live and editable; charts are native or vector with separate text.
- In the projected mode, nothing is smaller than 16 px.
