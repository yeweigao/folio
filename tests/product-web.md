The pricing section of the website for Ledgerline, invoicing software
for small accounting firms. Visitors are partners at those firms,
comparing tools on a laptop.

## Material

Three plans. One customer has agreed to be quoted; there are no other
customer names or logos to use.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Ledgerline`
- `Solo`
- `€19`
- `1 user, 50 invoices a month`
- `Team`
- `€49`
- `5 users, unlimited invoices`
- `Firm`
- `€129`
- `20 users, API access, audit log`
- `Kessler & Partner cut their month-end close from six days to two.`
- `Anna Kessler, partner, Kessler & Partner Steuerberatung`
- `Start a 30-day trial`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The section is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1440 px wide, height as the content needs. Web fonts
from Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.
