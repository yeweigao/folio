# Brief — board deck

Lindqvist Freight is a mid-sized shipping company. Its board meets on
26 November 2026 for the quarterly review. The deck has five slides.
The board reads it on a projector in a meeting room and has thirty
minutes. It needs to approve one plan change.

## Material

Revenue and operating cost, € million, by quarter:

| Quarter | Revenue | Operating cost |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 2024 | 41.2 | 37.9 |
| Q1 2025 | 43.0 | 39.4 |
| Q2 2025 | 44.1 | 40.9 |
| Q3 2025 | 47.9 | 43.0 |
| Q4 2025 | 46.3 | 43.8 |
| Q1 2026 | 48.8 | 44.6 |
| Q2 2026 | 50.2 | 45.1 |
| Q3 2026 | 53.6 | 46.7 |

The plan change: from Q1 2027 the Baltic route closes, and its two
vessels move to the North Sea route. The Baltic route lost €3.1 million
over the last four quarters. The decision asked of the board: approve
closing the Baltic route.
