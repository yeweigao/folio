# Brief — event poster

A poster for one concert evening. It hangs in tram shelters and
concert-hall foyers in Zürich, read from about three metres while
walking past.

## Material

Ensemble Lumen plays three works about clocks and time. The evening is
called Three Clocks.

## Facts

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

- `Ensemble Lumen`
- `Three Clocks`
- `Friday 4 December 2026`
- `20:00`
- `Tonhalle Zürich, Kleiner Saal`
- `György Ligeti — Poème symphonique for 100 metronomes`
- `Steve Reich — Clapping Music`
- `Joseph Haydn — Symphony No. 101 "The Clock"`
- `CHF 25 / 15 reduced`
- `ensemble-lumen.ch`

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.
