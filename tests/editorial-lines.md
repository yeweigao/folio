## Reader and outcome

A Chinese-speaking design client reads two 16:9 pages on a laptop. The first page must make chapter lookup immediate. The second must distinguish project facts from the design task. Use a white-dominant, restrained sans-serif visual language. Prioritize reading relationships and preserve open space. Do not invent facts, images, product claims or extra sections. The content is a concept sample.

## Evaluation

Inspect at full size and as thumbnails. Facts exact; small reading text legible; one lead; groups and lookup order clear; whitespace intentional; visible divisions justified by their content scope. Score authorship and relationship clarity separately. Do not reward line count by itself.
