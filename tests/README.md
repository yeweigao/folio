# Regression briefs

Three fixed briefs. After any change to SKILL.md, the theme, or the
templates, run all three with the old and new version, put the two
contact sheets side by side with names covered, and judge the finished
units — never the DESIGN.md files. A change ships only if the new
contact sheets win or tie on every brief and win on at least one.

| Brief | Material | Must appear | Must not appear |
|---|---|---|---|
| `board-deck.md` | 8 quarters of revenue/cost figures, one plan change | A governing hand; a signature programme; ≥2 turns; one full-bleed data exhibit; a ledger, not KPI tiles | Pie/donut, dual axis, gradient, centered hero |
| `event-poster.md` | A one-evening concert: name, date, place, three works | One lead readable at 3 m; a dialect chosen from the material and named; exactly one variation | Stock-photo mood image, inventory of details at caption size, border as decoration |
| `product-web.md` | A SaaS pricing + proof section | Real proof or none; a focus token used; content visible without scroll-reveal; one action | Three equal cards by default, fake logos, "Learn more" buttons |

Record the run in each brief's file: date, skill version, contact-sheet
verdict, the top failure and which philosophy line it broke.
