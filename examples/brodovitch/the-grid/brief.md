# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by in windows and streets, reading English or Simplified Chinese from two to five metres. In three seconds: the name (THE GRID, 格网), that it is an evening, that it is free. Close up, the sheet is the programme. They come without booking; those who stay find the foyer exhibition from 19:00.

## The idea

On this surface, *the grid* becomes *the editor's crop*: the plate edge sits on a module line and cuts the word GRID past its middle, so the ruler that frames the picture turns the word from paper-on-black to ink-on-white and its letters read as a chessboard.

Refused: a ruled grid of lines (the literal pictogram), a centred serif over a figure (the caricature).

## Assumptions and gaps
