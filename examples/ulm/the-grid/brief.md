# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by and museum-window visitors, mixed Chinese and English readers, at two to five metres. In three seconds: what (THE GRID, talks and a poster show), when (Saturday 14 November, 19:30), where (Hall 2). Then: how the evening runs, entry is free, no booking.

## The idea

On this poster, *the grid* becomes *one walking visitor drawn on the poster's own module in three stages of construction, so the three talks read as the rules that build a figure: the module, the system, the interval.*

- Cell above The Module, joined bands above The System, spaced figure above The Interval. Aicher's method, not a Munich copy.
- Colour is a code: blue square for Hall 2, orange circle for the Foyer.

## Assumptions and gaps

- A1 (594 x 841 mm) at 1000 x 1414 px is assumed; no printed proof.
- No Chinese was supplied for talk titles, speakers or the foyer line; none was invented.
- No room number is given for the Foyer.
