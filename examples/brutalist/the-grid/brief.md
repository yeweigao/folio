# Brief — The Grid

A poster for a public evening at the Museum of Design: three talks and a
poster exhibition about the system behind modern design. It hangs in the
museum's windows and around the city, read from two to five metres. The
programme is fictional and must say so.

Every string below must appear in the deliverable exactly as written.

## Deliverable

One self-contained HTML file. The poster is one element with class
`unit`, exactly 1000 × 1414 px (A-series proportion). Web fonts from
Google Fonts are allowed; no other external assets.

## Reader and outcome

Passers-by outside the museum's windows and around the city, reading
at two to five metres in English and Simplified Chinese at equal status. In
three seconds: what (THE GRID, 格网), when (date, hours), where (Hall 2). Up
close: the order of the evening. Outcome: they come on Saturday 14 November,
know entry is free, and know the programme is fictional.

## The idea

On this surface, *the evening's running order* becomes *the poster's own row
grid*: one row is a quarter hour, the field runs 19:00 to 22:00, and each talk
is a black module cell on the row of its start.
The starts 19:30, 20:15 and 21:00 fall on rows 2, 5 and 8 of 12, a constant
step of three rows.

## Sequence

One unit: Reference (the running order) with a subordinate Cover (the title);
lead is GRID at architectural scale. The dark footer closes the field
at 22:00.

## Assumptions and gaps

- Talk durations are not given: each block runs to the next start, the last to
  22:00; no duration is printed.
- "Foyer, from 19:00" has no stated end; the foyer track is open at the bottom.
- The key "1 row = 15 min" is the one number not in the brief: the scale of the
  construction, not an event fact.
- No logo or museum typeface supplied; none invented. Screen master:
  print needs a recomposed frame with trim and bleed.
