# The one source — identical for all nine dialects

A fictional public evening about the grid itself: no product, no brand,
no sales. Every fact below appears in every dialect's poster, word for
word where the dialect allows; only the design changes. That is the
discipline borrowed from the multi-system collections (see README):
hold the content still and the dialect has nowhere to hide.

- Title: THE GRID · 格网
- Subtitle: Three talks and a poster exhibition on the system behind
  modern design. · 三场讲座，一场海报展——现代设计背后的系统
- When: Saturday 14 November 2026, 19:30–22:00 (doors 19:00)
- Where: Hall 2, Museum of Design · 设计博物馆 二号厅
- 19:30 — The Module · Josef Müller-Brockmann: the grid for print
- 20:15 — The System · Otl Aicher: rules for every case
- 21:00 — The Interval · Kenya Hara: emptiness as a vessel
- Foyer, from 19:00 — Poster exhibition: nine dialects, one mother tongue
- Admission: free, seats unreserved · 免费入场，不设座位预订

All names, the venue, and the times are illustrative placeholders for a
fictional programme; the masters named are the talks' subjects. Every
poster carries the line "Illustrative example" in its apparatus.
