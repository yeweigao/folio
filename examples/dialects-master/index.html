Nine dialects, one evening

The same fictional programme — content.md, word for word — set nine times, once per Swiss dialect, each at full strength and each with its own locked DESIGN.md. Judge them as a contact sheet first (SKILL.md, Inspect): with the labels covered, each poster should still be recognisable as its dialect, and no two should be confusable. Open any frame's folder for the full-size poster, its DESIGN.md, and its token preview.
