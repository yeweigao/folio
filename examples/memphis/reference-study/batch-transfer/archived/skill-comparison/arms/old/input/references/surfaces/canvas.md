# Canvas

On an infinite canvas the unit is the frame, and position is information: distance groups, alignment relates, direction orders.

- **An 8 px spacing grid.** Frames in a group sit 80–160 px apart; groups 240 px or more.
- **At most three frame kinds per board,** at their real sizes: web 1440 × 900, slides 1920 × 1080, A4 1240 × 1754, square 1080 × 1080.
- **One skeleton per board.** Linear for flows, swim lanes for roles, or a map: one overview with three to six details.
- **Every frame aligns with a neighbor.**
- **Each zoom has its reader.** Body text reads at 100%, navigation at the overview.
- **Connectors are a last resort.** Orthogonal, never crossing a frame.

**Done means:** navigation is readable at the overview and body text at reading zoom; frames are named; drafts and guides are hidden before export.
