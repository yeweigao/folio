# Slides

A deck is either presented at a distance or read alone. Choose one mode per deck.

| Role (px at 1280 × 720) | Read alone | Projected, 3 m or more |
|---|---|---|
| Display | 60–140 | 96–160 |
| Slide title | 44–60 | 48–64 |
| Body | 18–22 | 26–32 |
| Labels, chart titles | 13–16 | 18–22 |
| Sources, page numbers | 12–13 | 16–18 |

At 1920 × 1080, multiply by 1.5. PPTX takes pt = px × 0.75.

- **Distance sets size, and size sets the word count.** Reflow before cutting copy or adding slides.
- **The exhibit changes; the axis does not.** Every slide hangs from the same master lines.
- **The content owns the area, not the furniture.**
- **A turn is a change of field or scale, and only at a real turn.**
- **Cuts, not transitions.** A build only orders attention.
- **The close is the one claim to keep,** not "Thank you".

**Done means:** every slide is viewed at full size in the actual render. A PPTX sets its East Asian face as well as its Latin one. Nothing projected is below 16 px.
