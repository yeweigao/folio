# Input gaps and production decisions

- The design describes a system without showing a reference composition. I therefore chose new curved contours, stepped partition positions, and all line lengths from the supplied relationships alone.
- The brief does not name a main room. I retained its artist group and the exact supplied “B2B until dawn” descriptor without adding a room name.
- The brief gives no organizer, admission, address, or further event facts. None were invented.
- Exact title typography is unspecified. Inter 700 (the supplied Study Sans) is used as editable live text. The title changes size across its two semantic lines to preserve a dominant readable event name.
- The two artist groups use different sizes (60 and 52 px) within the design’s approximate short-side relationship, while preserving identical weight, alignment, and generous line spacing.
- The secondary right axis is locally overlaid by the white curved field, implementing the stated permission for a black/white negative form to span two zones.
- Paper on black is used for the title, based on the design’s explicit black/white reversal; facts and artist groups use black on paper.
- The supplied date format is retained verbatim; no weekday or year interpretation is added.
- No graphical reference, previous artifact, skill, or outside aesthetic judgment was consulted.
