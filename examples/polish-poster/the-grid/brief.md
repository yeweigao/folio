## Reader and outcome

Mixed Chinese and English passers-by, design-literate or curious, read it in passing from two to five metres. In three seconds: what (The Grid), when (Saturday 14 November, 19:30), where (Hall 2, Museum of Design). Then come: entry is free, no booking.

## The idea

On this poster, *the grid* becomes *a circus safety net, hand-ruled in squared-paper blue, with one yellow figure leaping over it*: the system is what lets the design jump.

Tomaszewski's *Cyrk* territory; the net keeps the squared mesh and gives it a joke. The three talks read as the mesh, the whole net, the gap.
