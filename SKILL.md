# Folio

Design is deciding what is seen first, what recedes, and what never appears.

## Goal

- **Work a master would sign.** Made in the hand of a named school, for this subject and no other.
- **A skeleton per world.** The DESIGN.md holds the system, not the content; another piece of the same world, made from it alone, reads as one set.
- **Names carry methods.** A master is summoned by name and practised by his way of working; a name that brings only his look is a costume.

## Philosophy

The author's eye. It outranks every other file, and every judgment of the work names the line it rests on.

- **A unit has a lead, not a crowd.** One element is read first; everything else queues behind it.
- **The eye's path is designed, not discovered.** Reading follows declared axes; lookup gets clear entry points and a predictable order.
- **The decisive move comes from the subject, not from a style.** Every speaking unit has one leading idea you can say in a sentence; reference units stay quiet.
- **Layout serves the content, not the other way round.** Recompose first, then edit or paginate within scope; never hide content or shrink type to make it fit.
- **Measure is shared, not improvised.** Columns, spacing, baselines, and proportions belong to one system, fitted to the surface.
- **Space is placed, not left over.** Judge it by its job (group, separate, focus), not its amount; fix accidental gaps with scale or placement, never filler.
- **Hierarchy is steps, not slopes.** Few faces, few levels, wide gaps, one treatment per role.
- **Color is a role, not a decoration.** Hue carries meaning; every foreground stays distinct from the ground it actually touches.
- **A deliverable has rhythm, not monotony.** Dense, quiet, and loud units follow the content; repeat to orient, break to signal a change.
- **Information is encoded, not decorated.** Data takes a readable form; decoration never poses as evidence; meaning never depends on color alone.
- **An image is information, not filler.** Evidence, explanation, setting, or atmosphere; illustration never poses as documentary proof.
- **Style is a constraint system, not an element set.** Strip away colors and fonts, and the piece is still recognizable.
- **The frame is composed, not inherited.** Format, crop, and spread boundaries shape the composition; use the edge deliberately.

## Floors

Hard limits under every school. A failed floor blocks delivery.

- **Facts are exact.** Verbatim; nothing invented, renamed or dropped. A missing fact is asked for or reported, never printed as a placeholder.
- **Nothing invented poses as real.** Illustration is declared; a drawn object never stands in for real material.
- **Text separates from its ground.** 4.5:1; large text and essential cues 3:1; the surface's size floors at viewing distance.
- **Reading text is live.**
- **Static work stays static.**
- **Print frames are separate.** Trim, bleed, safe area and binding; a new format is recomposed, not scaled.
- **Licenses travel with assets.**
- **The export is inspected, not the draft.**

## Process

Goals, not steps; the route is yours.

- **Understand before drawing.** Facts verbatim into the brief; the reader, the distance, the language, and what should happen after they look. Ask once, only what changes the work.
- **Go wide, then cut.** Many directions, each one hand's method applied to this subject in a sentence. Cut the first idea, the default, the costume; show the best two or three.
- **Work as the hand worked.** Name what the master used (tools, materials, procedure) and use the nearest real ones.
- **Lock the skeleton.** Locked when a fresh session could make another piece of the world from it alone.
- **Judge the render, not the code.** At viewing size, then as thumbnails.
- **Be judged from outside.** Critics who did not make it; a judge holding the philosophy. Never grade your own work.
- **Machines hold the floors.**

**Critique** drafts the existing work's skeleton from evidence and reviews it; it makes nothing. **Refinement** changes the skeleton before the units. **Delivery** is the work, its skeleton, its brief, and what remains unverified; print goes as PDF with fonts embedded.

## Library

The school sets the grammar, the hand the way of working, the subject the idea.

**Swiss**, the International Typographic Style, is the mother tongue ([theme](references/themes/swiss/theme.md)): its hands, grid systems, methods, variations, anti-patterns. Alone, it suits material that argues, reports, testifies, counts or lists. A **dialect** is a named movement that overturns a few Swiss rules across the whole work:

| Dialect | The material | Departs from Swiss |
|---|---|---|
| [De Stijl](references/themes/swiss/dialects/de-stijl.md) | Relates: parts whose proportions and balance are the subject | Black bars and primary planes in equilibrium |
| [Bauhaus](references/themes/swiss/dialects/bauhaus.md) | Celebrates or explains, where geometry can build the subject | Primaries and primitives on one module, assembled into figures |
| [Brodovitch](references/themes/swiss/dialects/brodovitch.md) | Seduces, where the image is the subject | Didot type colliding with a daring crop; spreads paced like film |
| [Polish Poster School](references/themes/swiss/dialects/polish-poster.md) | Interprets a cultural subject | One authored, hand-made metaphor |
| [Ulm](references/themes/swiss/dialects/ulm.md) | Coordinates a system of related messages | Rules for every case; color and pictograms as codes |
| [Swiss Punk](references/themes/swiss/dialects/swiss-punk.md) | Provokes, still readable | The grid tested by rotation, layering and stepped type |
| [Memphis](references/themes/swiss/dialects/memphis.md) | Delights: products, events, youth culture | Clashing colors and pattern as material |
| [Kenya Hara, ma 間](references/themes/swiss/dialects/ma.md) | Invites: one object, craft or ceremony | Emptiness as the vessel; small, quiet type |
| [Swiss Brutal](references/themes/swiss/dialects/brutalist.md) | Specifies: structure is the content | Visible grid rules, zero radius, dense rows |
