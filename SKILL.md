# Folio

Design is deciding what is seen first, what recedes, and what never appears.

## Goal

- **Work a master would sign.** Made in the hand of a named school, for this subject and no other.
- **A skeleton per world.** The DESIGN.md holds the system, not the content; another piece of the same world, made from it alone, reads as one set.
- **Names carry methods.** A master is summoned by name and practised by his way of working; a name that brings only his look is a costume.

## Philosophy

The author's eye. It outranks every other file, and every judgment of the work names the line it rests on.

- **A unit has a lead, not a crowd.** One element is read first; everything else queues behind it.
- **The eye's path is designed, not discovered.** Reading follows declared axes; lookup gets clear entry points and a predictable order.
- **The decisive move comes from the subject, not from a style.** Every speaking unit has one leading idea you can say in a sentence; reference units stay quiet.
- **Layout serves the content, not the other way round.** Recompose first, then edit or paginate within scope; never hide content or shrink type to make it fit.
- **Measure is shared, not improvised.** Columns, spacing, baselines, and proportions belong to one system, fitted to the surface.
- **Space is placed, not left over.** Judge it by its job (group, separate, focus), not its amount; fix accidental gaps with scale or placement, never filler.
- **Hierarchy is steps, not slopes.** Few faces, few levels, wide gaps, one treatment per role. Differences used to establish rank must be clear at the intended viewing size; merge levels that still look alike.
- **Color is a role, not a decoration.** Hue carries meaning; every foreground stays distinct from the ground it actually touches.
- **A deliverable has rhythm, within and between units.** Recurring groups share a recognizable arrangement; changes in size, spacing or density mark importance, a change of subject or a pause. Dense, quiet and loud units follow the content. Genuine peers may stay regular.
- **Information is encoded, not decorated.** Data takes a readable form; decoration never poses as evidence; meaning never depends on color alone.
- **An image is information, not filler.** Evidence, explanation, setting, or atmosphere; illustration never poses as documentary proof.
- **Style is a constraint system, not an element set.** Strip away colors and fonts, and the piece is still recognizable.
- **The frame is composed, not inherited.** Format, crop, and spread boundaries shape the composition; use the edge deliberately.

## Routes

Goals, not steps: the route is yours, the map is here. Your memory of a name brings its look; the file brings its method. Open each file when its moment comes, and never skip it then.

**For a new direction, go wide, then show before you sign.** Each direction is one hand's method on this subject. Cut the first idea, the default and the costume; show the user two or three roughs to choose the feel before one is built out. A look is passed as a rough or a reference render, never described in prose. With no one to ask, make the roughs anyway, build the strongest and say why. A supplied reference selected for reconstruction already establishes the direction; preserve it and inspect the reconstruction against it.

## Themes

The Philosophy is the eye, the theme the grammar, a dialect the rules it overturns, the hand the way of working, the subject the idea. Nothing overrides the eye: a dialect changes how a lead, an axis or a space is made, never that the unit has one. A theme is a folder under `references/themes/` holding its hands, grammar, methods, dialects and caricature.

Choose the theme from the user's named direction or selected reference; with none, from the subject and reader. A device, a file format or a namesake subject is not a direction. A selected reference for reconstruction governs the direction.

- [Swiss](references/themes/swiss/theme.md): the International Typographic Style and nine movements; for typographic, editorial and information-led work.
- [Apple](references/themes/apple/theme.md): Apple's public marketing and launch language, with industry dialects, surfaces and Bento; for product, feature and launch work led by the object or its evidence.
