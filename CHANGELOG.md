# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Three layers, one voice.** SKILL.md states the order: the Philosophy is the eye and no dialect bends it; the theme is the grammar; a dialect overturns only the rules it names. design-md.md says how the skeleton merges them: an overturned rule is written only in the dialect's form, the rest of the theme is written as this world's values, what Swiss keeps stays, and the Philosophy is answered in the world's terms, never quoted. A dialect states only what its school changes.
- **The remaining six dialects corrected.** De Stijl shows relations, not things: a drawn, open grid balanced by opposition, pure primaries against non-colour; van Doesburg turned the lines where Mondrian turned only the frame; van der Leck's hand is now his Batavier Line poster and *Mine Triptych* (1916), the Delftsche Slaolie poster being Toorop's; Rietveld's overrun and the built *De Stijl* letter visit. The Polish poster's metaphor is two things in one form; Lenica's hand is the swelling line of *Wozzeck*, not cut paper; with no hand work, a labelled slot holds the place, never code. Ulm puts analysis before form; Bill's hand is *Fifteen Variations on a Single Theme*, and Gugelot's module joins. Swiss Punk is Swiss tested from inside its own school. ma is the interval in space and in time, after Isozaki. Swiss Brutal takes Banham's terms: structure shown, material as found. The De Stijl skeleton gains the abstracted figure and the built title, Ulm a variation series; neither is yet proven by a piece.
- **Bauhaus: elements as forces.** The dialect names what the Bauhaus adds to the philosophy every theme keeps: point, line and the planar forms acting on the basic plane (Kandinsky); direction as meaning, the diagonal for content that moves; primaries that mix only where two parts truly share something (Moholy-Nagy's transparencies, Albers); constructed, asymmetric type (Bayer, Albers, codified by Tschichold). It is told from De Stijl not by the diagonal but by building figures. Kandinsky becomes a hand; Malevich and Lissitzky visit with floating planes.
- **Brodovitch becomes the house editorial.** The dialect follows today's fashion campaign books after the Bazaar: photographs in black and white, one saturated colour that is the subject's own, a serif that speaks and a grotesque that labels, one collision of figure and word per loud unit. The Bazaar cut remains as a cover. The serif that speaks is a sharp one of moderate contrast (Instrument Serif), the Didone kept for the cut; Section and Gallery join the forms. A borrowed house's marks and template chrome (rounded cards, pills, checkmarks, KPI tiles) are the caricature. Its earlier pieces (the-grid, ballet, nighttrain, portfolio) predate it.
- **Memphis becomes the tile system.** The dialect follows Memphis graphics as practised now: a kit of flat primitives, two to four of them drawn from the subject, tiled whole across one square module in clashing hues, the type as a block among blocks. Sottsass and Du Pasquier remain its source; Camille Walala and Peter Shire visit. A kit that would fit any subject is the caricature. Its earlier pieces (the-grid, gelato, makers, oats) predate it.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-02

- **SKILL.md is theme-agnostic.** Goal, philosophy, floors, process and a one-line theme index; the description names no style. The dialect table moves into the Swiss theme.
- **Routes, not steps.** The process becomes a routing table: each row a situation and the file it opens; only the theme is ordered, before the first mark. Roughs go to the user to choose the feel before one is built out. The end-of-file library index is gone.
- **Routing by machine.** release-check fails any file under `references/` or `scripts/` not reached from SKILL.md, and any SKILL.md link outside Process and Themes.
- **No commercial faces named.** Lineages are named by class (grotesque, neo-grotesque, Didone); examples fall back to generic families.
- **The theme says how to use it.** A Use table opens theme.md: each choice (school, hand, grid, methods, breaks, test), where it is made, and where it lands in the skeleton. With a dialect, its hands govern; the Swiss theme gains its own test.
- **Folio's own skeleton (template 14).** Written for the agent that makes the next piece, not for Google's tooling: method before look, then Images (new), Layout, Typography, Colors, Shapes, Forms (was Components), Variations, Do's and Don'ts; tokens last as a yaml block, contrast pairs under `pairs`. Method says how each piece finds its idea; World says what every piece keeps and what each brings. The test gains a rival-world check: a skeleton that fits another world unchanged is a costume. Every size derives from S, the format's short side (folio-check S7).
- **Nine standalone design systems.** Each example DESIGN.md now stands alone: with it and their own content, any agent can make the piece. No skeleton holds content; each states how its method translates to content it was not made for (a master's method has no unsuitable content) and sets the forms any content brings. The posters remain, their content in their briefs. Rules were checked against each other: sizes set by a span are derived, never also scaled; every rule holds for a Chinese word as for a Latin one; a unit may carry a lead form and a quiet one; no translation in World is banned in Forms. Then each skeleton was cut to rules alone, about 1,100 words, by three tests per line (needed, a rule rather than an instance, said once); each now says where unneeded space goes and ends with a look at the render. The skeleton never writes the content's words, titles included.
- **Floors leave SKILL.md for review**, checked before the work is called done, scripts first: the goal is a master's signature, the floors only what nothing ships below. The duplicate "export is inspected" line is gone.
- **extending.md becomes CONTRIBUTING.md**, outside the agent's reading.
- **Not re-tested.** Structural changes; the 1.0.0 evidence stands until the next comparison.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-02

- **Goals and constraints, not procedure.** Four goals replace the eight steps; the thirteen lines and the floors are the only standing rules.
- **Names carry methods.** Every hand is written as a way of working: first move, tools and materials, procedure, one ban. The mother tongue gains its own hands. House defaults removed.
- **A skeleton per world.** DESIGN.md (template 13) holds the system, not the content; each piece's facts, idea and sequence live in its brief.
- **Outside review.** Critics, a judge and a process reader replace self-inspection; the rubric carries a dated list of the defaults current models converge on.
- **Tools, not conclusions.** render, contact, factcheck; real material or honest placeholders.
- **Only floors are hard.** folio-check checks completeness, placeholders, contrast and exports; quota gates removed.
- **Evidence.** Five fixed briefs, Claude Sonnet as maker, a blind Claude Opus judge: first on all five against 0.9.0 and no skill (mean authorship 6.4, 4.4, 3.6). A second maker working from one skeleton alone made a poster judged 9/10 for series coherence.
- **Known limits.** About 1.9× the tokens of no skill. Review falls back to clean self-passes where helpers cannot be spawned. A named master with no photograph remains weak.
